Diptera · Nematocera · Simuliidae

Black Flies of the Oriental Region

Black flies (Simuliidae) are small, stout-bodied biting flies whose larvae and pupae live in running water. Females of some species transmit Onchocerca filariae and Leucocytozoon blood parasites. Identification uses the adult (female and male), the pupa (gill filaments, cocoon) and the mature larva.

This site brings together the published dichotomous keys for the Oriental Region and updates them with later taxonomic work on Thailand and neighbouring countries.
