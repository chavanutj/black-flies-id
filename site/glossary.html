Frons:head ratio

♀

Also called: frons-head ratio

Ratio of the greatest width of the female frons (normalized to 1.0) to the greatest width of the head, used to separate closely related females in Gomphostilbia (Takaoka et al. 2020; Srisuka et al. 2022).

Antenna

Also called: antennomeres; flagellomeres

Also called: Lutz's organ

Cibarium

♀

Also called: buccopharyngeal armature

Internal feeding pump sclerite of the female head; its posterior margin may be smooth, bear a smooth or tuberculate medial projection (many Simulium s.str.), or carry ca. 100-120 spinous processes in Asiosimulium (Takaoka & Choochote 2004, 2005).

Lacinia

♀

Also called: maxillary lacinia

Blade-like cutting lobe of the female maxilla bearing countable rows of inner and outer teeth (e.g. 15–16 inner and 13 outer teeth in S. kittipati; 12 inner and 14–15 outer teeth in S. mirum) (Takaoka et al. 2016; Srisuka et al. 2024).

Mandible

♀♂

Toothed cutting mouthpart of the female (with countable inner and outer teeth, or toothed on one vs both sides of apex in Austrosimulium) and chewing mouthpart of the larva bearing apical comb-teeth and mandibular serrations (Dumbleton 1963; Takaoka et al. 2016; Srisuka et al. 2024).

Scutellum

♀♂

Posterior dorsal sclerite of the adult thorax immediately behind the scutum; bears upright long and short hairs (Takaoka et al. 2016; Srisuka et al. 2024).

♂

Aedeagal membrane

♂

Membranous lobe posterior to the male ventral plate covered with minute setae and supporting the dorsal plate (Takaoka et al. 2016; Srisuka et al. 2024).

Dorsal plate

♂

Sclerotized plate on the dorsal surface of the male aedeagal membrane, square and lightly pigmented in S. kittipati or short and sclerotized in S. mirum (Takaoka et al. 2016; Srisuka et al. 2024).

Larval antenna

Three-segmented sensory appendage with an apical sensillum on each side of the larval head capsule; its length relative to the labral fan stem and segment proportions are recorded in larval descriptions and keys (Takaoka et al. 2016, 2020; Srisuka et al. 2024).

Anal sclerite

X-shaped dorsal sclerite on the posterior abdominal segment of the larva immediately anterior to the posterior circlet, with paired anterior and posterior arms and adjacent sensilla (Takaoka et al. 2016, 2019; Srisuka et al. 2024).
