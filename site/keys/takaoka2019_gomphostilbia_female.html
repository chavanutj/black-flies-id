Thailand: Simulium (Gomphostilbia) females

Key to adult females of 34 Thai species of subgenus Gomphostilbia (Takaoka et al. 2019, pp. 59-60). Females of S. dentistylum, S. doisaketense, S. otsukai and S. prayongi are unknown; S. curtatum runs to couplet 22.

Update from later literature
UPDATE (Srisuka et al. 2022): S. khelangense sp. nov. (S. varicorne group, S. chumpornense subgroup; Lampang, Phitsanulok, Chiang Mai) also has 8 antennal flagellomeres, bare subcosta, hind tibia darkened on apical half and usually 3-4 outer mandibular teeth; distinguished from S. piroonae by longer sensory vesicle (0.33-0.39 vs 0.25-0.30x 3rd palp segment) and more slender fore basitarsus (6.29-6.38 vs 5.54-5.68x width).
