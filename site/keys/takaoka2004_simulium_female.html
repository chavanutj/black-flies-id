Thailand (2004): Simulium (Simulium) females

Historical baseline key (23 couplets) to adult females of 26 Thai species of Simulium s.str. (females of S. baimaii and S. thailandicum unknown and omitted). S. chiangmaiense, S. nakhonense and S. quinquestriatum are not separated (couplet 7a). Check update notes for Thai S. nobile and S. rufibasis.

SourceTakaoka, H. & Choochote, W. (2004) A list of and keys to black flies (Diptera: Simuliidae) in Thailand. Tropical Medicine and Health 32(2): 189-197. https://doi.org/10.2149/tmh.32.189
