Simulium decuplum☰
Update from later literature
UPDATE (Takaoka et al. 2019): Thai populations previously identified as S. decuplum were split into five new species (S. chiangraiense, S. huaikaeoense, S. khaokhoense, S. maeklangense, S. pamiangense; Takaoka et al. 2018d).
7′– Arms of genital fork without such a projection ⚠ Takaoka & Choochote 2004 p. 191
Simulium parahiyangum☰
Update from later literature
UPDATE (Takaoka et al. 2019): Thai populations previously identified as S. parahiyangum were described as S. ngaoense (Takaoka et al. 2018a).

Simulium asakoae☰
Update from later literature
UPDATE (Takaoka et al. 2020): The Thai S. asakoae species-group now includes 27 species (21 new species described in 2020); use takaoka2020_asakoae_female.
