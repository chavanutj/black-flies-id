Update from later literature
UPDATE (Srisuka et al. 2024): Two newer Thai species also have a pigmented subesophageal ganglion sheath: S. kittipati (gill histoblast 28-30 filaments; 71-73 hooklet rows) and S. khongchiamense (gill histoblast 31-33 filaments; labral fan 37-39 main rays vs 42-45 in S. phurueaense).
