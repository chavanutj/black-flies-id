Thailand: Simulium (Simulium) mature larvae

Key to mature larvae of 53 Thai species of subgenus Simulium s. str. (Takaoka et al. 2019, pp. 71-75). Larvae of S. barnesi, S. chanyae, S. kiewmaepanense, S. phukaense, S. setsukoae, S. takense, S. thailandicum and S. umphangense are unknown.
