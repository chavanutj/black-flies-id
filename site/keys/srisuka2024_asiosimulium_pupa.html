Subgenus Asiosimulium: pupae

Updated key to pupae of 8 of the 9 known species of subgenus Asiosimulium (Srisuka et al. 2024, p. 16; 6 couplets), including S. kittipati sp. nov. and S. khongchiamense. S. oblongum and S. phurueaense are not separated at couplet 5a; pupa of S. shanense is unknown.

SourceSrisuka, W., Takaoka, H., Taai, K., Maleewong, W., Aupalee, K. & Saeung, A. (2024) Morphological description and genetic analysis of a new black fly species (Diptera: Simuliidae) in the subgenus Asiosimulium from central Thailand. Parasites & Vectors 17: 379. https://doi.org/10.1186/s13071-024-06441-z
