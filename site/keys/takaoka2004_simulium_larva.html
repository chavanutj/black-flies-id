Thailand (2004): Simulium (Simulium) mature larvae

Historical baseline key (23 couplets) to mature larvae of 25 Thai species of Simulium s.str. (larvae of S. barnesi, S. chaliowae and S. thailandicum unknown and omitted). S. fenestratum and S. malayense are not separated (couplet 10b; printed as fensestratum).

SourceTakaoka, H. & Choochote, W. (2004) A list of and keys to black flies (Diptera: Simuliidae) in Thailand. Tropical Medicine and Health 32(2): 189-197. https://doi.org/10.2149/tmh.32.189
