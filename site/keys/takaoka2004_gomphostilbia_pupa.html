Thailand (2004): Simulium (Gomphostilbia) pupae

Historical baseline key (10 couplets) to pupae of 11 Thai species of Gomphostilbia. Check update notes for later re-identifications and splits.

SourceTakaoka, H. & Choochote, W. (2004) A list of and keys to black flies (Diptera: Simuliidae) in Thailand. Tropical Medicine and Health 32(2): 189-197. https://doi.org/10.2149/tmh.32.189
RegionThailand
Sexespupa
Size10 couplets, 11 terminal taxa, 5 leads with notes (⚠)

1 Gill of much inflated structure with 2 triplet groups of finger-like projections and 8 slender filaments ⚠ Takaoka & Choochote 2004 p. 192
Simulium gombakense☰
Update from later literature
UPDATE (Takaoka et al. 2019): Thai populations of S. gombakense were shown to include S. gombakense s.str. and the S. paiense complex (Takaoka et al. 2018e).

Simulium decuplum☰
Update from later literature
UPDATE (Takaoka et al. 2019): Thai populations previously identified as S. decuplum were split into five new species (Takaoka et al. 2018d).

5 Gill filaments arranged in 2 groups, i.e. 1 dorsal (4 individual and 2 paired filaments) and 1 ventral (2 paired filaments) Takaoka & Choochote 2004 p. 192
Simulium chumpornense☰

Simulium angulistylum☰
6 Antennal sheath with marked ridges each corresponding to flagellar segments 1-9, each ridge covered with several tubercles; gill filaments arranged in 3 groups (3+3+2 filaments from dorsal to ventral) ⚠ Takaoka & Choochote 2004 p. 192
Simulium parahiyangum☰
Update from later literature
UPDATE (Takaoka et al. 2019): Thai populations previously identified as S. parahiyangum were described as S. ngaoense (Takaoka et al. 2018a).
6′– Antennal sheath with less marked ridges corresponding to flagellar segments 1-9, each ridge covered with a few tubercles; gill filaments arranged in 4 groups (2+1+3+2 filaments from dorsal to ventral) Takaoka & Choochote 2004 p. 192

Simulium inthanonense☰
Update from later literature
UPDATE (Takaoka et al. 2020): S. inthanonense was transferred to the S. asakoae species-group; three additional Thai species (S. huaimorense, S. maewongense, S. myanmarense) also have a short anterodorsal cocoon projection (use takaoka2020_asakoae_pupa).

Simulium asakoae☰
Update from later literature
UPDATE (Takaoka et al. 2020): The Thai S. asakoae species-group now includes 27 species; use takaoka2020_asakoae_pupa.
