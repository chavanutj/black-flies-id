Simulium varicorne species-group (females)

Key to adult females of 14 species of the Simulium (Gomphostilbia) varicorne species-group (Srisuka et al. 2022, p. 9; 13 couplets), including S. khelangense sp. nov. from Thailand. Female of S. breviflagellum (Vietnam) is unknown and omitted.
