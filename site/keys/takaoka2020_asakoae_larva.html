Thailand: Simulium asakoae species-group (mature larvae)

Key to mature larvae of 21 of the 27 Thai species of the Simulium (Gomphostilbia) asakoae species-group (Takaoka et al. 2020, pp. 145-146; 21 couplets; S. inthanonense keys out twice as morph 1 and morph 2). Larvae of S. banluangense, S. kiewfinense, S. maelanoiense, S. nanthaburiense, S. puaense and S. songense are unknown.

SourceTakaoka, H., Srisuka, W., Fukuda, M. & Saeung, A. (2020) Twenty-one new species of the Simulium (Gomphostilbia) asakoae species group (Diptera, Simuliidae) in Thailand, with their genetic relationships. ZooKeys 950: 51-152. https://doi.org/10.3897/zookeys.950.51298
