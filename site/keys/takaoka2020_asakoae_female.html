Thailand: Simulium asakoae species-group (females)

Key to adult females of 21 of the 27 Thai species of the Simulium (Gomphostilbia) asakoae species-group (Takaoka et al. 2020, pp. 141-142; 20 couplets). Females of S. banluangense, S. chaowaense, S. kiewfinense, S. phapeungense, S. rampae and S. teerachanense are unknown.
