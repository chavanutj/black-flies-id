Thailand (2004): subgenera of Simulium (mature larvae)

Historical baseline key (4 couplets) to 5 subgenera of Simulium s.l. in Thailand (mature larvae), including Montisimulium. Excludes Asiosimulium (erected in 2005). Superseded by Takaoka et al. 2019.

SourceTakaoka, H. & Choochote, W. (2004) A list of and keys to black flies (Diptera: Simuliidae) in Thailand. Tropical Medicine and Health 32(2): 189-197. https://doi.org/10.2149/tmh.32.189
RegionThailand
Sexeslarva
Size4 couplets, 5 terminal taxa, 5 leads with notes (⚠)

1 Hypostomium very wide, with 13 apical teeth ⚠ Takaoka & Choochote 2004 p. 191
Subgenus Daviesellum
Note
Printed as "Daviesellum".
1′– Hypostomium of moderate width, with 9 apical teeth
→ 2
2 Last abdominal segment lacks ventral papillae or with small ones ⚠ Takaoka & Choochote 2004 p. 191
Subgenus Simulium
Note
Printed as "Simulium s. str.".
Update from later literature
UPDATE (Takaoka & Choochote 2005): Subgenus Asiosimulium (S. oblongum) also has 9 hypostomal teeth and very small ventral papillae, but has the antenna subequal in length to the stem of the labral fan (usually longer in Simulium s.str.).
2′– Last abdominal segment with large ventral papillae
