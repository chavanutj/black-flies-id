Subgenus Asiosimulium: adult females

Updated key to adult females of 8 of the 9 known species of subgenus Asiosimulium (Srisuka et al. 2024, p. 15; 7 couplets), including S. kittipati sp. nov. and S. khongchiamense. Female of S. shanense (Myanmar) is unknown.

SourceSrisuka, W., Takaoka, H., Taai, K., Maleewong, W., Aupalee, K. & Saeung, A. (2024) Morphological description and genetic analysis of a new black fly species (Diptera: Simuliidae) in the subgenus Asiosimulium from central Thailand.
