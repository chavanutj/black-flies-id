Thailand: Simulium asakoae species-group (pupae)

Key to pupae of Thai species of the Simulium (Gomphostilbia) asakoae species-group (Takaoka et al. 2020, pp. 144-145; 13 couplets). Ten species are indistinguishable at couplet 12a (and pupae of S. kiewfinense and S. nanthaburiense also match 12a though omitted from the printed list).

SourceTakaoka, H., Srisuka, W., Fukuda, M. & Saeung, A. (2020) Twenty-one new species of the Simulium (Gomphostilbia) asakoae species group (Diptera, Simuliidae) in Thailand, with their genetic relationships. ZooKeys 950: 51-152. https://doi.org/10.3897/zookeys.950.51298
