UPDATE (Srisuka et al. 2024): Two newer Thai species also have tuberculate head and thorax: S. khongchiamense (thoracic integument sparsely covered with tubercles; gill 31-33 filaments) and S. kittipati (thoracic integument densely covered with tubercles; gill 28-30 filaments).
