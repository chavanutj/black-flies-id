Thailand (2004): Simulium (Simulium) males

Historical baseline key (21 couplets) to adult males of 24 Thai species of Simulium s.str. (males of S. baimaii, S. barnesi, S. suchariti and S. triglobus unknown and omitted). S. chiangmaiense, S. nakhonense and S. thailandicum are not separated (couplet 15b).

SourceTakaoka, H. & Choochote, W. (2004) A list of and keys to black flies (Diptera: Simuliidae) in Thailand. Tropical Medicine and Health 32(2): 189-197. https://doi.org/10.2149/tmh.32.189
RegionThailand
Sexesmale
