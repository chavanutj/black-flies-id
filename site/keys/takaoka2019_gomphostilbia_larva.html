Thailand: Simulium (Gomphostilbia) mature larvae

Key to mature larvae of 34 Thai species of subgenus Gomphostilbia (Takaoka et al. 2019, pp. 63-64). Larvae of S. chiangdaoense, S. datfaense, S. otsukai and S. prayongi are unknown.
