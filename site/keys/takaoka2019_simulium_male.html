Thailand: Simulium (Simulium) males

Key to adult males of 53 Thai species of subgenus Simulium s. str. (Takaoka et al. 2019, pp. 68-69). Males of S. barnesi, S. bullatum, S. triglobus, S. kiewmaepanense and S. umphangense are unknown.
