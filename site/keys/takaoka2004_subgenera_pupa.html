Thailand (2004): subgenera of Simulium (pupae)

Historical baseline key (3 couplets) to 4 subgenera of Simulium s.l. in Thailand (pupae). Excludes Montisimulium (pupa of S. (M.) sp. G unknown) and Asiosimulium (erected in 2005). Superseded by Takaoka et al. 2019.

SourceTakaoka, H. & Choochote, W. (2004) A list of and keys to black flies (Diptera: Simuliidae) in Thailand. Tropical Medicine and Health 32(2): 189-197. https://doi.org/10.2149/tmh.32.189
RegionThailand
Sexespupa
Size3 couplets, 4 terminal taxa, 4 leads with notes (⚠)

Subgenus Daviesellum

3′– Dorsal surface of abdominal segment 2 with spines (in place of such distinct hooks) much smaller than hooks on abdominal segments 3 and 4 ⚠ Takaoka & Choochote 2004 p. 190
Subgenus Simulium
Note
Printed as "Simulium s. str.". Footnote: pupa of Montisimulium not included (that of S. (M.) sp. G is unknown).
Update from later literature
UPDATE (Takaoka & Choochote 2005): Subgenus Asiosimulium (S. oblongum) also lacks grapnel-like hooklets and anterodorsal cocoon projection and has small spines on tergite 2, but has a dendroid gill of 31-33 filaments and lacks dorsal spine-combs on abdominal segments 5-9.
