Simulium takense Takaoka & Choochote, 2005

ClassificationGenus Simulium, subgenus Simulium
Statusvalid
DistributionThailand (Takaoka et al. 2019) | Thailand (Srisuka et al. 2017)
ThailandThailand (Takaoka et al. 2019, p. 57) | Thailand: 69 mature larvae (0.4% of total), 1.7% stream occurrence; associated with calcareous waterfalls (Srisuka et al. 2017)
Diagnostic notesSimulium multistriatum species-group (Takaoka et al. 2019). Larva unknown (Takaoka et al. 2019, p. 75).
SourcesTakaoka et al. 2019, Srisuka et al. 2017

The chain of characters leading to this taxon in each key (a ready-made diagnosis).

Thailand: Simulium (Simulium) females (Takaoka et al. 2019) – couplet 32a
  1. 1b Claw without any tooth
  2. 15a Scutum with 5 distinct longitudinal vittae
  3. 16b Scutum with 1 median, 2 submedian and 2 sublateral dark vittae when illuminated anteriorly and viewed dorsally
  4. 27b Mid femur with darkened area. Spermatheca 1 in number
  5. 28b Hind femur darkened at least on more than apical 1/2
  6. 29a Paraproct in lateral view tapered ventrally
  7. 30a Cibarium with minute spinous processes dorsomedially
  8. 32a Ovipositor valve with 7-10 medium-long hairs
Thailand: Simulium (Simulium) males (Takaoka et al. 2019) – couplet 39b
  1. 1b Basal section of radius bare or with few hairs apically
  2. 7b Scutum otherwise
  3. 9b Mid femur and tibia otherwise
  4. 11b Mid tibia whitish or yellowish (at least on posterior surface) on basal 2/5 or more and dark brown on rest
  5. 29b Hind basitarsus entirely light to dark brown, or so with basal 2/5-1/3 whitish yellow or dark yellow. Body of ventral plate longer than wide ⚠
  6. 33b Hind basitarsus brownish black with basal 1/3-2/5 whitish yellow or dark yellow
  7. 36a Upper-eye facets in 20 or 21 vertical columns and 20 or 21 horizontal rows
  8. 37b Ventral plate with setae
  9. 38b Ventral plate with teeth
  10. 39b Ventral plate in ventral view with posterior margin concave
Thailand: Simulium (Simulium) pupae (Takaoka et al. 2019) – couplet 44a
  1. 1b Gill with 6-10 fiaments ⚠
  2. 4b Gill with 8-10 filaments
  3. 35a Gill with 8 filaments
  4. 36b Cocoon shoe shaped
  5. 42b Cocoon without open spaces
  6. 44a At least 2 gill filaments directed posteriorly. Terminal hooks present
