Simulium lamdongense Takaoka & Sofian-Azirun, 2015

ClassificationGenus Simulium, subgenus Gomphostilbia
Statusvalid
DistributionVietnam: Lam Dong (Lac Duong, 1,539 m, paratypes/topotype) and Nghe An (Pu Mat) (Putt et al. 2023)
Diagnostic notesS. batoense species-group. Monophyletic in COI but exhibits high intraspecific divergence (0-5.45%, mean 3.50%; up to 4.70% between Lam Dong paratypes and Nghe An specimens) and splits into 2 distinct OTUs across PTP_ML, ASAP and GMYC (Putt et al. 2023 pp. 10, 17).
SourcesPutt et al. 2023
