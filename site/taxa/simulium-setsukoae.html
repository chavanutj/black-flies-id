Simulium setsukoae Takaoka & Choochote, 2004

ClassificationGenus Simulium, subgenus Simulium
Statusvalid
Other names in keysSimulium sp. D
DistributionThailand (Takaoka et al. 2019) | Thailand (Takaoka & Choochote 2004).
ThailandThailand (Takaoka et al. 2019, p. 57) | Listed from Thailand (Takaoka & Choochote 2004 p. 190; Note 3 states S. sp. D collected biting on human attractant in Doi Inthanon NP by Takaoka & Suzuki 1984 was described as S. setsukoae by Takaoka & Choochote 2004).
ImportanceFemales (originally reported as S. sp. D) collected biting on human attractant in Doi Inthanon National Park (Takaoka & Suzuki 1984, cited in Takaoka & Choochote 2004 p. 190 Note 3).
Diagnostic notesSimulium tuberosum species-group, S. rufibasis subgroup (Takaoka et al. 2019). Recorded as Simulium sp. D in Takaoka & Suzuki (1984). Larva unknown (Takaoka et al. 2019, p. 75). | Simulium s.str. (tuberosum species-group). Female: unvittate scutum, abdominal segment 7 with ventral pair of short clustered hairs subequal to surrounding hairs. Male: much-enlarged hind basitarsus, upper eye with 20 vertical columns of large facets. Pupa: 6 short-stalked gill filaments with dorsalmost filament much thicker than counter filament, dorsal spine-combs on segment 7, no terminal hooks. Larva: dark grey to greyish black body, cephalic apotome yellow with posteromedian dark area, one of dorsal pair of pharate pupal gill filaments much thicker than counter filament (Takaoka & Choochote 2004 pp. 190, 194-197).
SourcesTakaoka et al. 2019, Takaoka & Choochote 2004
