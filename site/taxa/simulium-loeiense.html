Simulium loeiense Takaoka, Srisuka & Fukuda, 2020

ClassificationGenus Simulium, subgenus Gomphostilbia
Statusvalid
DistributionThailand (Loei) (Takaoka et al. 2020)
ThailandType locality: Loei Province, 1,525 m, 17°16'51.7"N, 6-IX-2018 (Takaoka et al. 2020, pp. 77-78)
Diagnostic notesS. asakoae species-group (COI subgroup IV). Female: body 1.9-2.2 mm, mandible with several outer teeth, claw tooth 0.50x claw, sensory vesicle 0.26-0.32x 3rd palp segment, labrum 0.7x clypeus, 5th palpal segment 2.6x 3rd (vs 2.1x in S. maelanoiense). Male: brown upper-eye facets in 11 vertical columns and 13 horizontal rows, head nearly as wide as thorax, subcosta with many hairs, hind basitarsus 1.2-1.3x hind femur, ventral plate nearly straight ventrally in caudal view (vs rounded in S. maewongense). Pupa: abdominal tergites 1-2 yellowish (vs darkened in S. asakoae) with several minute tubercles on each side, terminal hook outer margin 2.2x inner margin (vs 2.7x in S. thungchangense). Larva: postgenal cleft 1.2-1.3x postgenal bridge, abdominal segments 1-5, 7 and 8 grey, segment 6 unpigmented (Takaoka et al. 2020, pp. 77-81).
SourcesTakaoka et al. 2020
