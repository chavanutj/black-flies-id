Simulium maeaiense Takaoka & Srisuka, 2011

ClassificationGenus Simulium, subgenus Nevermannia
Statusvalid
DistributionThailand, Vietnam (Takaoka et al. 2019) | Thailand (Srisuka et al. 2017; Putt et al. 2023) | Vietnam: Lao Cai, Sa Pa, 1,893 m (Putt et al. 2023)
ThailandThailand (Takaoka et al. 2019, p. 56) | Thailand: 241 mature larvae (1.2% of total), 8.6% stream occurrence; strongly associated with high elevation in northern/central regions (Srisuka et al. 2017)
Diagnostic notesSimulium feuerborni species-group (Takaoka et al. 2019). | S. feuerborni species-group. Paraphyletic in COI (0.50-2.48% p-distance) and merged with S. bachmaense, S. feuerborni A, S. phami and S. pumatense into 1 OTU by PTP_ML, ASAP and GMYC (Putt et al. 2023).
SourcesTakaoka et al. 2019, Srisuka et al. 2017, Putt et al. 2023

How it keys out

The chain of characters leading to this taxon in each key (a ready-made diagnosis).

Thailand: Simulium (Nevermannia) females (Takaoka et al. 2019) – couplet 3a
  1. 1b Antenna almost all darkened or mostly so except few basal segments pale. Hind tibia brownish
  2. 2a Scutum with 3 dark longitudinal vittae
  3. 3a Genital fork with strongly sclerotized horizontal bar at base of anterodorsal projection of each arm
Thailand: Simulium (Nevermannia) males (Takaoka et al. 2019) – couplet 4a
  1. 1b Antenna almost all darkened. Hind basitarsus inflated, and nearly as wide as hind tibia
  2. 2a Paramere with 3-7 hooks ⚠
  3. 3b Upper-eye facets in 14-19 vertical columns
  4. 4a Upper-eye facets in 19 vertical columns
Thailand: Simulium (Nevermannia) pupae (Takaoka et al. 2019) – couplet 5a
  1. 1b Gill with 6 filaments
  2. 3b All filaments extending forwards close together
  3. 4a Cocoon with anterodorsal projection
  4. 5a Gill filaments arranged as 2+1+2+1 from inside to outside, lying horizontally
Thailand: Simulium (Nevermannia) mature larvae (Takaoka et al. 2019) – couplet 5a
  1. 1a Abdomen with characteristic reddish-brown markings dorsally
  2. 2b Thoracic segment 1 without colored band dorsally
  3. 4b Labral fan with 23 or more primary rays
  4. 5a Postgenal cleft 0.3 times as long as postgenal bridge
