Simulium kandisense Takaoka, 2025

ClassificationGenus Simulium, subgenus Gomphostilbia
Statusvalid
DistributionIndonesia: West Sumatra, Tigosakato, Tanjung Kandis, Padang, ca. 120 m (brook 1.0 m wide, sun-exposed, slow flow, 30°C; holotype male with pupal exuviae/cocoon) (Takaoka 2025)
Diagnostic notesS. asakoae species-group (Takaoka 2025 pp. 392-394). Male (n=1): body 2.2 mm; upper-eye large facets in 13 vertical columns x 15 horizontal rows; sensory vesicle globular, small (0.11x 3rd palpal segment); scutum densely covered with golden-yellow hairs; fore coxa yellow; fore basitarsus 6.8x as long as wide; hind tibia whitish on basal half; hind basitarsus enlarged, wedge-shaped, 3.6x as long as wide and 1.0x width of tibia and femur; hair tuft on base of radial vein dark brown (shared with only 4 other species in the 71-species S. asakoae group: S. nepalense, S. inthanonense, S. rutengense, S. yunnanense, separated from them by yellow scutal hairs, basal-half yellow hind tibia and 13x15 eye facets); ventral plate body 0.6x as long as wide with lateral margins depressed medially, posterior margin convex dorsomedially and ventral margin depressed medially in caudal view; paramere with 3 long and 1 short hooks. Pupa (n=1): body 2.8 mm; head/thorax with round tubercles, all trichomes unbranched; gill 8 slender filaments arranged (3+3)+2, ventral pair 2.8 mm (1.25x thickness of middle triplet), dorsal/middle triplets 2.1-2.4 mm; terminal hooks triangular with crenulated outer margins 1.6-1.7x inner margins (narrower than in S. pitasawatae [3.6-3.9x], S. tanahrataense [2.7-2.8x], S. rutengense [2.7x], S. phapeungense [2.5-2.6x], S. kiewfinense [2.3-2.7x]); cocoon simple wall pocket-shaped (3.0 x 1.0 mm). Female and larva unknown.
