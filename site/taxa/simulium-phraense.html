Thailand: Simulium (Simulium) females (Takaoka et al. 2019) – couplet 17a
  1. 1b Claw without any tooth
  2. 15a Scutum with 5 distinct longitudinal vittae
  3. 16a Scutum with 1 median, 2 submedian and 2 lateral dark vittae when illuminated anteriorly and viewed dorsally
  4. 17a Fore tibia medium to dark brown, without white area on outer surface. Hind tibia dark brown to brownish black with base yellow. Inner margin of ovipositor valve with ventrally produced round flap
Thailand: Simulium (Simulium) males (Takaoka et al. 2019) – couplet 28b
  1. 1b Basal section of radius bare or with few hairs apically
  2. 7b Scutum otherwise
  3. 9b Mid femur and tibia otherwise
  4. 11a Mid tibia mostly dark (including posterior surface)
  5. 12b Fore tibia medium to dark brown, without white area on outer surface
  6. 27b Abdomen with dorsolateral pair of silvery spots on segments 2, 5, 6 and 7
  7. 28b Hind basitarsus whitish yellow on basal 3/5 and brownish black on apical 2/5
Thailand: Simulium (Simulium) pupae (Takaoka et al. 2019) – couplet 47a
  1. 1b Gill with 6-10 fiaments ⚠
  2. 4b Gill with 8-10 filaments
  3. 35b Gill with 10 filaments
  4. 46b Gill filaments arranged as 2+2+2+2+2 or 2+1+2+1+2+2 from dorsal to ventral. Four or five dorsal filaments slightly to markedly thicker than others
  5. 47a Gill filaments lacking ridges and furrows
Thailand: Simulium (Simulium) mature larvae (Takaoka et al. 2019) – couplet 43a
  1. 1b Histoblast of pupal gill with 3, 6, 8 or 10 filaments
  2. 3b Histoblast of pupal gill with 6-10 filaments
  3. 4b Histoblast of pupal gill with 8 or 10 filaments
  4. 32b Histoblast of pupal gill with 10 filaments
  5. 42b Histoblast of pupal gill with 10 filaments arranged as 2+2+2+2+2 or 2+1+2+1+2+2 from dorsal to ventral
  6. 43a Histoblast of pupal gill with 10 filaments arranged as 2+1+2+1+2+2 from dorsal to ventral. Filaments without ridges and furrows
