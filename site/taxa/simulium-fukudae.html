How it keys out

The chain of characters leading to this taxon in each key (a ready-made diagnosis).

Thailand: Simulium (Gomphostilbia) females (Takaoka et al. 2019) – couplet 10b
  1. 1b Antenna with 9 flagellomeres
  2. 6a Posterior surface of hind tibia mostly darkened except basal tip
  3. 7b Sensory vesicle 0.4 or less times length of third palpomere of maxillary palp
  4. 10b Frons : head ratio 1.0 : 5.4-6.4
Thailand: Simulium (Gomphostilbia) males (Takaoka et al. 2019) – couplet 15a
  1. 1b Antenna with 8 or 9 flagellomeres
  2. 2b Antenna with 9 flagellomeres
  3. 5b Hind basitarsus slender, much narrower than hind tibia
  4. 12b Mid and hind femora almost dark except base pale
  5. 13a Hind tibia almost dark except base pale
  6. 14a Scutum with brassy pubescence; abdominal segments 2, 5, 6 and 7 each with dorsolateral pair of shiny whitish-pruinose patches
  7. 15a Paramere with fine setae on outer surface
Thailand: Simulium (Gomphostilbia) pupae (Takaoka et al. 2019) – couplet 9b
  1. 1b Gill filamentous
  2. 7a Gill with 6 filaments
  3. 8b Cocoon with anterodorsal projection
  4. 9b Cocoon with short anterodorsal projection not extending beyond anteroventral tips of cocoon
Thailand: Simulium (Gomphostilbia) mature larvae (Takaoka et al. 2019) – couplet 10a
  1. 1b Abdomen without protuberances dorsally
  2. 6a Postgenal cleft long, approaching or completely reaching posterior margin of hypostoma
  3. 7b Abdomen not constricted between segments 4 and 5 when viewed dorsally
  4. 8b Abdomen moderately or densely covered with branched dark spinous setae or spinules dorsally on segments 5-8
  5. 10a Histoblast of gill with 6 filaments
