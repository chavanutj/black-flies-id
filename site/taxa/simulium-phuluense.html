Simulium phuluense Takaoka & Sofian-Azirun, 2017

ClassificationGenus Simulium, subgenus Simulium
Statusvalid
DistributionVietnam: Lao Cai, Sa Pa, 1,192-1,750 m (topotypes OQ117893-OQ117896) (Putt et al. 2023)
Diagnostic notesS. variegatum species-group. High intraspecific COI divergence (0-4.46%, mean 2.27%); three topotypes cluster with Thai S. chamlongi (0.00-0.25% COI divergence) in a single OTU, though morphologically distinguished from S. chamlongi by female leg color, female mandible teeth, male upper-eye facets, pupal frontal tubercles/abdominal segment 9 spine-combs and larval labral fan rays (Putt et al. 2023 p. 17; Aupalee et al. 2024 p. 13).
SourcesPutt et al. 2023, Aupalee et al. 2024
