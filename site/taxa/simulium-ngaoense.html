Simulium ngaoense Takaoka, Srisuka & Saeung, 2018

ClassificationGenus Simulium, subgenus Gomphostilbia
Statusvalid
DistributionThailand, Vietnam (Takaoka et al. 2019) | Thailand (GenBank MZ508540, MZ508541, MZ508550) | Vietnam: Nghe An, Quy Chau, 80 m (Putt et al. 2023)
ThailandThailand (Takaoka et al. 2019, p. 55) | Thailand (GenBank COI sequences MZ508540-MZ508541, MZ508550) (Putt et al. 2023 Fig. 2)
Diagnostic notesSimulium batoense species-group, S. parahiyangum subgroup (Takaoka et al. 2019). Previously treated in Thailand as S. parahiyangum (not sensu Takaoka & Sigit, 1991). Described in Takaoka et al. (2018a). | S. batoense species-group. Monophyletic in COI (0-0.50% p-distance) and supported by PTP_ML, ASAP and GMYC (Putt et al. 2023).
SourcesTakaoka et al. 2019, Putt et al. 2023

  5. 9b Arms of genital fork without such projection
