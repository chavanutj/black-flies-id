Thailand: Simulium (Nevermannia) females (Takaoka et al. 2019) – couplet 5b
  1. 1b Antenna almost all darkened or mostly so except few basal segments pale. Hind tibia brownish
  2. 2a Scutum with 3 dark longitudinal vittae
  3. 3b Genital fork without strongly sclerotized horizontal bar at base of anterodorsal projection of each arm
  4. 4b Mandible with 20-23 inner teeth
  5. 5b Frons : head ratio 1 : 4.6-5.2
Thailand: Simulium (Nevermannia) pupae (Takaoka et al. 2019) – couplet 9b
  1. 1b Gill with 6 filaments
  2. 3b All filaments extending forwards close together
  3. 4b Cocoon without anterodorsal projection
  4. 7b Common basak stalk much shorter than interspiracular trunk ⚠
  5. 8b Frons and thorax covered with light brown small tubercles. Facial trichome medium-long and somewhat stout, 2.5 times as long as frontal ones. Gill filaments covered with small tubercles on ridge
  6. 9b Stalk of ventral paired filaments short
