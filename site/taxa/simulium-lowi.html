Simulium lowi Takaoka & Adler, 2017

ClassificationGenus Simulium, subgenus Simulium
Statusvalid
DistributionVietnam: Vinh Phuc, Tam Dao, 975 m (paratypes OQ117868-OQ117869) (Adler et al. 2016; Putt et al. 2023)
Diagnostic notesS. tuberosum species-group; formally described from S. brevipar cytoform B of Adler et al. (2016) (fixed IIIL-13,81,82; probable Y-linked IIIL-83). Only Vietnamese S. tuberosum group species recovered as monophyletic in COI (0.00% p-distance) and supported by PTP_ML, ASAP and GMYC (Putt et al. 2023 pp. 2, 10, 12).
SourcesAdler et al. 2016, Putt et al. 2023
