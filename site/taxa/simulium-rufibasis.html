Simulium rufibasis Brunetti, 1911

ClassificationGenus Simulium, subgenus Simulium
Statusvalid
Other names in keysSimulium sp. E
DistributionReported from Thailand (Takaoka & Kuvangkadilok 1999; Takaoka & Choochote 2004), though Thai populations were later recognized as S. tenebrosum and S. sp. E as S. doipuiense (Takaoka et al. 2018g, 2019). | India (type locality; Mukherjee et al. 2022) | Thailand (cytoform A, 1,100-2,300 m) | Vietnam (cytoform B later described as S. rosliramlii) (Adler et al. 2016)
ThailandTontong Waterfall (500 m), Doi Phuka NP, Nan Province, northern Thailand, with S. triglobus (Takaoka & Kuvangkadilok 1999 p. 505); listed from Thailand (Takaoka & Choochote 2004 p. 190, Note 4 tentatively including paler-legged S. sp. E). Thai records later re-identified as S. tenebrosum and S. doipuiense (Takaoka et al. 2019). | Thailand (cytoform A, undifferentiated sex chromosomes, 1,100-2,300 m, reproductively isolated from sympatric S. doipuiense A) (Adler et al. 2016 pp. 14-15, 22)
Diagnostic notesSimulium s.str. (tuberosum species-group). Female: unvittate scutum, ventral pair of long clustered hairs on abdominal segment 7, mid tibia white on basal 1/2 or more. Male: much-enlarged hind basitarsus (3.2-3.5x as long as wide) pale on basal 1/3, upper eye 15-17 vertical columns. Pupa: 6 short-stalked gill filaments (2 dorsal filaments subequal in thickness), dorsal spine-combs on segment 7, no terminal hooks. Larva: reddish brown body, cephalic apotome yellow with posteromedian dark area, dorsal pair of pharate pupal gill filaments subequal in thickness (Takaoka & Choochote 2004 pp. 190, 194-197). | S. tuberosum species-group; species complex. Cytoform A (Thailand, provisionally anchored to Indian type) has fixed IIIL-8,11,13 and undifferentiated sex chromosomes; Vietnamese cytoform B was formally described as S. rosliramlii (Adler et al. 2016; Putt et al. 2023).
SourcesTakaoka & Kuvangkadilok 1999, Takaoka & Choochote 2004, Mukherjee et al. 2022, Adler et al. 2016

  1. 1b Basal portion of radial vein bare
  2. 4b Scutum otherwise

  4. 7a Mid tibia mostly dark (including posterior surface)

  6. 9a Hind basitarsus white or whitish yellow on basal 1/2 or less and dark brown to brownish black on the rest

  8. 11b Upper eye with 15-17 vertical columns of large facets
