Simulium chamlongi Takaoka & Suzuki, 1984

ClassificationGenus Simulium, subgenus Simulium
Statusvalid
DistributionMyanmar, China (Yunnan, Tibet), Vietnam, Thailand (Takaoka et al. 2019; Thailand omitted on p. 57 distribution line but listed in Thai checklist and p. 54) | Thailand (Takaoka & Choochote 2004). | Thailand (Chiang Mai, Chiang Rai, nationwide) | Vietnam (Lam Dong: Lac Duong, 1,492 m) (Srisuka et al. 2017; Putt et al. 2023; Aupalee et al. 2024, 2026)
ThailandThailand; listed in Thai checklist and as human-biting in Thailand (Takaoka et al. 2019, pp. 54, 57) | Listed from Thailand (Takaoka & Choochote 2004 p. 190). | Thailand: 217 mature larvae (1.1% of total), 20.7% stream occurrence (Srisuka et al. 2017) | Chiang Mai (Doi Pha Hom Pok 1,529 m; Mae Kam Pong Waterfall, Mae On 1,076 m) and Chiang Rai (Pangkhon, 1,386 m) (Aupalee et al. 2024)
ImportanceKnown to bite humans in Thailand (Choochote et al. 2005; Pramual et al. 2016; cited in Takaoka et al. 2019, p. 54). | Human-biting species in Thailand (Putt et al. 2023; Aupalee et al. 2024, 2026).
Diagnostic notesSimulium variegatum species-group (Takaoka et al. 2019). | Simulium s.str. (variegatum species-group). Female: claw with small subbasal tooth, basal section of radial vein bare, mid and hind femora mostly yellowish. Male: mid tibia mostly dark, fore tibia with white shiny area, hind basitarsus somewhat enlarged (ca. 5.0x as long as wide, pale on basal 1/2 or less). Pupa: 6 filamentous gills, simple thickly woven wall-pocket cocoon without windows, head and thorax bare (except posterior 1/2 of thorax), dorsal spine-combs on segments 7-9, terminal hooks present. Larva: lacking abdominal protuberances, postgenal cleft widened medially, pharate pupal gill with 6 filaments (Takaoka & Choochote 2004 pp. 193-196). | S. variegatum species-group. Wing centroid size 3.03 ± 0.19 mm (range 2.62-3.31 mm); wing shape GM accuracy 80.00% (24/30) (Aupalee et al. 2024). Non-monophyletic in COI (intraspecific divergence up to 4.46%): Vietnamese S. chamlongi forms a discrete OTU, whereas Thai S. chamlongi clusters with topotypes of Vietnamese S. phuluense (0.00-0.25% COI divergence) despite morphological differences in female leg color, female mandible teeth, male upper-eye facets, pupal frontal tubercles/tergite 9 spine-combs and larval labral fan rays (Putt et al. 2023; Aupalee et al. 2024).
SourcesTakaoka et al. 2019, Takaoka & Choochote 2004, Srisuka et al. 2017, Putt et al. 2023, Aupalee et al. 2024, Aupalee et al. 2026
