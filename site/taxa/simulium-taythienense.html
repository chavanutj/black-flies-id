Simulium taythienense Takaoka, Sofian-Azirun & Ya'cob, 2014

ClassificationGenus Simulium, subgenus Simulium
Statusvalid
DistributionVietnam: Nghe An, Pu Mat (COI OQ117858-OQ117861) (Putt et al. 2023; Takaoka 2025)
Diagnostic notesS. striatum species-group. Non-monophyletic in COI (0.74-1.98% p-distance); distinguished morphologically from S. tavanense and S. quinquestriatum by short brassy hairs on male scutum and from S. nakhonense by male upper-eye facets (Putt et al. 2023 p. 16). Compared with S. bengkuruense (Takaoka 2025 Table 1, p. 406): female frons ratio 1.4, male hind basitarsus ratio 4.4-4.6, pupal middle gill triplets not overlapping.
SourcesTakaoka 2025, Putt et al. 2023
