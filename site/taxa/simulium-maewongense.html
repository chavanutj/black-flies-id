Simulium maewongense Takaoka, Srisuka & Saeung, 2020

ClassificationGenus Simulium, subgenus Gomphostilbia
Statusvalid
DistributionThailand (Kamphaeng Phet) (Takaoka et al. 2020)
ThailandType locality: Chon Yen, Mae Wong National Park, Klong Lan District, Kamphaeng Phet Province, 1,322 m, 16°06'02.9"N 99°06'23.8"E, 12-VII-2018 (Takaoka et al. 2020, p. 74)
Diagnostic notesS. asakoae species-group (COI subgroup IV). Female: mandible lacking outer teeth (vs 4-5 teeth in S. gyorkosae), sensory vesicle 0.36x 3rd palp segment, claw tooth 0.56x claw. Male: brown upper-eye facets in 11 vertical columns and 13-14 horizontal rows (vs 14-15 columns in S. myanmarense), head nearly as wide as thorax, ventral plate rounded ventrally in caudal view (vs nearly straight in S. loeiense). Pupa: cocoon with short anterodorsal projection/bulge, primary stalks of dorsal and middle gill triplets nearly as long as common stalk (indistinguishable from S. myanmarense in pupal couplet 8b). Larva: postgenal cleft 0.9-1.2x postgenal bridge, abdominal segments 1-2 grey/green, 3-4 unpigmented, 7-8 not grey with distinct reddish brown pigment (Takaoka et al. 2020, pp. 74-77).
SourcesTakaoka et al. 2020

The chain of characters leading to this taxon in each key (a ready-made diagnosis).

Thailand: Simulium asakoae species-group (females) (Takaoka et al. 2020) – couplet 16a
  1. 1b Hair tuft of base of radial vein yellow
  2. 2b Sensory vesicle short to medium-long, less than 0.4 times length of third palpal segment (Fig. 1A)
  3. 4b Mandible without distinct teeth on outer margin (Fig. 14A)
  4. 16a Sensory vesicle 0.36 times as long as third palpal segment; claw tooth 0.56 times as long as claw
Thailand: Simulium asakoae species-group (males) (Takaoka et al. 2020) – couplet 14a
  1. 1b Hair tuft of base of radial vein yellow
  2. 2b Upper-eye (large) facets medium to dark brown
  3. 4b Antenna brownish except scape, pedicel and base of first flagellomere yellowish
  4. 5b Hind basitarsus 1.0–1.3 times as wide as hind femur (Fig. 4C)
  5. 11b Upper-eye (large) facets in 11 or more vertical columns
  6. 12a Upper-eye (large) facets in 11 vertical columns
  7. 13a Head nearly as wide as thorax
  8. 14a Ventral plate rounded ventrally when viewed caudally (Fig. 7D)
Thailand: Simulium asakoae species-group (pupae) (Takaoka et al. 2020) – couplet 8b
  1. 1b Cocoon with or without anterodorsal bulge or short anterodorsal projection (up to ca. 1.0 mm long) extended at most up to anteroventral tips of cocoon
  2. 3b Dorsum of abdominal segments 1 and 2 unpigmented or light yellowish
  3. 5b Dorsum of abdominal segment 1 without minute tubercles
  4. 7a Cocoon with short anterodorsal projection (Figs 7G, 20I)
  5. 8b Primary stalks of dorsal and middle triplets of gill filaments nearly as long as their common stalk (Fig. 7E)
Thailand: Simulium asakoae species-group (mature larvae) (Takaoka et al. 2020) – couplet 14b
  1. 1b Abdominal segments 1–4 not unicolorous
  2. 12b Abdominal color pattern otherwise
  3. 13a Abdominal segments 1 and 2 grey or green but abdominal segments 3 and 4 unpigmented
  4. 14b Abdominal segments 7 and 8 not grey with distinct reddish brown pigment (Fig. 25M)
