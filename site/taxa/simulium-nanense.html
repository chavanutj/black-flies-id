ClassificationGenus Simulium, subgenus Montisimulium
Statusvalid
DistributionThailand (Takaoka et al. 2019) | Thailand (Srisuka et al. 2017)

  2. 2b Eyebrow not well defined, only somewhat darkened at most near both ends
