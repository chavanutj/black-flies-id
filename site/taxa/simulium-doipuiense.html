Simulium doipuiense Takaoka & Choochote, 2005

ClassificationGenus Simulium, subgenus Simulium
Statusvalid
Other names in keysSimulium sp. E
DistributionThailand, Vietnam (Takaoka et al. 2019) | Thailand (Tak, northern/central regions, 400-1,800 m) | Vietnam (Lao Cai: Sa Pa 708-1,750 m; Thua Thien Hue: Bach Ma 1,187-1,274 m; Lam Dong: Dinh Kno 1,722 m) (Adler et al. 2016; Srisuka et al. 2017; Putt et al. 2023; Aupalee et al. 2024, 2026)
ThailandThailand (Takaoka et al. 2019, p. 57) | Thailand: 1,298 mature larvae (6.7% of total; 5th most predominant), 25.9% stream occurrence; predominant in Northern (15.4%, n=860) and Central (10%, n=345) regions at high elevations (Srisuka et al. 2017) | Tak Province: Umphang (Mae Klong Kee 1,188-1,264 m; Pa Deuk Dum Bun 1,263 m), n=39 females on human bait (Aupalee et al. 2024)
ImportanceKnown to bite humans in Thailand (Choochote et al. 2005; Pramual et al. 2016; cited in Takaoka et al. 2019, p. 54). | Human-biting species complex in Thailand (Putt et al. 2023; Aupalee et al. 2024, 2026).
Diagnostic notesSimulium tuberosum species-group, S. rufibasis subgroup. Species complex (Tangkawanit et al. 2009; Adler et al. 2016; cited in Takaoka et al. 2019). Recorded as Simulium sp. E in Takaoka & Suzuki (1984). | S. tuberosum species-group; species complex. Five Vietnamese cytoforms discovered by Adler et al. (2016): cytoform A (fixed IIIL-11,13; shared with Thailand, 113 larvae in Lao Cai), C (1 larva in Lam Dong, later described as S. fuscicoxae), D (fixed IIIL-11,13,79, Thua Thien Hue), E (reproductively isolated in sympatry with A at Sa Pa, X-linked IIIL-68/69/70+100hb1) and F (1 larva with IIIL-87,88,89). Wing centroid size of Thai females 2.72 ± 0.09 mm (range 2.56-2.98 mm); wing shape GM accuracy 89.74% (35/39) (Aupalee et al. 2024). COI shows high intraspecific variation (up to 7.92% in Vietnam) and non-monophyly with S. rosliramlii (0.74% min distance) and S. tenebrosum complex (ASAP/PTP/GMYC merge S. doipuiense and S. tenebrosum as 1 species; mean K2P 2.64%); separated morphologically from S. tenebrosum by hind tibia/basitarsus color and from S. rosliramlii by female antennal color, male upper-eye facets and pupal frontal tubercles; nuclear ECP1 resolves S. doipuiense vs S. tenebrosum (Putt et al. 2023; Aupalee et al. 2024).
