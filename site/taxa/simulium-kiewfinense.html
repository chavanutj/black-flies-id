Simulium kiewfinense Takaoka, Srisuka & Fukuda, 2020

ClassificationGenus Simulium, subgenus Gomphostilbia
Statusvalid
DistributionThailand (Lampang) (Takaoka et al. 2020)
ThailandType locality: Kiew Fin, Muang Pan District, Lampang Province, 1,446 m, 18°51'38.8"N 99°22'15.2"E, 6-IV-2018 (Takaoka et al. 2020, p. 117)
Diagnostic notesS. asakoae species-group (COI subgroup IV). Female and mature larva unknown. Male: brown upper-eye (large) facets in 16-17 vertical columns and 16-17 horizontal rows, subcosta bare (vs haired in S. junkumae), wing 1.9-2.0 mm, fore basitarsus 6.6-7.1x greatest width (vs 7.8-8.1 in S. junkumae and 7.5-8.4 in S. hongthaii), coxite 1.8x greatest width (vs 2.2 in S. hongthaii). Pupa: abdominal tergites 1-2 light yellowish without minute tubercles, terminal hook outer margin 2.3-2.7x inner margin (matches pupal couplet 12a though omitted from printed list) (Takaoka et al. 2020, pp. 117-119).
SourcesTakaoka et al. 2020
