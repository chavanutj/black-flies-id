Simulium huense

ClassificationGenus Simulium, subgenus Simulium
Statusvalid
Other names in keysSimulium cavum
DistributionVietnam: Thua Thien Hue (Bach Ma, 434-1,274 m; originally studied as S. cavum Takaoka & Ya'cob) (Adler et al. 2016; Putt et al. 2023)
Diagnostic notesS. tuberosum species-group. Chromosomally identical to Thai S. yuphae cytoform A (fixed IIIL-12,13; undifferentiated sex chromosomes), differing slightly in male upper-eye facet columns and pupal frontal tubercle size; either homosequential with S. yuphae A or conspecific (junior synonym of S. yuphae) (Adler et al. 2016 pp. 22-23; Putt et al. 2023 p. 15).
SourcesAdler et al. 2016, Putt et al. 2023
