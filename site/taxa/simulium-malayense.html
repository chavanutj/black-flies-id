Simulium malayense Takaoka & Davies, 1995

ClassificationGenus Simulium, subgenus Simulium
Statusvalid
DistributionPeninsular Malaysia, Thailand, Vietnam (Takaoka et al. 2019) | Peninsular Malaysia (original description, Takaoka & Davies 1995) and Thailand (Kuvangkadilok & Takaoka 2000; Takaoka & Choochote 2004). | Thailand (Srisuka et al. 2017)
ThailandThailand (Takaoka et al. 2019, p. 57) | Mae Yai Waterfall (100 m), Khao Sok NP, Surat Thani Province, southern Thailand (1 reared male from pupa on fallen leaf in 1 m stream, with S. grossifilum and S. yongi, 2.VIII.1999) (Kuvangkadilok & Takaoka 2000 p. 174); listed from Thailand (Takaoka & Choochote 2004 p. 189). | Thailand: 21 mature larvae (0.1% of total), 1.7% stream occurrence (Srisuka et al. 2017)
Diagnostic notesSimulium multistriatum species-group. Species complex (Thaijarern et al. 2018, cited in Takaoka et al. 2019). | Simulium s.str. (multistriatum species-group). Female: vittate scutum, smooth medial cibarial projection, 1 spermatheca, hind basitarsus whitish yellow on basal 3/5, sternite 8 with 10-16 long hairs/side. Male: upper eye 15 vertical columns x 16 horizontal rows, ventral plate with 2 nearly parallel vertical rows of posterior teeth. Pupa: 8 gill filaments, easily separated from other multistriatum-group species by simple wall-pocket cocoon without anterolateral windows. Larva: not separated from S. fenestratum in Takaoka & Choochote (2004 p. 196) (Kuvangkadilok & Takaoka 2000 p. 174; Takaoka & Choochote 2004 pp. 194-196).
SourcesTakaoka et al. 2019, Kuvangkadilok & Takaoka 2000, Takaoka & Choochote 2004, Srisuka et al. 2017
