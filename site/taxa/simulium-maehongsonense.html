Simulium maehongsonense Takaoka, Srisuka & Saeung, 2020

ClassificationGenus Simulium, subgenus Gomphostilbia
Statusvalid
DistributionThailand (Mae Hong Son) (Takaoka et al. 2020) | Thailand (Putt et al. 2023)
ThailandType locality: Pha Peung, Muang District, Mae Hong Son Province, 1,034 m, 19°36'58.5"N 97°59'48.2"E, 12-VII-2017 (Takaoka et al. 2020, p. 99) | Thailand (COI reference sequences LC509552, LC509554) (Putt et al. 2023)
Diagnostic notesS. asakoae species-group (COI subgroup IV). Female: short sensory vesicle (0.25-0.32x 3rd palp segment), mandible lacking distinct outer teeth, fore basitarsus 5.5x width, labrum 0.52x clypeus (vs 0.65x in S. songense). Male: brown upper-eye facets in 13 vertical columns and 14 horizontal rows, hind basitarsus 1.0-1.3x hind femur, fore basitarsus 6.5-7.3x width (vs 8.3x in S. phulocense), ventral plate nearly straight/trapezoidal ventrally in caudal view (vs rounded in S. phulocense). Pupa: cocoon without anterodorsal projection (vs with projection in S. phulocense), terminal hook outer margin 3.3-4.0x inner margin, ventral pair gill filaments 2.1-2.3 mm (vs 2.5-3.0 mm in S. pitasawatae). Larva: postgenal cleft 1.4-2.2x postgenal bridge, abdominal segment 1 grey and segments 2-4 ochreous (Takaoka et al. 2020, pp. 99-103). | S. asakoae species-group; COI intraspecific p-distance 0.00% (Putt et al. 2023).
SourcesTakaoka et al. 2020, Putt et al. 2023

The chain of characters leading to this taxon in each key (a ready-made diagnosis).

Thailand: Simulium asakoae species-group (females) (Takaoka et al. 2020) – couplet 18a
  1. 1b Hair tuft of base of radial vein yellow
  2. 2b Sensory vesicle short to medium-long, less than 0.4 times length of third palpal segment (Fig. 1A)
  3. 4b Mandible without distinct teeth on outer margin (Fig. 14A)
  4. 16b Sensory vesicle 0.25–0.32 times as long as third palpal segment; claw tooth 0.46–0.50 times as long as claw
  5. 17a Fore basitarsus 5.5 times as long as its greatest width
  6. 18a Labrum 0.52 times as long as clypeus
Thailand: Simulium asakoae species-group (males) (Takaoka et al. 2020) – couplet 21b
  1. 1b Hair tuft of base of radial vein yellow
  2. 2b Upper-eye (large) facets medium to dark brown
  3. 4b Antenna brownish except scape, pedicel and base of first flagellomere yellowish
  4. 5b Hind basitarsus 1.0–1.3 times as wide as hind femur (Fig. 4C)
  5. 11b Upper-eye (large) facets in 11 or more vertical columns
  6. 12b Upper-eye (large) facets in 12–17 vertical columns
  7. 16b Upper-eye (large) facets in 12–14 vertical columns
  8. 19a Upper-eye (large) facets in 13 or 14 horizontal rows
  9. 20b Ventral plate with ventral margin nearly straight when viewed caudally (Fig. 11F)
  10. 21b Upper-eye (large) facets in 13 vertical columns
Thailand: Simulium asakoae species-group (pupae) (Takaoka et al. 2020) – couplet 13a
  1. 1b Cocoon with or without anterodorsal bulge or short anterodorsal projection (up to ca. 1.0 mm long) extended at most up to anteroventral tips of cocoon
  2. 3b Dorsum of abdominal segments 1 and 2 unpigmented or light yellowish
  3. 5b Dorsum of abdominal segment 1 without minute tubercles
  4. 7b Cocoon without anterodorsal projection
  5. 9b Terminal hooks widened, plate-like, with outer margin over 1.5 times length of inner margin, and crenulated
  6. 10b Stalk of ventral pair thinner than interspiracular trunk
  7. 11b Gill arranged as (3+3)+2 from dorsal to ventral, with medium-long common basal stalk shorter than interspiracular trunk ⚠
  8. 12b Outer margin of terminal hook 3.3–4.0 times as long as inner margin
  9. 13a Filaments of ventral pair 2.1–2.3 mm long (Fig. 14G)
Thailand: Simulium asakoae species-group (mature larvae) (Takaoka et al. 2020) – couplet 12a
  1. 1b Abdominal segments 1–4 not unicolorous
  2. 12a Abdominal segment 1 grey but abdominal segments 2–4 ochreous (Fig. 25P)
