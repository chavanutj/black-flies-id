Simulium caudisclerum Takaoka & Davies, 1995

ClassificationGenus Simulium, subgenus Nevermannia
Statusvalid
Other names in keysSimulium sp. A
DistributionPeninsular Malaysia (Takaoka & Davies 1995); reported from Thailand (Kuvangkadilok & Takaoka 2000; Takaoka & Choochote 2004), though Thai records were later recognized as S. chomthongense (Takaoka et al. 2012, 2019; Adler 2024 marks Thailand as error).
ThailandReported from Thailand as S. sp. A (Takaoka & Suzuki 1984) and assigned to S. caudisclerum (Kuvangkadilok & Takaoka 2000 p. 167; Takaoka & Choochote 2004 p. 189); Thai populations were subsequently recognized as S. chomthongense (Takaoka et al. 2012, 2019).
Diagnostic notesNevermannia (vernum species-group). Female: antenna mostly dark, hind tibia nearly all brown, scutum brownish black without vittae. Male: inflated hind basitarsus, unvittate scutum, paramere with a single parameral hook. Pupa: 4 gill filaments. Larva: no dorsal reddish-brown markings, ventrolateral accessory sclerite on each side of last segment, mandibular serrations 1 large + 1 small tooth with supernumerary serrations (Takaoka & Choochote 2004 p. 193).
SourcesKuvangkadilok & Takaoka 2000, Takaoka & Choochote 2004
