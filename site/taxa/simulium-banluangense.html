Simulium banluangense Takaoka, Srisuka & Fukuda, 2020

ClassificationGenus Simulium, subgenus Gomphostilbia
Statusvalid
DistributionThailand (Chiang Mai) (Takaoka et al. 2020)
ThailandType locality: Ban Luang, Chiang Mai Province, 804 m, 18°32'33.3"N 98°35'32.5"E, 12-VI-2001 (Takaoka et al. 2020, p. 110)
Diagnostic notesS. asakoae species-group. Female and mature larva unknown. Male: brown upper-eye (large) facets in 14 vertical columns and 14-15 horizontal rows, fore basitarsus 6.4x greatest width, hind basitarsus spindle-shaped (0.9x hind femur), ventral plate rounded ventrally in caudal view. Pupa: gill with short common basal stalk and short stalk of ventral pair thicker than interspiracular trunk (vs medium-long 1.3x common basal stalk and thinner than trunk in S. doisaketense); terminal hooks triangular/widened plate-like (Takaoka et al. 2020, pp. 110-113).
SourcesTakaoka et al. 2020

Thailand: Simulium asakoae species-group (males) (Takaoka et al. 2020) – couplet 7a
  1. 1b Hair tuft of base of radial vein yellow
  2. 2b Upper-eye (large) facets medium to dark brown
  3. 4b Antenna brownish except scape, pedicel and base of first flagellomere yellowish
  4. 5a Hind basitarsus 0.9 time as wide as hind femur (Fig. 7A)
  5. 6a Ventral plate with ventral margin rounded when viewed caudally (Fig. 17D)
  6. 7a Fore basitarsus 6.4 times as long as its greatest width
Thailand: Simulium asakoae species-group (pupae) (Takaoka et al. 2020) – couplet 10a
  1. 1b Cocoon with or without anterodorsal bulge or short anterodorsal projection (up to ca. 1.0 mm long) extended at most up to anteroventral tips of cocoon
  2. 3b Dorsum of abdominal segments 1 and 2 unpigmented or light yellowish
  3. 5b Dorsum of abdominal segment 1 without minute tubercles
  4. 7b Cocoon without anterodorsal projection
  5. 9b Terminal hooks widened, plate-like, with outer margin over 1.5 times length of inner margin, and crenulated
  6. 10a Stalk of ventral pair thicker than interspiracular trunk (Fig. 17E, F)
