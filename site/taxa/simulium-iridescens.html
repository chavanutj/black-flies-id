Simulium iridescens De Meijere, 1913

ClassificationGenus Simulium, subgenus Simulium
Statusvalid
DistributionIndonesia: Java (type locality); Sumatran records of Takaoka et al. (2000) reassigned to S. kotsuboyui and S. solokense spp. nov., so Sumatran occurrence requires confirmation (Takaoka 2025)
ImportanceAttacked cattle in Java (Friederichs 1925, cited in Takaoka 2025 p. 391).
Diagnostic notesS. iridescens species-group. Compared with S. kotsuboyui (Takaoka 2025 p. 399): female/male mid and hind femora brownish black except base yellow; male upper-eye facets in 13-14 vertical columns and hind basitarsus slightly wider than hind tibia and femur; pupal frons covered with tubercles moderately on lower half and sparsely on upper half; pupal gill filament annular ridges with relatively short interridge distances.
