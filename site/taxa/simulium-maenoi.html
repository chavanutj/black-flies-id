Simulium maenoi Takaoka & Choochote, 2002

ClassificationGenus Simulium, subgenus Simulium
Statusvalid
DistributionThailand, Vietnam (Takaoka et al. 2019) | Thailand (Takaoka & Choochote 2004). | Northern Vietnam (Putt et al. 2023)
ThailandThailand (Takaoka et al. 2019, p. 56) | Listed from Thailand (Takaoka & Choochote 2004 p. 189).
ImportanceAttracted to humans in northern Vietnam (Putt et al. 2023 p. 2).
Diagnostic notesSimulium griseifrons species-group (Takaoka et al. 2019). | Simulium s.str. (griseifrons species-group). Female: vittate scutum, bare basal radial vein, mid tibia white on basal 2/3-3/4, cibarium with tuberculate medial projection and bare near base. Male: hind basitarsus entirely light to dark brown, ventral plate gradually narrowed posteriorly without posterior teeth. Pupa: 6 gill filaments decreasing in thickness ventrally, wall-pocket cocoon with small anterolateral window, frons with very large tubercles, terminal hooks present. Larva: body 5.9-6.4 mm, elongate spot beside postgenal cleft positive, rectal organ 8-12 lobules/lobe (Takaoka & Choochote 2004 pp. 194-197).
SourcesTakaoka et al. 2019, Takaoka & Choochote 2004, Putt et al. 2023
