Simulium novemarticulatum Takaoka & Davies, 1995

ClassificationGenus Simulium, subgenus Gomphostilbia
Statusvalid
DistributionPeninsular Malaysia, Thailand (Takaoka et al. 2019) | Peninsular Malaysia, Thailand (Srisuka et al. 2022)
ThailandThailand (Takaoka et al. 2019, p. 56) | Thailand (Takaoka et al. 2010, cited in Srisuka et al. 2022, p. 2)
Diagnostic notesSimulium varicorne species-group, S. novemarticulatum subgroup (Takaoka et al. 2019). | S. varicorne species-group. Female: antenna with 7 flagellomeres; sensory vesicle 0.21-0.25x length of third palpal segment (vs 0.29-0.31x in S. charlesi) (Srisuka et al. 2022, p. 9).
SourcesTakaoka et al. 2019, Srisuka et al. 2022
