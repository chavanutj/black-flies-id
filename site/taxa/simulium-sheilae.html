Simulium sheilae Takaoka & Davies, 1995

ClassificationGenus Simulium, subgenus Gomphostilbia
Statusvalid
DistributionMalaysia (Peninsular, Sarawak, Sabah), Thailand (Takaoka et al. 2019) | Peninsular Malaysia (original description, Takaoka & Davies 1995) and Thailand (Kuvangkadilok & Takaoka 2000; Takaoka & Choochote 2004). | Malaysia (Peninsular Malaysia [type locality], Sabah, Sarawak); Thailand (all 6 regions); Indonesia (Sumatra) (Takaoka & Tenedero 2022a; Srisuka et al. 2017)
ThailandThailand (Takaoka et al. 2019, p. 55) | Boripat Waterfall (50 m), Thalay Bun NP, Songkhla Province (1 male, 4 pupae, 255 larvae, 1.VI.1999), Tone Phrew Waterfall (125 m), Khao Ban Tad WS, Trang Province (3 females, 1 male, 6 pupae, 32 larvae, 2.VI.1999), Huai Yang Waterfall (75 m), Prachuap Khiri Khan Province (1 male, 4.VI.1999) (Kuvangkadilok & Takaoka 2000 pp. 172-173); listed from Thailand (Takaoka & Choochote 2004 p. 189). | Thailand: 556 mature larvae (2.9% of total), 44.8% stream occurrence across all 6 regions (lowland streams); in Southern region present at 100% of sites (12.1%, n=289) and in Western region at 62.5% of sites (Srisuka et al. 2017)
Diagnostic notesSimulium ceylonicum species-group, S. sheilae subgroup. Species complex (Jitklang et al. 2008, cited in Takaoka et al. 2019). | Gomphostilbia (ceylonicum species-group). Female: enlarged oblong sensory vesicle (0.68x or ca. 0.7x length of 3rd maxillary palpal segment), frons-head ratio 1.0:5.7, hind tibia whitish yellow on basal 2/5-1/2 without dark subbasal spot. Male: enlarged hind basitarsus, upper eye 13 horizontal rows of large facets, hind tibia mostly brown-black with basal 1/3 or less pale (Kuvangkadilok & Takaoka 2000 p. 173 also notes almost brown male hind basitarsus). Pupa: 8 long filaments (>1.9 mm), cocoon with anterodorsal bulge, terminal hooks weakly undulate on outer margin. Larva: lacking dorsal protuberances, postgenal cleft very long reaching hypostomium, abdomen not constricted between segments 4 and 5 (Kuvangkadilok & Takaoka 2000 pp. 172-173; Takaoka & Choochote 2004 pp. 191-192). | S. ceylonicum species-group. Compared with S. apoense (Takaoka & Tenedero 2022a p. 337): male sensory vesicle 0.23x 3rd palpal segment, hind basitarsus 0.9x tibia and 1.1x femur; pupal terminal hooks wider basally with undulate outer margins.
SourcesTakaoka et al. 2019, Kuvangkadilok & Takaoka 2000, Takaoka & Choochote 2004, Takaoka & Tenedero 2022a, Srisuka et al. 2017
