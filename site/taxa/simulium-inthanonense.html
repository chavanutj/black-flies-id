Simulium inthanonense Takaoka & Suzuki, 1984

ClassificationGenus Simulium, subgenus Gomphostilbia
Statusvalid
DistributionChina (Yunnan), Thailand, Vietnam (Takaoka et al. 2019) | Thailand (Takaoka & Choochote 2004). | Thailand (Chiang Mai), China, Vietnam (Takaoka et al. 2020) | Thailand (Srisuka et al. 2017; Takaoka 2025)
ThailandThailand (Takaoka et al. 2019, p. 55) | Listed from Thailand (Takaoka & Choochote 2004 p. 189). | Doi Inthanon NP, Chiang Mai Province (Takaoka et al. 2020, pp. 135-140) | Thailand: 753 mature larvae (3.9% of total), 19.0% stream occurrence; 2nd most predominant in Northern region (11.6%, n=647) and strongly associated with high elevation (Srisuka et al. 2017)
Diagnostic notesSimulium ceylonicum species-group, S. sheilae subgroup (Takaoka et al. 2019). | Gomphostilbia (placed in ceylonicum species-group in Takaoka & Choochote 2004 and Takaoka et al. 2019; later transferred to asakoae group in Takaoka et al. 2020). Female: hind tibia whitish yellow on basal 2/5 or 1/2 without subbasal spot, frons-head ratio 1.0:4.8, sensory vesicle 0.57x 3rd palpal segment. Male: enlarged hind basitarsus, upper eye with 16 horizontal rows of large facets. Pupa: 8 long gill filaments (>1.9 mm), cocoon with distinct anterodorsal projection. Larva: postgenal cleft short, much shorter than postgenal bridge (Takaoka & Choochote 2004 pp. 191-192). | Transferred from S. ceylonicum species-group to S. asakoae species-group (COI subgroup V, sole member) based on laterally emarginated male ventral plate and COI phylogeny; male fully described for the first time and female/pupa/larva redescribed (Takaoka et al. 2020, pp. 135-140, 147). Female and male: brownish hair tuft at base of radial vein (departing from typical yellow tuft of asakoae group); female sensory vesicle relatively long, mandible with distinct outer teeth; male upper-eye facets in 17 vertical columns and 17 (rarely 18) horizontal rows, subcosta bare, hind basitarsus narrower than hind tibia and femur. Pupa: abdominal tergites 1-5 light greyish brown, cocoon with short anterodorsal projection. Larva: postgenal cleft <= postgenal bridge, two colour morphs: morph 1 (segments 1-3 greenish grey) and morph 2 (segments 1 and 3 greenish grey) (Takaoka et al. 2020). | S. asakoae species-group; shares dark brown hair tuft at base of radial vein with S. kandisense, differing by male upper-eye large facets in 17 vertical columns and 17-18 horizontal rows (vs 13 x 15 in S. kandisense) (Takaoka 2025 p. 394).
SourcesTakaoka et al. 2019, Takaoka & Choochote 2004, Takaoka et al. 2020, Takaoka 2025, Srisuka et al. 2017
