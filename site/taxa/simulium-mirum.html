Simulium mirum Takaoka, Sofian-Azirun & Low, 2016

ClassificationGenus Simulium, subgenus Simulium
Statusvalid
Other names in keysSimulium laterale (sensu Smart & Clifford 1969)
DistributionBorneo: Malaysia (Sabah: Mt. Kinabalu 1,591-1,728 m [type locality], Mesilau 1,714 m; Sarawak: Bakalalan 928 m, Bario, Pueh) and Indonesia (Kalimantan) (Takaoka et al. 2016)
Diagnostic notesS. melanopus species-group; formerly misidentified as S. laterale by Smart & Clifford (1969) and Simulium sp. by Takaoka et al. (2015). First confirmed genetically-inherited male dimorphism in Simuliidae (Form A and Form B confirmed conspecific by COI+COII+12S+16S, intraspecific 0-1.10% vs 5.34-6.26% to related species). Male (Form A n=8, Form B n=7, Takaoka et al. 2016 pp. 5-11): body 2.5-3.5 mm; Form A scutum brilliantly iridescent with median vitta and round lateral nonpruinose spots + upper-eye facets 12-14 columns x 15-16 rows; Form B scutum with inverted-T nonpruinose portion + upper-eye facets 16-17 columns x 17-18 rows (separated from S. timpohonense by entirely darkened hind tibia); fore coxa yellow; fore basitarsus dilated (4.33x); hind basitarsus enlarged (3.95x; 0.93x tibia, 1.07x femur); ventral plate with dentate posterolateral margins. Female (n=10): body 2.5-2.8 mm; fore coxa yellow; basal radial vein haired and hind tibia entirely darkened (separating from S. maklarini and S. cheedhangi); cibarium with 75 minute processes; claw with small subbasal tooth; sternite 7 with large sternal plate. Pupa (n=25): 2.7-3.6 mm; head bare except medial area around facial trichomes covered with tubercles (absent in S. maklarini); 6 gill filaments (2+2+2, 0.7-1.4 mm) with sharply edged reticulate annular ridges; no terminal hooks; shoe-shaped cocoon (3.2-3.7 x 1.2-1.5 mm). Mature larva (n=7): 4.5-5.2 mm; antennal segments 1.00:1.15-1.19:0.65-0.73 (vs 0.95-0.98 for 2nd/1st in S. cheedhangi); labral fan 40-41 rays; postgenal cleft 3.0-3.3x bridge; posterior circlet 84-90 rows (vs 115-170 in S. laterale).
SourcesTakaoka et al. 2016
