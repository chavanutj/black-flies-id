Simulium brevipar Takaoka & Davies, 1995

ClassificationGenus Simulium, subgenus Simulium
Statusvalid
DistributionPeninsular Malaysia, Indonesia (Sumatra), Thailand, Vietnam (Takaoka et al. 2019) | Thailand (Takaoka & Choochote 2004). | Malaysia: Peninsular Malaysia (Cameron Highlands, Brinchang 1,813 m and Tapah 711 m = cytoform A) | Thailand (confirmed as cytoform A) | Vietnam (Tam Dao = cytoform B, later S. lowi; Sa Pa = cytoform C) (Adler et al. 2016; Srisuka et al. 2017; Putt et al. 2023)
ThailandThailand (Takaoka et al. 2019, p. 57) | Listed from Thailand (Takaoka & Choochote 2004 p. 190; some earlier Thai records refer to S. yuphae per Takaoka et al. 2019). | Thailand: 16 mature larvae (0.1% of total), 1.7% stream occurrence (Srisuka et al. 2017); presence of S. brevipar cytoform A (s.str.) confirmed chromosomally in Thailand (Adler et al. 2016 p. 21)
Diagnostic notesSimulium tuberosum species-group, S. brevipar subgroup (Takaoka et al. 2019). | Simulium s.str. (tuberosum species-group). Female: unvittate scutum with dark brown hairs only, no ventral hair cluster on abdominal segment 7, hind basitarsus white on basal 3/5 or more, sensory vesicle medium-sized (0.3x length of 3rd palpal segment). Male: enlarged wedge-shaped hind basitarsus pale on basal 3/5 or slightly less, dorsolateral silvery spots on abdominal segments 2, 6 and 7. Pupa: 6 almost sessile gill filaments, no terminal hooks, thoracic integument with pit-like organ at base of gill. Larva: dark grey body, rectal organ with 7-12 secondary lobules per lobe, posterior circlet 70-74 rows of up to 12-14 hooklets (Takaoka & Choochote 2004 pp. 194-197). | S. tuberosum species-group. Cytoform A (s.str., Malaysia/Thailand) is fixed for IIIL-13 with autosomal polymorphism IS-26; Vietnamese cytoform B (fixed IIIL-13,81,82; later formally described as S. lowi) and cytoform C (1 female larva from Sa Pa with homozygous IIIL-84,85,86 on top of IIIL-13) were discovered by Adler et al. (2016).
SourcesTakaoka et al. 2019, Takaoka & Choochote 2004, Adler et al. 2016, Srisuka et al. 2017
