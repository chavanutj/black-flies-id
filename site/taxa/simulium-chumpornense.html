Simulium chumpornense Takaoka & Kuvangkadilok, 2000

ClassificationGenus Simulium, subgenus Gomphostilbia
Statusvalid
DistributionThailand (Takaoka et al. 2019) | Thailand (Kuvangkadilok & Takaoka 2000; Takaoka & Choochote 2004). | Thailand (Srisuka et al. 2022) | Thailand (Srisuka et al. 2017; Aupalee et al. 2024, 2026)
ThailandThailand (Takaoka et al. 2019, p. 55) | Type locality: Ka Po Waterfall (altitude 40 m), Chumphon Province ('Chumporn Province' in text), southern Thailand (4.VI.1999, trailing grasses in 5-6 m stream, 26 C, pH 9.3, with S. nobile; holotype female, paratypes 1 male, 2 pupae, 39 larvae) (Kuvangkadilok & Takaoka 2000 p. 171); listed from Thailand (Takaoka & Choochote 2004 p. 189). | Thailand (Doi Inthanon NP, Chiang Mai; Srisuka et al. 2022, p. 2) | Thailand: 182 mature larvae (0.9% of total), 27.6% stream occurrence across all 6 regions (lowland streams); recorded only in cold season in northern region (Srisuka et al. 2017)
ImportanceCaptured using human attractants in Doi Inthanon NP (Choochote et al. 2005); natural vector of Leucocytozoon and Trypanosoma blood parasites (Thaijarern et al. 2019; Pramual et al. 2020, cited in Srisuka et al. 2022, p. 2). | Blood meal cytb analysis confirmed human blood feeding in Thailand (Gomontean et al. 2024, cited in Aupalee et al. 2024, 2026); natural/potential vector of avian Leucocytozoon spp. and Trypanosoma avium in domestic chickens and wild birds in Thailand (Putt et al. 2023; Aupalee et al. 2024, 2026).
Diagnostic notesSimulium varicorne species-group, S. chumpornense subgroup (Takaoka et al. 2019). Described in Kuvangkadilok & Takaoka (2000). | Gomphostilbia (varicorne species-group); described by Takaoka & Kuvangkadilok in Kuvangkadilok & Takaoka (2000 pp. 167-172). Antenna 10-segmented (2+8, separating from 9-segmented S. novemarticulatum); scape, pedicel, 2nd and 4th flagellar segments yellow (differs from S. burtoni). Female (body 1.8 mm, wing 1.6 mm): sensory vesicle ellipsoidal, 0.36x length of 3rd palpal segment (vs globular in S. shogakii); hind tibia much angulate posteriorly at basal and distal 1/3; tergites 2, 6-8 shiny (tergite 5 dull, separating from S. burtoni); spermatheca oblong (1.8x as long as wide). Male (ca. 2.0 mm, head lost): ventral plate transverse and nearly flat (similar to S. varicorne; produced ventrally in S. burtoni and S. shogakii); paramere with 3 long hooks and several short incomplete ones. Pupa (2.0 mm): uniquely arranged 8 short slender gill filaments (1.2-1.6 mm) in 2 groups (1 dorsal stout trunk giving rise to 5 filaments + 1 ventral stout trunk bifurcating into 2 filaments; vs 3+3+2 in S. varicorne, S. shogakii, S. burtoni and S. novemarticulatum); antennal sheath smooth; 3 grapnel-like hooklets per side on segment 9; wall-pocket cocoon 2.8 x 1.5-1.8 mm. Larva (3.8-4.0 mm): labral fan 28-31 rays; thoracic segment 3 and abdominal segments 1-5 each with 2 pairs (dorsal and dorsolateral) of conical protuberances; postgenal cleft deep leaving narrow bridge; rectal gill 8 lobules/lobe; posterior circlet ca. 60 rows of up to 11 hooklets (Kuvangkadilok & Takaoka 2000 pp. 167-172; Takaoka & Choochote 2004 pp. 191-192). | S. varicorne species-group, S. chumpornense subgroup. Female: antenna with 8 flagellomeres, pleural membrane bare, subcosta bare, hind tibia darkened on apical 1/3, head 3.9-4.0x greatest width of frons, height of frons 1.7x narrowest width (vs 1.3-1.4x in S. sumbaense) (Srisuka et al. 2022, pp. 8-9).

  5. 5a Hind tibia yellowish white on basal 2/3

  3. 3b Ventral plate nearly flat
  4. 4a Upper-eye facets in 15 vertical columns and 15 horizontal rows
Thailand: Simulium (Gomphostilbia) pupae (Takaoka et al. 2019) – couplet 15a
  1. 1b Gill filamentous
  2. 7b Gill with 8 or 10 filaments
  3. 10b Gill with 8 filaments
  4. 12b Frons and anterodorsal part of thorax with tubercles
  5. 13b Face with 1 trichome on each side
  6. 14b Gill filaments arranged as 3+3+2 or 6+2 from dorsal to ventral
  7. 15a Gill filaments arranged in 2 groups, i.e. one dorsal (4 individual and 2 paired filaments) and one ventral (2 paired filaments)
