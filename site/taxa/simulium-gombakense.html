Simulium gombakense Takaoka & Davies, 1995

ClassificationGenus Simulium, subgenus Gomphostilbia
Statusvalid
Other names in keysSimulium (Morops) gombakense
DistributionPeninsular Malaysia, Myanmar, Thailand (Takaoka et al. 2019) | Peninsular Malaysia (original description, Takaoka & Davies 1995) and Thailand (Kuvangkadilok & Takaoka 2000; Takaoka & Choochote 2004). | Thailand (Srisuka et al. 2017)
ThailandThailand (Takaoka et al. 2019, p. 55) | Mae Klang Waterfall (940 m), Doi Inthanon NP, Chiang Mai Province, northern Thailand (18 larvae, 18.V.1997) and Riang Thong Waterfall (175 m), Khao-Pu Khao-Ya NP, Phatthalung Province, southern Thailand (1 reared female from pupa, 2.VI.1999) (Kuvangkadilok & Takaoka 2000 p. 173); listed from Thailand (Takaoka & Choochote 2004 p. 189; Thai populations later shown to include S. gombakense s.str. and S. paiense complex in Takaoka et al. 2018e, 2019). | Thailand: 90 mature larvae (0.5% of total), 13.8% stream occurrence; wide elevational range from 412 m (Mae Klang Waterfall, Doi Inthanon) and 500 m foothills up to 2,100 m on Doi Pha Hom Pok (Srisuka et al. 2017)
Diagnostic notesSimulium gombakense species-group (Takaoka et al. 2019). Thai populations formerly under S. gombakense were shown to consist of S. gombakense s. str. and three lineages of S. paiense (complex) (Takaoka et al. 2018e, cited in Takaoka et al. 2019, p. 54). | Gomphostilbia (originally described in subgenus Morops by Takaoka & Davies 1995, transferred to Gomphostilbia by Takaoka 2000 as cited in Kuvangkadilok & Takaoka 2000 p. 173; placed in batoense group in Takaoka & Choochote 2004, now in its own gombakense species-group in Takaoka et al. 2019). Female omitted as unknown in Takaoka & Choochote (2004 p. 191, although Kuvangkadilok & Takaoka 2000 p. 173 noted 1 reared female from Phatthalung). Male: slender hind basitarsus, hind tibia whitish yellow on basal 1/2 without dark subbasal spot. Pupa: distinctive much-inflated gill with 2 triplet groups of 6 finger-like projections and 8 slender thread-like filaments. Larva: abdomen almost bare except last segment, pharate pupal gill inflated (Kuvangkadilok & Takaoka 2000 p. 173; Takaoka & Choochote 2004 pp. 192).
SourcesTakaoka et al. 2019, Kuvangkadilok & Takaoka 2000, Takaoka & Choochote 2004, Srisuka et al. 2017
