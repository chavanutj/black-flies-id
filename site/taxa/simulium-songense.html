Simulium songense Takaoka, Srisuka & Fukuda, 2020

ClassificationGenus Simulium, subgenus Gomphostilbia
Statusvalid
DistributionThailand (Nan and Phrae) (Takaoka et al. 2020) | Thailand (Putt et al. 2023)
ThailandType locality: Nam Dan Village, Pua District, Nan Province, 1,157 m, 19°11'10.3"N 101°04'41.7"E, 25-VII-2017; also Phrae (Takaoka et al. 2020, pp. 124-126) | Thailand (COI reference sequences LC509528-LC509529) (Putt et al. 2023)
Diagnostic notesS. asakoae species-group (COI subgroup IV). Male and mature larva unknown. Female: mandible without distinct outer teeth, sensory vesicle 0.25-0.32x 3rd palp segment, fore basitarsus 5.5x greatest width (vs 6.0x in S. myanmarense and 6.7x in S. monglaense), labrum 0.65x clypeus (vs 0.52x in S. maehongsonense). Pupa: short stalk of ventral gill pair half length of interspiracular trunk, terminal hook outer/inner margin 2.0-2.9 (couplet 12a) (Takaoka et al. 2020, pp. 124-126). | S. asakoae species-group; COI intraspecific p-distance 0.25% (Putt et al. 2023).
SourcesTakaoka et al. 2020, Putt et al. 2023

The chain of characters leading to this taxon in each key (a ready-made diagnosis).

Thailand: Simulium asakoae species-group (females) (Takaoka et al. 2020) – couplet 18b
  1. 1b Hair tuft of base of radial vein yellow
  2. 2b Sensory vesicle short to medium-long, less than 0.4 times length of third palpal segment (Fig. 1A)
  3. 4b Mandible without distinct teeth on outer margin (Fig. 14A)
  4. 16b Sensory vesicle 0.25–0.32 times as long as third palpal segment; claw tooth 0.46–0.50 times as long as claw
  5. 17a Fore basitarsus 5.5 times as long as its greatest width
  6. 18b Labrum 0.65 times as long as clypeus
Thailand: Simulium asakoae species-group (pupae) (Takaoka et al. 2020) – couplet 12a
  1. 1b Cocoon with or without anterodorsal bulge or short anterodorsal projection (up to ca. 1.0 mm long) extended at most up to anteroventral tips of cocoon
  2. 3b Dorsum of abdominal segments 1 and 2 unpigmented or light yellowish
  3. 5b Dorsum of abdominal segment 1 without minute tubercles
  4. 7b Cocoon without anterodorsal projection
  5. 9b Terminal hooks widened, plate-like, with outer margin over 1.5 times length of inner margin, and crenulated
  6. 10b Stalk of ventral pair thinner than interspiracular trunk
  7. 11b Gill arranged as (3+3)+2 from dorsal to ventral, with medium-long common basal stalk shorter than interspiracular trunk ⚠
  8. 12a Outer margin of terminal hook 2.0–2.9 times as long as inner margin ⚠
