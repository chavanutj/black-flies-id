Simulium yongi Takaoka & Davies, 1997

ClassificationGenus Simulium, subgenus Simulium
Statusvalid
DistributionPeninsular Malaysia, Thailand (Takaoka et al. 2019) | Peninsular Malaysia (original description, Takaoka & Davies 1997) and Thailand (Kuvangkadilok & Takaoka 2000; Takaoka & Choochote 2004). | Thailand (Srisuka et al. 2017)
ThailandThailand (Takaoka et al. 2019, p. 56) | Mae Yai Waterfall (100 m), Khao Sok NP, Surat Thani Province, southern Thailand (1 reared female from pupa on fallen leaf in ca. 1.0 m stream, with S. grossifilum and S. malayense, 2.VIII.1999) (Kuvangkadilok & Takaoka 2000 p. 175); listed from Thailand (Takaoka & Choochote 2004 p. 189). | Thailand: 38 mature larvae (0.2% of total), 3.5% stream occurrence; recorded only in cold season in central region (Srisuka et al. 2017)
Diagnostic notesSimulium crocinum species-group (Takaoka et al. 2019). | Simulium s.str. (placed in griseifrons species-group in Takaoka & Choochote 2004; transferred to crocinum species-group in Takaoka et al. 2019). Female: unvittate scutum with yellowish and dark hairs, darker legs than S. rudnicki, hind basitarsus white on slightly more than basal 1/2, no ventral hair cluster on abdominal segment 7. Male: ventral plate body ca. 1.8x as wide as long and much shorter than arms. Pupa: 6 gill filaments with inner filament of ventralmost pair narrowed basally, very thin transparent wall-pocket cocoon without windows, head/thorax bare, dorsal spine-combs on segment 8, no terminal hooks. Larva: abdomen widest on segment 8, cephalic apotome mostly pale yellow (Kuvangkadilok & Takaoka 2000 p. 175; Takaoka & Choochote 2004 pp. 194-196).
SourcesTakaoka et al. 2019, Kuvangkadilok & Takaoka 2000, Takaoka & Choochote 2004, Srisuka et al. 2017
