Simulium myanmarense Takaoka, Srisuka & Saeung, 2017

ClassificationGenus Simulium, subgenus Gomphostilbia
Statusvalid
DistributionMyanmar, Thailand (Phayao and Lampang) (Takaoka et al. 2020) | Myanmar; Thailand (Putt et al. 2023; Aupalee et al. 2024, 2026)
ThailandConfirmed for Thailand morphologically and genetically: Pha Dang Village, Chiang Kham District, Phayao Province, 901 m, 20-III-2018; Pa Miang Village, Muang Pan District, Lampang Province, 1,097 m, 9-VIII-2016 (Takaoka et al. 2020, pp. 135, 149) | Thailand: Chiang Mai, Pang-Bong village, Doi Saket (990 m), n=2 infected wild females confirmed by COI (99.70-99.83% identity; haplotypes AK-H3, AK-H4) (Aupalee et al. 2026 Table 5)
ImportanceWild females caught on human bait in Pang-Bong village, Chiang Mai (Aupalee et al. 2026 Tables 3-5): 2/100 individual S. asakoae group flies identified as S. myanmarense tested positive in head+thorax for Leucocytozoon schoutedeni (LC-13, n=1) and Trypanosoma avium (TP-01, n=1); potential vector of avian blood protozoa.
Diagnostic notesS. asakoae species-group (COI subgroup VII). Female: mandible lacking outer teeth, fore basitarsus 6.0x width (5.9-6.5 in key), hind basitarsus 6.5x width. Male: brown upper-eye facets in 15 (rarely 14) vertical columns and 15 (rarely 16) horizontal rows, hind basitarsus 0.9x hind femur, ventral plate rounded ventrally, wing 2.0 mm (vs 1.6-1.7 mm in S. chaowaense). Pupa: cocoon with short anterodorsal projection, primary stalks of dorsal and middle gill triplets subequal to common stalk (couplet 8b with S. maewongense). Larva: abdominal segments 1-2 greyish, 3-4 unpigmented, 7-8 grey with faint reddish brown pigment (Takaoka et al. 2020, pp. 135, 141-146). | S. asakoae species-group; COI intraspecific p-distance 0.25-0.50% (Putt et al. 2023); non-S. asakoae band pattern (709 bp only) in multiplex PCR (Aupalee et al. 2026).
SourcesTakaoka et al. 2020, Putt et al. 2023, Aupalee et al. 2024, Aupalee et al. 2026

How it keys out

The chain of characters leading to this taxon in each key (a ready-made diagnosis).

Thailand: Simulium asakoae species-group (females) (Takaoka et al. 2020) – couplet 19a
  1. 1b Hair tuft of base of radial vein yellow
  2. 2b Sensory vesicle short to medium-long, less than 0.4 times length of third palpal segment (Fig. 1A)
  3. 4b Mandible without distinct teeth on outer margin (Fig. 14A)
  4. 16b Sensory vesicle 0.25–0.32 times as long as third palpal segment; claw tooth 0.46–0.50 times as long as claw
  5. 17b Fore basitarsus 5.9–6.5 times as long as its greatest width
  6. 19a Hind basitarsus 6.5 times as long as its greatest width
Thailand: Simulium asakoae species-group (males) (Takaoka et al. 2020) – couplet 8b
  1. 1b Hair tuft of base of radial vein yellow
  2. 2b Upper-eye (large) facets medium to dark brown
  3. 4b Antenna brownish except scape, pedicel and base of first flagellomere yellowish
  4. 5a Hind basitarsus 0.9 time as wide as hind femur (Fig. 7A)
  5. 6a Ventral plate with ventral margin rounded when viewed caudally (Fig. 17D)
  6. 7b Fore basitarsus 7.4–8.8 times as long as its greatest width
  7. 8b Wing length 2.0 mm
Thailand: Simulium asakoae species-group (pupae) (Takaoka et al. 2020) – couplet 8b
  1. 1b Cocoon with or without anterodorsal bulge or short anterodorsal projection (up to ca. 1.0 mm long) extended at most up to anteroventral tips of cocoon
  2. 3b Dorsum of abdominal segments 1 and 2 unpigmented or light yellowish
  3. 5b Dorsum of abdominal segment 1 without minute tubercles
  4. 7a Cocoon with short anterodorsal projection (Figs 7G, 20I)
  5. 8b Primary stalks of dorsal and middle triplets of gill filaments nearly as long as their common stalk (Fig. 7E)
Thailand: Simulium asakoae species-group (mature larvae) (Takaoka et al. 2020) – couplet 14a
  1. 1b Abdominal segments 1–4 not unicolorous
  2. 12b Abdominal color pattern otherwise
  3. 13a Abdominal segments 1 and 2 grey or green but abdominal segments 3 and 4 unpigmented
  4. 14a Abdominal segments 7 and 8 grey with faint reddish brown pigment (Fig. 25L)
