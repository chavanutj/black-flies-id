  2. 15b Scutum without dorsal vittae
  3. 36b Mid tarsomeres 2 and 3 light brown to brownish black
  4. 37a Mid femur entirely yellow
  5. 38a Mid tibia yellowish white with apical cap light to medium brown
Thailand: Simulium (Simulium) males (Takaoka et al. 2019) – couplet 31a
  1. 1b Basal section of radius bare or with few hairs apically
  2. 7b Scutum otherwise
  3. 9b Mid femur and tibia otherwise
  4. 11b Mid tibia whitish or yellowish (at least on posterior surface) on basal 2/5 or more and dark brown on rest
  5. 29a Hind basitarsus whitish on basal 1/2 or little more and brownish black on rest. Body of ventral plate wider than long
  6. 30b Scutum covered with golden-yellow recumbent hairs interspersed with brown hairs anteriorly
  7. 31a Ventral plate with body nearly quadrate, shorter than arms

  1. 1b Gill with 6-10 fiaments ⚠
  2. 4a Gill with 6 filaments

  4. 7a Cocoon wall-pocket shaped
  5. 8a Cocoon with lateral windows anteriorly
  6. 9a Gill filaments subequal in length and thickness to one another
  7. 10b Gill filaments almost sessile. Spine-combs present only on abdominal segment 8
  8. 12a Small raised area near base of gill with tubercles
