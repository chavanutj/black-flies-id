Simulium junkumae Takaoka, Srisuka & Saeung, 2020

ClassificationGenus Simulium, subgenus Gomphostilbia
Statusvalid
DistributionThailand (Chiang Mai) (Takaoka et al. 2020)
ThailandType locality: Doi Pui Temple, Doi Suthep-Pui, Muang District, Chiang Mai Province, 1,395 m, 18°49'09.5"N 98°53'14.3"E, 7-IV-2017 (Takaoka et al. 2020, p. 113)
Diagnostic notesS. asakoae species-group (COI subgroup IV). Female: mandible without outer teeth, narrow frons (frons:head ratio 1.0:4.7-5.5 vs 1.0:4.1-4.2 in S. hongthaii and 1.0:4.0 in S. muangpanense), hind basitarsus 5.9-6.2x width. Male: upper-eye facets in 15-17 vertical columns and 15-17 horizontal rows, subcosta with 7-12 hairs (vs bare in S. hongthaii), wing length 2.2-2.5 mm, ventral plate ventral margin nearly straight in caudal view (vs rounded in S. rampae). Pupa: terminal hook outer/inner margin 2.0-2.9 (couplet 12a). Larva: labral fan 24-25 primary rays, postgenal cleft <= postgenal bridge, abdominal segments 1-3 greyish, 7-8 light ochreous with reddish brown pigment (Takaoka et al. 2020, pp. 113-117).
SourcesTakaoka et al. 2020

  1. 1b Hair tuft of base of radial vein yellow
  2. 2b Sensory vesicle short to medium-long, less than 0.4 times length of third palpal segment (Fig. 1A)
  3. 4b Mandible without distinct teeth on outer margin (Fig. 14A)
  4. 16b Sensory vesicle 0.25–0.32 times as long as third palpal segment; claw tooth 0.46–0.50 times as long as claw
  5. 17b Fore basitarsus 5.9–6.5 times as long as its greatest width
  6. 19b Hind basitarsus 5.9–6.2 times as long as its greatest width
  7. 20a Frons: head ratio 1.0:4.7–5.5

  1. 1b Hair tuft of base of radial vein yellow
  2. 2b Upper-eye (large) facets medium to dark brown
  3. 4b Antenna brownish except scape, pedicel and base of first flagellomere yellowish
  4. 5b Hind basitarsus 1.0–1.3 times as wide as hind femur (Fig. 4C)
  5. 11b Upper-eye (large) facets in 11 or more vertical columns
  6. 12b Upper-eye (large) facets in 12–17 vertical columns
  7. 16a Upper-eye (large) facets in 16 or 17 vertical columns
  8. 17b Subcosta with 7–12 hairs; wing length 2.2–2.5 mm
  9. 18a Ventral plate with ventral margin nearly straight when viewed caudally (Fig. 18I)
Thailand: Simulium asakoae species-group (pupae) (Takaoka et al. 2020) – couplet 12a
  1. 1b Cocoon with or without anterodorsal bulge or short anterodorsal projection (up to ca. 1.0 mm long) extended at most up to anteroventral tips of cocoon
  2. 3b Dorsum of abdominal segments 1 and 2 unpigmented or light yellowish
  3. 5b Dorsum of abdominal segment 1 without minute tubercles
  4. 7b Cocoon without anterodorsal projection
  5. 9b Terminal hooks widened, plate-like, with outer margin over 1.5 times length of inner margin, and crenulated
  6. 10b Stalk of ventral pair thinner than interspiracular trunk
  7. 11b Gill arranged as (3+3)+2 from dorsal to ventral, with medium-long common basal stalk shorter than interspiracular trunk ⚠
  8. 12a Outer margin of terminal hook 2.0–2.9 times as long as inner margin ⚠
