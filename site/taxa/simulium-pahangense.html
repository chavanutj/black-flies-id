Simulium pahangense Takaoka & Davies, 1995

ClassificationGenus Simulium, subgenus Daviesellum
Statusvalid
Other names in keysSulcicnephia unidens
DistributionPeninsular Malaysia, Thailand (Takaoka et al. 2019) | Peninsular Malaysia and Thailand (Takaoka & Choochote 2004). | Thailand (Srisuka et al. 2017)
ThailandThailand (Takaoka et al. 2019, p. 55) | Listed from Thailand (Takaoka & Choochote 2004 p. 189). | Thailand: 9 mature larvae (0.1% of total), 5.2% stream occurrence; high elevations from northern to central regions along Myanmar border (rainy season in north) and lowland streams in southern region (Srisuka et al. 2017)
Diagnostic notesSubgenus Daviesellum (Takaoka et al. 2019). Synonym: Sulcicnephia unidens Takaoka & Davies, 1995 (printed as 'Sulchichnephia unidens' on p. 55). | Daviesellum. Distinguished from S. courtneyi: female genital fork without anteriorly directed projection; male ventral plate much shorter than wide in ventral view; pupal frons mostly bare (tuberculate only narrowly near lower margin); larval postgenal cleft long with apex reaching posterior margin of hypostomium (Takaoka & Choochote 2004 p. 191).
SourcesTakaoka et al. 2019, Takaoka & Choochote 2004, Srisuka et al. 2017
