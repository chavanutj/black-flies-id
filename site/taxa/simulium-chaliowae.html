Simulium chaliowae Takaoka & Boonkemtong, 1999

ClassificationGenus Simulium, subgenus Simulium
Statusvalid
DistributionThailand (Takaoka et al. 2019) | Thailand (Takaoka & Kuvangkadilok 1999; Takaoka & Choochote 2004).
ThailandThailand (Takaoka et al. 2019, p. 57) | Type locality: Maekham Waterfall, Phrae Province ('Phare Province' in text), northern Thailand (altitude 290 m, 20 C, on fallen leaves in fast-flowing 3 m stream, 10.XII.1998; holotype female, paratypes 1 female, 1 male, 1 pharate female, 1 pharate male) (Takaoka & Kuvangkadilok 1999 pp. 499, 501); listed from Thailand (Takaoka & Choochote 2004 p. 189).
Diagnostic notesSimulium multistriatum species-group (Takaoka et al. 2019). Described in Takaoka & Kuvangkadilok (1999). | Simulium s.str. (multistriatum species-group); described by Takaoka & Boonkemtong in Takaoka & Kuvangkadilok (1999 pp. 497-501). Female (body 2.2 mm, wing 2.2 mm): 5-vittate scutum, simple claws, sensory vesicle 0.4x length of 3rd palpal segment, basal section of radius bare except 1-4 hairs near apex, mid femur blackish brown with basal 1/5-1/4 yellow (vs almost entirely dark in S. chainarongi), sternite 8 with 23-34 stout hairs/side, single globular spermatheca with 2 short empty accessory ducts (ca. 0.3x main duct). Male (2.6 mm, wing 2.0 mm): upper eye 17 vertical columns x 18 horizontal rows; hind basitarsus entirely light brown, enlarged (3.6x as long as wide); style 3.4x greatest width with spinose horn-like basal protuberance; ventral plate subquadrate with ca. 10 posterior teeth in 2 irregular rows (differs from S. fenestratum by toothed basal protuberance of style). Pupa (2.8 mm): 8 short paired gill filaments (1.2-0.8 mm, nearly 170 deg angle), dorsalmost ca. 1.4x as thick as ventralmost (vs 2.0x in S. chainarongi); shoe-shaped cocoon (vs fenestrate wall-pocket cocoon of most multistriatum-group species). Mature larva unknown (Takaoka & Kuvangkadilok 1999 pp. 497-501; Takaoka & Choochote 2004 pp. 194-196).
SourcesTakaoka et al. 2019, Takaoka & Kuvangkadilok 1999, Takaoka & Choochote 2004
