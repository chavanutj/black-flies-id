Simulium thailandicum Takaoka & Suzuki, 1984

ClassificationGenus Simulium, subgenus Simulium
Statusvalid
DistributionChina, Thailand, Vietnam (Takaoka et al. 2019) | Thailand (Takaoka & Choochote 2004). | Thailand (Srisuka et al. 2017)
ThailandThailand (Takaoka et al. 2019, p. 57) | Listed from Thailand (Takaoka & Choochote 2004 p. 190). | Thailand: 228 mature larvae (1.2% of total), 12.1% stream occurrence (Srisuka et al. 2017)
Diagnostic notesSimulium striatum species-group (Takaoka et al. 2019). Female and larva unknown (Takaoka et al. 2019, p. 75). | Simulium s.str. (striatum species-group). Female and mature larva unknown in Takaoka & Choochote (2004 pp. 194, 197). Male not separated from S. chiangmaiense and S. nakhonense (couplet 15b, p. 195). Pupa: 10 gill filaments arranged 2+2+2+2+2 with 4-5 dorsal filaments thicker, head integument with angular tubercles (round in S. nakhonense) (Takaoka & Choochote 2004 p. 196).
SourcesTakaoka et al. 2019, Takaoka & Choochote 2004, Srisuka et al. 2017
