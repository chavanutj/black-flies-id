Simulium feuerborni Edwards, 1934

ClassificationGenus Simulium, subgenus Nevermannia
Statusvalid
Other names in keysSimulium sp. B
DistributionIndonesia (Bali, Java, Sumatra), Thailand (Takaoka et al. 2019) | Malaysia/Indonesia and Thailand (Kuvangkadilok & Takaoka 2000; Takaoka & Choochote 2004). | Thailand (Srisuka et al. 2017; Putt et al. 2023)
ThailandThailand (Takaoka et al. 2019, p. 56) | Reported from Thailand as S. sp. B (Takaoka & Suzuki 1984) and assigned to S. feuerborni (Kuvangkadilok & Takaoka 2000 p. 167); listed from Thailand (Takaoka & Choochote 2004 p. 189). | Thailand: 142 mature larvae (0.7% of total), 8.6% stream occurrence (as S. feuerborni complex) (Srisuka et al. 2017); COI cytoform A reference sequences JX484806, JX484807, JX484823, JX484824 (Putt et al. 2023)
Diagnostic notesSimulium feuerborni species-group. Species complex (Pramual et al. 2015; Ya'cob et al. 2017b; cited in Takaoka et al. 2019). Recorded as Simulium sp. B (pupa) in Takaoka & Suzuki (1984). | Nevermannia (feuerborni species-group). Female: antenna mostly dark, scutum reddish brown with 3 dark longitudinal vittae. Male: inflated hind basitarsus, scutum whitish pruinose with 3 dark vittae, paramere with 3 or 4 hooks. Pupa: 6 gill filaments extending forwards close together, 2 ventral filaments with rather long stalk. Larva: abdomen with distinct reddish-brown dorsal markings (Takaoka & Choochote 2004 p. 193). | S. feuerborni species-group; species complex. Cytoform A COI intraspecific p-distance 0.25-0.50%, but PTP_ML, ASAP and GMYC merge it with Vietnamese S. bachmaense, S. maeaiense, S. phami and S. pumatense into a single COI OTU (Putt et al. 2023).
SourcesTakaoka et al. 2019, Kuvangkadilok & Takaoka 2000, Takaoka & Choochote 2004, Srisuka et al. 2017, Putt et al. 2023

The chain of characters leading to this taxon in each key (a ready-made diagnosis).

Thailand: Simulium (Nevermannia) females (Takaoka et al. 2019) – couplet 5a
  1. 1b Antenna almost all darkened or mostly so except few basal segments pale. Hind tibia brownish
  2. 2a Scutum with 3 dark longitudinal vittae
  3. 3b Genital fork without strongly sclerotized horizontal bar at base of anterodorsal projection of each arm
  4. 4b Mandible with 20-23 inner teeth
  5. 5a Frons : head ratio 1 : 5.4 ⚠
Thailand: Simulium (Nevermannia) males (Takaoka et al. 2019) – couplet 6a
  1. 1b Antenna almost all darkened. Hind basitarsus inflated, and nearly as wide as hind tibia
  2. 2a Paramere with 3-7 hooks ⚠
  3. 3b Upper-eye facets in 14-19 vertical columns
  4. 4b Upper-eye facets in 14-17 vertical columns
  5. 5b Upper-eye facets in 14-16 vertical columns. Paramere with 3-8 hooks ⚠
  6. 6a Upper-eye facets in 16 vertical columns. Paramere with 3 or 4 hooks ⚠
Thailand: Simulium (Nevermannia) pupae (Takaoka et al. 2019) – couplet 6a
  1. 1b Gill with 6 filaments
  2. 3b All filaments extending forwards close together
  3. 4a Cocoon with anterodorsal projection
  4. 5b Gill filaments arranged otherwise, not lying horizontally
  5. 6a Gill filaments arranged as 4+2 from dorsal to ventral ⚠

  3. 3a Ventral surface of head capsule darkened medially ⚠
