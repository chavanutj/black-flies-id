Simulium chaudinhense Takaoka & Sofian-Azirun, 2017

ClassificationGenus Simulium, subgenus Gomphostilbia
Statusvalid
DistributionVietnam: Nghe An, Quy Chau, 80 m (paratypes OQ117766-OQ117769, BZF OQ427832-OQ427835) (Putt et al. 2023)
Diagnostic notesS. asakoae species-group. Non-monophyletic in COI with 0.25% minimum interspecific distance to S. asakoae s.str. (intraspecific 0-0.99%) (Putt et al. 2023). Shares identical 3'-end bases with S. asakoae at the multiplex PCR primer sites of Aupalee et al. (2026), which may cause cross-reaction in Vietnam.
SourcesPutt et al. 2023, Aupalee et al. 2026
