Simulium weji Takaoka, 2001

ClassificationGenus Simulium, subgenus Simulium
Statusvalid
DistributionThailand (Takaoka et al. 2019) | Thailand (Takaoka & Choochote 2004). | Thailand (Adler et al. 2016; Srisuka et al. 2017)
ThailandThailand (Takaoka et al. 2019, p. 57) | Listed from Thailand (Takaoka & Choochote 2004 p. 190). | Thailand: 609 mature larvae (3.1% of total), 6.9% stream occurrence; 2nd most predominant in Western region (12%, n=406); associated with calcareous waterfalls (Srisuka et al. 2017)
Diagnostic notesSimulium tuberosum species-group, S. rufibasis subgroup (Takaoka et al. 2019). | Simulium s.str. (tuberosum species-group). Female: unvittate scutum, ventral pair of long clustered hairs on abdominal segment 7, mid tibia almost light brown with yellowish base. Male: enlarged wedge-shaped hind basitarsus pale on basal 3/5 or slightly less, dorsolateral silvery spots on abdominal segments 2, 5, 6 and 7. Pupa: 6 almost sessile gill filaments, no terminal hooks, no pit-like organ at gill base. Larva: cephalic apotome without posteromedian dark area, rectal organ 14-16 lobules/lobe, posterior circlet ca. 86 rows of up to 17 hooklets (Takaoka & Choochote 2004 pp. 194-197). | S. tuberosum species-group; shows low cytogenetic diversity (suggesting 1 species) but high molecular diversity partitioning into possible cryptic species (cited in Adler et al. 2016 p. 23).
SourcesTakaoka et al. 2019, Takaoka & Choochote 2004, Adler et al. 2016, Srisuka et al. 2017
