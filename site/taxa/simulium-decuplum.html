Simulium decuplum Takaoka & Davies, 1995

ClassificationGenus Simulium, subgenus Gomphostilbia
Statusvalid
DistributionPeninsular Malaysia; reported from Thailand (Kuvangkadilok & Takaoka 2000; Takaoka & Choochote 2004), though Thai populations were later split into five new species (Takaoka et al. 2018d, 2019). | India (Mukherjee et al. 2022) | Thailand (Srisuka et al. 2017)
ThailandListed from Thailand (Takaoka & Choochote 2004 p. 189); collected with S. grossifilum in southern Thailand (Kuvangkadilok & Takaoka 2000 p. 174). Thai populations were subsequently split into five new species in the S. decuplum subgroup (Takaoka et al. 2018d; Takaoka et al. 2019). | Thailand: 1,175 mature larvae (6.0% of total), 4th most frequent taxon (60.3%, 35/58 sites); predominant in Northern (6.4%), Central (6.3%, 80% sites), Northeastern (90% sites) and Eastern (18.5%, 100% sites) regions; associated with low elevation (Srisuka et al. 2017)
Diagnostic notesGomphostilbia (batoense species-group, decuplum subgroup). Female: 11-segmented antenna, dark subbasal spot on hind tibia, enlarged sensory vesicle (0.5-0.6x 3rd palpal segment), arms of genital fork with short anteriorly directed projection. Male: slender hind basitarsus, shiny patches on abdominal segments 2, 6 and 7, upper eye with 12-13 vertical columns and 15 horizontal rows of large facets. Pupa: 10 gill filaments. Larva: thoracic cuticle dorsally with 7-11-branched minute dark spinules, pharate pupal gill with 10 filaments (Takaoka & Choochote 2004 pp. 191-192).
SourcesKuvangkadilok & Takaoka 2000, Takaoka & Choochote 2004, Mukherjee et al. 2022, Srisuka et al. 2017

Thailand (2004): Simulium (Gomphostilbia) females (Takaoka & Choochote 2004) – couplet 7a
  1. 1b Antenna composed of 11 segments
  2. 3b Mid and hind femora alomst dark except base pale ⚠

  4. 5a Hind tibia with a dark subbasal spot
  5. 6b Sensory vesicle enlarged, 0.5 or 0.6 times as long as 3rd maxillary palpal segment
  6. 7a Arms of genital fork with a short projection directed anteriorly ⚠
Thailand (2004): Simulium (Gomphostilbia) males (Takaoka & Choochote 2004) – couplet 9a
  1. 1b Antenna composed of 11 segments
  2. 3b Hind basitarsus slender, much thinner than hind tibia
  3. 6b Mid and hind femora almost dark except base pale
  4. 7a Hind tibia almost dark except base pale
  5. 8b Abdominal segments 2, 6 and 7 each with a dorsolateral pair of shiny whitish-pruinose patches
  6. 9a Upper eye with 12 or 13 vertical columns and 15 horizontal rows of large facets ⚠
