Simulium chiangdaoense Takaoka & Srisuka, 2009

ClassificationGenus Simulium, subgenus Gomphostilbia
Statusvalid
DistributionThailand (Takaoka et al. 2019) | Thailand (Chiang Mai) (Takaoka et al. 2020) | Thailand (Srisuka et al. 2017; Aupalee et al. 2026)
ThailandThailand (Takaoka et al. 2019, p. 55) | Pha Tea Do mountain, Mae Chaem, Chiang Mai Province, 901 m, 19°28'18.3"N 100°28'00.8"E, 27-VI-2018 (Takaoka et al. 2020, pp. 140-141) | Thailand: 515 mature larvae (2.7% of total), 10.3% stream occurrence; 5th most predominant in Northern region (5.3%, n=298) and strongly associated with high elevation (Srisuka et al. 2017) | Used in Thailand multiplex PCR validation (n=4) (Aupalee et al. 2026)
Diagnostic notesSimulium asakoae species-group (Takaoka et al. 2019). Larva unknown (Takaoka et al. 2019, p. 64). | S. asakoae species-group (COI subgroup III). Female: body length 2.4-2.6 mm, mandible with outer teeth, labrum 0.61-0.68x clypeus, claw tooth 0.47x claw. Male: brown upper-eye facets in 12-14 vertical columns and 15-16 horizontal rows, ventral plate rounded ventrally. Pupa: cocoon with elongate anterodorsal projection (>=2.0 mm), gill with 8 filaments. Larva (newly keyed in Takaoka et al. 2020, Fig. 25R): abdominal segments 1-3 light green, segment 4 light ochreous, pharate pupal gill with 8 filaments (Takaoka et al. 2020). | S. asakoae species-group; yields only 709 bp common band (non-S. asakoae) in multiplex PCR (Aupalee et al. 2026).
SourcesTakaoka et al. 2019, Takaoka et al. 2020, Srisuka et al. 2017, Aupalee et al. 2026
