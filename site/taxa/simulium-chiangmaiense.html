Simulium chiangmaiense Takaoka & Suzuki, 1984

ClassificationGenus Simulium, subgenus Simulium
Statusvalid
DistributionMyanmar, Thailand, Vietnam (Takaoka et al. 2019) | Thailand (Takaoka & Choochote 2004). | Thailand (Srisuka et al. 2017)
ThailandThailand (Takaoka et al. 2019, p. 57) | Listed from Thailand (Takaoka & Choochote 2004 p. 190). | Thailand: 79 mature larvae (0.4% of total), 3.5% stream occurrence (Srisuka et al. 2017)
Diagnostic notesSimulium striatum species-group (Takaoka et al. 2019). Reinstated as a valid species (previously synonymized with S. nakhonense by Pangjanda & Pramual 2017) based on nuclear BZF gene sequence differences (Takaoka et al. 2018c) and arrangement and size of pupal gill filaments (Takaoka et al. 2018h) (Takaoka et al. 2019, p. 54). | Simulium s.str. (striatum species-group). Female and male not separated from S. nakhonense (and S. quinquestriatum in female / S. thailandicum in male) in Takaoka & Choochote (2004 pp. 193, 195). Pupa: 8 gill filaments with 2 ventralmost paired filaments much thinner than others, shoe-shaped cocoon roughly woven anteriorly with open spaces. Larva: abdominal segments 1-8 each with a pair of dorsal protuberances, pharate pupal gill with 8 filaments (Takaoka & Choochote 2004 pp. 196). Reinstated as valid in Takaoka et al. (2019) after synonymization with S. nakhonense by Pangjanda & Pramual (2017).
SourcesTakaoka et al. 2019, Takaoka & Choochote 2004, Srisuka et al. 2017
