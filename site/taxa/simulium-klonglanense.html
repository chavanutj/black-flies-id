Simulium klonglanense Takaoka, Srisuka & Saeung, 2020

ClassificationGenus Simulium, subgenus Gomphostilbia
Statusvalid
DistributionThailand (Kamphaeng Phet) (Takaoka et al. 2020)
ThailandType locality: Klong Nam Lai, Klong Lan District, Kamphaeng Phet Province, 196 m, 16°12'28.3"N 99°15'47.8"E, 27-VI-2013 (Takaoka et al. 2020, p. 126)
Diagnostic notesS. asakoae species-group (COI subgroup I, sister to S. namdanense at 1.54% divergence). Female: elongate sensory vesicle 0.65-0.68x 3rd palp segment (vs 0.50-0.54x in S. lurauense), mandible with 4 distinct outer teeth, wing length 1.6 mm (vs 2.0 mm in S. namdanense). Male: upper-eye facets in 13-14 vertical columns and 14-15 horizontal rows (vs 15-16 x 16-17 in S. namdanense), hind basitarsus 0.9x hind tibia and femur (vs 0.8x tibia and 0.7-0.8x femur in S. lurauense), ventral plate nearly straight medially in caudal view. Pupa: cocoon without anterodorsal projection, small conical non-crenulated terminal hooks. Larva: postgenal cleft 3.7-4.0x postgenal bridge (vs 2.5x in S. namdanense), abdominal segments 1-4 grey (Takaoka et al. 2020, pp. 126-130).
SourcesTakaoka et al. 2020

Thailand: Simulium asakoae species-group (females) (Takaoka et al. 2020) – couplet 3a
  1. 1b Hair tuft of base of radial vein yellow
  2. 2a Sensory vesicle elongate, more than 0.5 times length of third palpal segment (Fig. 22A)
  3. 3a Wing length 1.6 mm
Thailand: Simulium asakoae species-group (males) (Takaoka et al. 2020) – couplet 10a
  1. 1b Hair tuft of base of radial vein yellow
  2. 2b Upper-eye (large) facets medium to dark brown
  3. 4b Antenna brownish except scape, pedicel and base of first flagellomere yellowish
  4. 5a Hind basitarsus 0.9 time as wide as hind femur (Fig. 7A)
  5. 6b Ventral plate with ventral margin nearly straight medially when viewed caudally (Fig. 20F)
  6. 9b Upper-eye (large) facets in 14–16 vertical columns
  7. 10a Upper-eye (large) facets in 14 vertical columns
Thailand: Simulium asakoae species-group (pupae) (Takaoka et al. 2020) – couplet 9a
  1. 1b Cocoon with or without anterodorsal bulge or short anterodorsal projection (up to ca. 1.0 mm long) extended at most up to anteroventral tips of cocoon
  2. 3b Dorsum of abdominal segments 1 and 2 unpigmented or light yellowish
  3. 5b Dorsum of abdominal segment 1 without minute tubercles
  4. 7b Cocoon without anterodorsal projection
  5. 9a Terminal hooks conical, with outer margin as long as or slightly longer than inner margin and not crenulated (Figs 22K, 23K)
Thailand: Simulium asakoae species-group (mature larvae) (Takaoka et al. 2020) – couplet 8b
  1. 1a Abdominal segments 1–4 unicolorous
  2. 2b Abdominal segments 1–4 greyish or greenish grey or greenish
  3. 5b Pharate pupal gill with medium-long common basal stalk
  4. 6a Postgenal cleft 1.5–4.0 times length of postgenal bridge
  5. 7b Head spots indistinct or faintly positive
  6. 8b Postgenal cleft 3.7–4.0 times as long as postgenal bridge (Fig. 22M)
