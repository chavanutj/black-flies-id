Simulium kotsuboyui Takaoka, 2025

ClassificationGenus Simulium, subgenus Simulium
Statusvalid
DistributionIndonesia: West Sumatra (Airmancur, Bukittinggi, 560 m [type locality]; Lubukbargalung to Lubuksulasih, 980 m; Pasaman, 340 m) (Takaoka 2025)
Diagnostic notesS. iridescens species-group; formerly misidentified as S. iridescens in Sumatra (Takaoka et al. 2000). Female (n=6, Takaoka 2025 pp. 394-399): body 2.3-2.5 mm; frontal ratio 1.2:1.0:1.1; sensory vesicle 0.22-0.29x 3rd palpal segment; cibarium with 56-61 round processes; scutum coppery-haired; fore basitarsus dilated (3.6-3.8x wide); mid and hind femora entirely darkened (vs base yellow in S. iridescens); claw with small subbasal tooth; subcosta and basal radial vein bare; sternite 8 with 10-13 stout hairs/side; spermatheca globular (1.1x wide). Male (n=7): body 2.4-2.7 mm; upper-eye large facets in 17-18 columns x 17-18 rows (vs 13-14 columns in S. iridescens); hind basitarsus slightly narrower than hind tibia and femur (vs slightly wider in S. iridescens); style ventrolateral length/width ratio 2.2 (vs 2.5 in S. solokense). Pupa (n=13): head with frons nearly bare but face covered with tubercles (vs both bare in S. solokense; tubercles on frons in S. iridescens); thorax entirely covered with tubercles (vs narrowly bare anteriorly in S. solokense); gill 6 filaments (2+2+2) with regular well-defined annular ridges having relatively long interridge distances (vs short in S. iridescens, irregular/weak in S. solokense); cocoon wall pocket-shaped (2.8-3.0 x 1.0-1.3 mm). Larva unknown.
