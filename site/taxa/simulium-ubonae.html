Simulium ubonae

ClassificationGenus Simulium, subgenus Simulium
Statusvalid
DistributionThailand (Putt et al. 2023)
ThailandThailand (COI reference sequences MK460434, MK460436, MK460437, MK460440) (Putt et al. 2023)
Diagnostic notesS. multistriatum species-group. COI intraspecific p-distance 0.00%; shares 0.00-0.25% COI distance with S. lacduongense and S. laui; separated morphologically by bare basal radial vein in female (vs haired in S. lacduongense), slightly concave inner margins of female ovipositor valves (vs divergent in S. laui) and male upper-eye facets (Putt et al. 2023 p. 16).
SourcesPutt et al. 2023
