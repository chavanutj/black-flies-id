Simulium choochotei Takaoka, 2002

ClassificationGenus Simulium, subgenus Simulium
Statusvalid
DistributionThailand (Takaoka et al. 2019) | Thailand (Takaoka & Choochote 2004).
ThailandThailand (Takaoka et al. 2019, p. 56) | Listed from Thailand (Takaoka & Choochote 2004 p. 189).
Diagnostic notesSimulium griseifrons species-group (Takaoka et al. 2019). Described in Takaoka & Choochote (2002). | Simulium s.str. (griseifrons species-group). Female: simple claw, vittate scutum, basal section of radial vein fully haired, hind basitarsus whitish on basal 1/2, triangular ovipositor valve, cibarium with round short medial projection. Male: basal portion of radial vein fully haired, shiny whitish-grey spots on abdominal segments 2, 5, 6 and 7, style nearly uniform width in ventrolateral view. Pupa: 6 gill filaments, loosely woven shoe/boot-shaped cocoon with many small open spaces. Larva: abdomen widest on segment 8, cephalic apotome light to medium brown, posterior circlet with ca. 210 rows of hooklets (Takaoka & Choochote 2004 pp. 193-196).
SourcesTakaoka et al. 2019, Takaoka & Choochote 2004
