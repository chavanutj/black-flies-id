Simulium hongthaii Takaoka, Sofian-Azirun & Ya'cob, 2014

ClassificationGenus Simulium, subgenus Gomphostilbia
Statusvalid
DistributionVietnam: Vinh Phuc (Tam Dao 997 m, paratype), Thua Thien Hue (Hue; Bach Ma 432 m), Lao Cai (Sa Pa 1,019 m) (Putt et al. 2023)
Diagnostic notesS. asakoae species-group. Non-monophyletic in COI with high intraspecific divergence (3.47-8.66%, mean 6.06%) across 4 Vietnamese localities (suggesting a species complex) and 0.00% min interspecific distance to S. sanchayense; clusters with S. asakoae in COI trees and shares 3'-end primer bases with S. asakoae in multiplex PCR (Putt et al. 2023; Aupalee et al. 2024, 2026).
SourcesPutt et al. 2023, Aupalee et al. 2024, Aupalee et al. 2026
