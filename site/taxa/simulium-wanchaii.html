Simulium wanchaii Takaoka & Choochote, 2006

ClassificationGenus Simulium, subgenus Asiosimulium
Statusvalid
DistributionThailand (Takaoka et al. 2019) | Thailand (Srisuka et al. 2024) | Thailand (Srisuka et al. 2017)
ThailandThailand (Takaoka et al. 2019, p. 54) | Doi Suthep-Pui NP (Chiang Mai) and Phu Langka (Phayao), northern Thailand (Srisuka et al. 2024, p. 2, Fig. 1) | Thailand: 34 mature larvae (0.2% of total), 3.5% stream occurrence; restricted to sun-exposed lowland slow-flowing rock-bed streams during rainy season (Srisuka et al. 2017)
ImportanceAdult females caught using human attractants in Doi Suthep-Pui NP, Chiang Mai (Takaoka & Choochote 2006) and Phu Langka, Phayao (Srisuka et al. 2024, p. 2).
Diagnostic notesSubgenus Asiosimulium (Takaoka et al. 2019). | Subgenus Asiosimulium (COI Clade II; high intraspecific COI divergence up to 4.39% suggests possible cryptic diversity). Female: cercus normal (1.2x basal width), abdominal segment 5 shiny dorsally, spermatheca globular, genital fork without incision between arms. Male: hind basitarsus 0.90-1.00x hind tibia, abdominal segments 5-8 without shiny patches, upper-eye (large) facets in 18-19 vertical columns. Pupa: head and thorax moderately tuberculate, gill with 19 filaments. Larva: postgenal cleft without pigmented sheath of subesophageal ganglion, labral fan 38-40 primary rays, mandible with supernumerary serrations (Srisuka et al. 2024, pp. 13-16).
SourcesTakaoka et al. 2019, Srisuka et al. 2024, Srisuka et al. 2017
