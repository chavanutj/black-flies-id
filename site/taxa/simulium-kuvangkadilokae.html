Simulium kuvangkadilokae Pramual & Tangkawanit, 2008

ClassificationGenus Simulium, subgenus Gomphostilbia
Statusvalid
DistributionThailand (Takaoka et al. 2019) | Thailand (Srisuka et al. 2022) | Thailand (Srisuka et al. 2017)
ThailandThailand (Takaoka et al. 2019, p. 55) | Thailand (Srisuka et al. 2022, pp. 2, 4) | Thailand: 98 mature larvae (0.5% of total), 3.5% stream occurrence; northeast Thailand, dominant in rainy season (Srisuka et al. 2017)
Diagnostic notesSimulium varicorne species-group, S. chumpornense subgroup (Takaoka et al. 2019). | S. varicorne species-group, S. chumpornense subgroup. Closest relative of S. khelangense (COI distance 1.23-2.81%). Female: antenna with 8 flagellomeres, pleural membrane bare, subcosta bare, hind tibia darkened on apical half, fore basitarsus 5.56x greatest width (5.54-5.68 in key), head 3.78-4.05x greatest width of frons (vs 4.30-4.54x in S. piroonae and 4.21-4.66x in S. khelangense), labrum 0.57-0.59x clypeus (Srisuka et al. 2022, pp. 4, 9).
SourcesTakaoka et al. 2019, Srisuka et al. 2022, Srisuka et al. 2017

  1. 1b Antenna with 8 or 9 flagellomeres
  2. 2a Antenna with 8 flagellomeres
  3. 3b Ventral plate nearly flat
  4. 4b Upper-eye facets in 11 or 12 vertical columns and 12 or 13 horizontal rows
Thailand: Simulium (Gomphostilbia) pupae (Takaoka et al. 2019) – couplet 2a
  1. 1a Gill of inflated structure
  2. 2a Gill with 12 slender filaments
Thailand: Simulium (Gomphostilbia) mature larvae (Takaoka et al. 2019) – couplet 2a
  1. 1a Abdomen with protuberances dorsally
  2. 2a Abdominal segments 1 and 2 each with 4 pairs of protuberances
