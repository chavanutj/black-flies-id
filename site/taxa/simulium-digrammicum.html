Simulium digrammicum Edwards, 1928

ClassificationGenus Simulium, subgenus Simulium
Statusvalid
DistributionPeninsular Malaysia, Thailand (Takaoka et al. 2019) | Thailand (Takaoka & Choochote 2004).
ThailandThailand (Takaoka et al. 2019, pp. 53, 56) | Listed from Thailand (Takaoka & Choochote 2004 p. 189; male, pupa and larva described from Thailand in Takaoka & Choochote 2002 ref. 8 p. 197).
Diagnostic notesSimulium griseifrons species-group (Takaoka et al. 2019). One of the first two black fly species recorded from Thailand (Edwards 1928, cited in Takaoka et al. 2019, p. 53). | Simulium s.str. (griseifrons species-group). Female: simple claw, vittate scutum, basal section of radial vein fully haired, triangular ovipositor valve, cibarium with narrow long medial projection. Male: basal radial vein haired, fore basitarsus moderate hair crest, spindle-shaped hind basitarsus, ventral plate gradually narrowed posteriorly. Pupa: 6 gill filaments, tightly woven shoe-shaped cocoon with anterolateral flap and small window on each side, terminal hooks present. Larva: body 5.0-5.5 mm, elongate spot beside postgenal cleft negative, rectal organ 14-18 lobules/lobe (Takaoka & Choochote 2004 pp. 193-197).
SourcesTakaoka et al. 2019, Takaoka & Choochote 2004
