How it keys out

The chain of characters leading to this taxon in each key (a ready-made diagnosis).

Thailand: Simulium (Simulium) females (Takaoka et al. 2019) – couplet 28a
  1. 1b Claw without any tooth
  2. 15a Scutum with 5 distinct longitudinal vittae
  3. 16b Scutum with 1 median, 2 submedian and 2 sublateral dark vittae when illuminated anteriorly and viewed dorsally
  4. 27b Mid femur with darkened area. Spermatheca 1 in number
  5. 28a Hind femur yellowish almost entirely
Thailand: Simulium (Simulium) males (Takaoka et al. 2019) – couplet 43a
  1. 1b Basal section of radius bare or with few hairs apically
  2. 7b Scutum otherwise
  3. 9b Mid femur and tibia otherwise
  4. 11b Mid tibia whitish or yellowish (at least on posterior surface) on basal 2/5 or more and dark brown on rest
  5. 29b Hind basitarsus entirely light to dark brown, or so with basal 2/5-1/3 whitish yellow or dark yellow. Body of ventral plate longer than wide ⚠
  6. 33b Hind basitarsus brownish black with basal 1/3-2/5 whitish yellow or dark yellow
  7. 36b Upper-eye facets in 15-18 vertical columns and 16-19 horizontal rows
  8. 40b Upper-eye facets in 17 or 18 vertical columns and 17-19 horizontal rows. Ventral plate with teeth irregularly situated on posterior surface
  9. 41b Sensory vesicle 0.2 times as long as third palpomere of maxillary palp
  10. 42b Basal projection of style with several spines
  11. 43a Mid tibia whitish except apical cap darkened
Thailand: Simulium (Simulium) pupae (Takaoka et al. 2019) – couplet 38a
  1. 1b Gill with 6-10 fiaments ⚠
  2. 4b Gill with 8-10 filaments
  3. 35a Gill with 8 filaments
  4. 36a Cocoon wall-pocket shaped
  5. 37a Cocoon without lateral windows
  6. 38a Gill filaments widely divergent, angle between stalks of dorsal and ventral pairs 180 degrees
