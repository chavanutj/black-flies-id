Simulium vanluni Ya'cob, Takaoka & Sofian-Azirun, 2017

ClassificationGenus Simulium, subgenus Simulium
Statusvalid
DistributionPeninsular Malaysia, Thailand (Takaoka et al. 2019)
ThailandThailand (Takaoka et al. 2019, p. 57)
Diagnostic notesSimulium nobile species-group (Takaoka et al. 2019). Previously treated in Thailand as S. nobile (not sensu De Meijere, 1911). Described in Ya'cob et al. (2017a).
SourcesTakaoka et al. 2019

Thailand: Simulium (Simulium) females (Takaoka et al. 2019) – couplet 11a
  1. 1a Claw with small subbasal tooth
  2. 2b Basal section of radius bare. Fore basitarsus with moderate dorsal hair crest
  3. 5b Mid and hind femora mostly brownish
  4. 8b Scutum moderately covered with brassy hairs
  5. 11a Antenna yellow or tawny with 2 apical flagellomeres blackish
Thailand: Simulium (Simulium) males (Takaoka et al. 2019) – couplet 8a
  1. 1b Basal section of radius bare or with few hairs apically
  2. 7a Scutum broadly silvery pruinose with transverse, inverted-V shaped, black band
  3. 8a Ventral plate with narrow body, parallel-sided when viewed ventrally
Thailand: Simulium (Simulium) pupae (Takaoka et al. 2019) – couplet 33b
  1. 1b Gill with 6-10 fiaments ⚠
  2. 4a Gill with 6 filaments
  3. 5b Gill with 6 thread-like filaments (with basal portions somewhat inflated or not) without minute spines
  4. 7b Cocoon shoe shaped or boot shaped
  5. 27b Cocoon tightly woven
  6. 29b Cocoon without such flap and window
  7. 30b Gill filaments subequal in length and thickness to one another. Facial and frontal trichomes unbranched
  8. 32a Abdominal segment 8 with spine-combs dorsally
  9. 33b Frons sparsely or moderately covered with tubercles
