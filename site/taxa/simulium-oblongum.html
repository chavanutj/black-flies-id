Simulium oblongum Takaoka & Choochote, 2005

ClassificationGenus Simulium, subgenus Asiosimulium
Statusvalid
DistributionThailand (Takaoka et al. 2019) | Thailand (Takaoka & Choochote 2005). | Thailand (Srisuka et al. 2024) | Thailand (Srisuka et al. 2017)
ThailandThailand (Takaoka et al. 2019, p. 54) | Type locality: Phu Hung, Muang District, Mukdahan Province ('Muk Da Han Province' in text), NE Thailand (20.X.2004, altitude 270 m, slow-flowing sun-exposed lowland rocky stream 0.8 m wide, 0.03 m deep, 29 C, on trailing grasses and rocks with S. aureohirtum; holotype female, paratypes 2 females, 1 male, 2 pupal exuviae, 2 mature larvae) (Takaoka & Choochote 2005 pp. 39-40). | Thailand; lowland species found at <1,000 m (Srisuka et al. 2024, pp. 2, 15, Fig. 1) | Thailand: 487 mature larvae (2.5% of total), 17.2% stream occurrence; 2nd most predominant in Northeastern region (13.4%, n=353); widespread in northeastern, central and eastern regions in sun-exposed slow-flowing lowland rock-bed streams during rainy season (Srisuka et al. 2017)
Diagnostic notesSubgenus Asiosimulium (Takaoka et al. 2019). | Type species of subgenus Asiosimulium Takaoka & Choochote, 2005 (pp. 33-41). Adult: katepisternum and pleural membrane bare, basal portion of radius fully haired, calcipala and pedisulcus well developed. Female (body 3.0-3.2 mm, wing 2.3 mm): antenna 2+9 segments; sensory vesicle ellipsoidal, 0.36x 3rd palpal segment; lacinia 10-11 inner and 12-14 outer teeth; mandible 22-24 inner and 8-10 outer teeth; cibarium uniquely with ca. 100-120 spinous processes arranged on anterior surface; claw with large basal tooth (0.55x claw); tergites 5-8 shiny; genital fork arms wide with anterodorsal apodemes; cercus uniquely elongate (about twice as long as basal width in lateral view); spermatheca ovoid with reticulate pattern and internal setae. Male (body 2.7 mm, wing 1.9 mm): upper eye 19 vertical columns x 19 horizontal rows; style short (0.67x coxite) with apical spine; ventral plate lamellate, transverse, much shorter than wide, strongly sclerotized anteriorly without median notch; paramere completely lacking parameral hooks; dorsal plate subrectangular. Pupa (3.5-4.0 mm): gill dendroid with 31-33 slender filaments (0.4-2.0 mm) in 5-6 groups; abdomen dorsally lacking spine-combs on segments 5-9; terminal hooks cone-shaped; segment 9 with 1-2 sclerotized spines laterally; wall-pocket cocoon 3.2-3.8 x 2.0-2.2 mm without anterodorsal projection. Mature larva (6.2-6.8 mm, greyish): antenna subequal in length to stem of labral fan (proportions 1.0:1.3:1.0); labral fan 43-45 rays; hypostomium 9 apical teeth with weakly serrated lateral margins and 7-8 bristles/side; deep postgenal cleft leaving narrow bridge; rectal organ compound (7-11 lobules/lobe); ventral papillae very small; posterior circlet ca. 76 rows of up to 15 hooklets. Distinguished from related subgenera Boreosimulium, Inseliellum and Nevermannia by the spinose female cibarium, elongate female cercus, hookless male paramere, 31-33-filament dendroid pupal gill without dorsal spine-combs, and short larval antenna (Takaoka & Choochote 2005 pp. 33-41). | Subgenus Asiosimulium (COI Clade I). Female: cercus very elongated (~2x basal width), mandible with 22-24 inner and 8-10 outer teeth, lowland (<1,000 m) (vs 20 inner, 12-13 outer teeth and >1,100 m in S. phurueaense). Male: hind basitarsus 0.80x hind tibia, sensory vesicle <0.30x 3rd palp segment, abdominal segments 5-8 without shiny dorsolateral patches. Pupa: head and thorax moderately tuberculate, gill 31-33 filaments (couplet 5a with S. phurueaense). Larva: postgenal cleft without pigmented sheath of subesophageal ganglion, labral fan 43-45 primary rays, mandible without supernumerary serration (Srisuka et al. 2024, pp. 14-16).
SourcesTakaoka et al. 2019, Takaoka & Choochote 2005, Srisuka et al. 2024, Srisuka et al. 2017
