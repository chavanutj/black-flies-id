Simulium rampae Takaoka, Srisuka & Saeung, 2019

ClassificationGenus Simulium, subgenus Gomphostilbia
Statusvalid
DistributionThailand (Takaoka et al. 2020) | Thailand (Putt et al. 2023)
ThailandThailand (Srisuka et al. 2019, cited in Takaoka et al. 2020, pp. 141-147) | Thailand (COI reference sequences LC472507-LC472508) (Putt et al. 2023)
Diagnostic notesS. asakoae species-group (COI subgroup III, shares close COI similarity with S. udomi). Female unknown. Male: brown upper-eye facets in 16-17 vertical columns, subcosta with 7-12 hairs, wing 2.2-2.5 mm, ventral plate rounded ventrally in caudal view (vs nearly straight in S. junkumae). Pupa: gill arranged 3+2+(2+1) or 2+2+(2+1) with long common basal stalk 1.3-1.5x interspiracular trunk. Larva: abdominal segments 1-4 greyish/greenish grey, pharate pupal gill with elongate common basal stalk (Takaoka et al. 2020, pp. 142-147, 150). | S. asakoae species-group; COI intraspecific p-distance 0.00% (Putt et al. 2023).
SourcesTakaoka et al. 2020, Putt et al. 2023

Thailand: Simulium asakoae species-group (males) (Takaoka et al. 2020) – couplet 18b
  1. 1b Hair tuft of base of radial vein yellow
  2. 2b Upper-eye (large) facets medium to dark brown
  3. 4b Antenna brownish except scape, pedicel and base of first flagellomere yellowish
  4. 5b Hind basitarsus 1.0–1.3 times as wide as hind femur (Fig. 4C)
  5. 11b Upper-eye (large) facets in 11 or more vertical columns
  6. 12b Upper-eye (large) facets in 12–17 vertical columns
  7. 16a Upper-eye (large) facets in 16 or 17 vertical columns
  8. 17b Subcosta with 7–12 hairs; wing length 2.2–2.5 mm
  9. 18b Ventral plate with ventral margin rounded when viewed caudally
Thailand: Simulium asakoae species-group (pupae) (Takaoka et al. 2020) – couplet 11a
  1. 1b Cocoon with or without anterodorsal bulge or short anterodorsal projection (up to ca. 1.0 mm long) extended at most up to anteroventral tips of cocoon
  2. 3b Dorsum of abdominal segments 1 and 2 unpigmented or light yellowish
  3. 5b Dorsum of abdominal segment 1 without minute tubercles
  4. 7b Cocoon without anterodorsal projection
  5. 9b Terminal hooks widened, plate-like, with outer margin over 1.5 times length of inner margin, and crenulated
  6. 10b Stalk of ventral pair thinner than interspiracular trunk
  7. 11a Gill arranged as 3+2+(2+1) or 2+2+(2+1) from dorsal to ventral, with long common basal stalk 1.3–1.5 times length of interspiracular trunk
Thailand: Simulium asakoae species-group (mature larvae) (Takaoka et al. 2020) – couplet 5a
  1. 1a Abdominal segments 1–4 unicolorous
  2. 2b Abdominal segments 1–4 greyish or greenish grey or greenish
  3. 5a Pharate pupal gill with elongate common basal stalk
