Simulium baimaii Kuvangkadilok & Takaoka, 1999

ClassificationGenus Simulium, subgenus Simulium
Statusvalid
DistributionThailand (Takaoka et al. 2019) | Thailand (Takaoka & Kuvangkadilok 1999; Takaoka & Choochote 2004). | Thailand (Srisuka et al. 2017)
ThailandThailand (Takaoka et al. 2019, p. 57) | Type locality: Phra Aung Waterfall, Phu Kradung National Park, Loei Province, NE Thailand (altitude 1,124 m, on stones in fast-flowing 3 m wide stream; holotype pupa and 2 paratype mature larvae, 25.X.1995 and 23.V.1996) (Takaoka & Kuvangkadilok 1999 pp. 507-508); listed from Thailand (Takaoka & Choochote 2004 p. 190). | Thailand: 69 mature larvae (0.4% of total), 1.7% stream occurrence; breeds at Phu Kradueng, Loei Province, in slow-flowing sun-exposed high-mountain streams (Srisuka et al. 2017)
Diagnostic notesSimulium malyschevi species-group (Takaoka et al. 2019). Described in Takaoka & Kuvangkadilok (1999). | Described by Kuvangkadilok & Takaoka in Takaoka & Kuvangkadilok (1999 pp. 507-508); originally unplaced to subgenus due to unknown adults, placed in Simulium s.str. unplaced to species-group for convenience by Takaoka & Choochote (2004 p. 190 Note 5), and later placed in Simulium (Simulium) malyschevi species-group (Takaoka et al. 2019; Adler 2024). Pupa (body 3.1 mm): uniquely among Oriental Simuliidae has a gill of 2 filaments arising from a long inflated trunk (ca. 2 mm total length) with a pale basal bulb, surface irregularly wrinkled and densely covered with minute tubercles forming reticulate patterns near base; antennal sheath with 8 small tuberculate ridges; terga 7-9 with spine-combs; terminal hooks absent; wall-pocket cocoon 2.8 x 1.0 mm, thickly woven without windows. Mature larva (6.0-6.5 mm, yellowish): labral fan ca. 60 rays; antenna proportions 1.0:1.2:0.7; hypostomium with 9 teeth (median and intermediate teeth bent dorsally); postgenal cleft rounded apically, ca. 2.5x length of postgenal bridge; rectal gill compound (12-14 secondary lobules per lobe); posterior circlet 90-102 rows of up to 16 hooklets; ventral papillae and accessory sclerite absent. Compared with S. (Nevermannia) taulingense (Taiwan, 2 bulbous gill structures) and 2-filamented species of Afrotropical Byssodon and Neotropical Hearlea/Mayacnephia (Takaoka & Kuvangkadilok 1999 p. 508).
SourcesTakaoka et al. 2019, Takaoka & Kuvangkadilok 1999, Takaoka & Choochote 2004, Srisuka et al. 2017
