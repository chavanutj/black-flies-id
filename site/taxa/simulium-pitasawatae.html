Simulium pitasawatae Takaoka, Srisuka & Saeung, 2020

ClassificationGenus Simulium, subgenus Gomphostilbia
Statusvalid
DistributionThailand (Chiang Mai) (Takaoka et al. 2020) | Thailand (Takaoka 2025; Aupalee et al. 2024, 2026)
ThailandType locality: Huai Mor Nuea Village, Doi Saket, Chiang Mai Province, 1,154 m, 19°03'36.8"N 99°19'15.7"E, 2-II-2019 (Takaoka et al. 2020, p. 106) | Thailand: Chiang Mai, Doi Saket (Ban Pang Dang/Ban Pang Bong; Pang-Bong village 990 m, n=14 infected wild females confirmed by 100% COI match LC509533, haplotype AK-H6) (Aupalee et al. 2024, 2026)
ImportanceMost frequently infected species in Pang-Bong village, Chiang Mai (Aupalee et al. 2026 Tables 3-5): 14/27 pathogen-positive individual S. asakoae group flies caught on human bait were S. pitasawatae, positive in head+thorax for Leucocytozoon sabrazesi (5 flies, incl. 2 co-infected with Trypanosoma avium), L. schoutedeni (1), T. avium (6), Leptomonas spiculata (1), Onchocerca sp. Type I (2) and a putative new Onchocerca sp. (1, FL-07, closest 92.43% to O. skrjabini, sister to O. ramachandrini).
Diagnostic notesS. asakoae species-group (COI subgroup IV). Female: body 1.9-2.2 mm, mandible with 3-5 distinct outer teeth, claw tooth 0.43-0.47x claw, sensory vesicle 0.32-0.35x 3rd palp segment. Male: brown upper-eye facets in 14 vertical columns and 15 horizontal rows, fore basitarsus 7.9-8.3x width (vs 6.8x in S. tamdaoense), ventral plate nearly straight in caudal view. Pupa: dorsal triplet of gill filaments sessile or with extremely short stalk (vs nearly as long as common stalk in S. tamdaoense/S. tanahrataense), terminal hook outer margin 3.7-3.9x inner margin (3.3-4.0 in key; vs 1.8-1.9x in S. tamdaoense), ventral pair filaments 2.5-3.0 mm (vs 2.1-2.3 mm in S. maehongsonense). Larva: postgenal cleft 1.2-2.4x postgenal bridge, abdominal segments 1-4 light ochreous, labral fan 23-24 primary rays (vs 29-30 in S. sutheppuiense) (Takaoka et al. 2020, pp. 106-110). | S. asakoae species-group. Compared with S. kandisense (Takaoka 2025 p. 394): male upper-eye facets 13-14 x 15, narrower fore basitarsus (7.9-8.3x wide), pupal upper gill triplet stalk extremely short, wider terminal hooks (outer/inner margin 3.6-3.9x). Wild Thai females are morphologically indistinguishable from S. asakoae; yields 709 bp non-S. asakoae band in multiplex PCR (Aupalee et al. 2024, 2026).
SourcesTakaoka et al. 2020, Takaoka 2025, Aupalee et al. 2024, Aupalee et al. 2026

Thailand: Simulium asakoae species-group (females) (Takaoka et al. 2020) – couplet 12a
  1. 1b Hair tuft of base of radial vein yellow
  2. 2b Sensory vesicle short to medium-long, less than 0.4 times length of third palpal segment (Fig. 1A)
  3. 4a Mandible with distinct teeth on outer margin (Fig. 22B)
  4. 5a Body length 1.9–2.2 mm
  5. 6b Claw tooth 0.43–0.47 times length of claw
  6. 11b Sensory vesicle 0.26–0.35 times length of third palpal segment
  7. 12a Sensory vesicle 0.32–0.35 times length of third palpal segment
Thailand: Simulium asakoae species-group (males) (Takaoka et al. 2020) – couplet 22a
  1. 1b Hair tuft of base of radial vein yellow
  2. 2b Upper-eye (large) facets medium to dark brown
  3. 4b Antenna brownish except scape, pedicel and base of first flagellomere yellowish
  4. 5b Hind basitarsus 1.0–1.3 times as wide as hind femur (Fig. 4C)
  5. 11b Upper-eye (large) facets in 11 or more vertical columns
  6. 12b Upper-eye (large) facets in 12–17 vertical columns
  7. 16b Upper-eye (large) facets in 12–14 vertical columns
  8. 19b Upper-eye (large) facets in 15 or 16 horizontal rows
  9. 22a Ventral plate with ventral margin nearly straight when viewed caudally (Fig. 16F)
Thailand: Simulium asakoae species-group (pupae) (Takaoka et al. 2020) – couplet 13b
  1. 1b Cocoon with or without anterodorsal bulge or short anterodorsal projection (up to ca. 1.0 mm long) extended at most up to anteroventral tips of cocoon
  2. 3b Dorsum of abdominal segments 1 and 2 unpigmented or light yellowish
  3. 5b Dorsum of abdominal segment 1 without minute tubercles
  4. 7b Cocoon without anterodorsal projection
  5. 9b Terminal hooks widened, plate-like, with outer margin over 1.5 times length of inner margin, and crenulated
  6. 10b Stalk of ventral pair thinner than interspiracular trunk
  7. 11b Gill arranged as (3+3)+2 from dorsal to ventral, with medium-long common basal stalk shorter than interspiracular trunk ⚠
  8. 12b Outer margin of terminal hook 3.3–4.0 times as long as inner margin
  9. 13b Filaments of ventral pair 2.5–3.0 mm long (Fig. 16G)
Thailand: Simulium asakoae species-group (mature larvae) (Takaoka et al. 2020) – couplet 4a
  1. 1a Abdominal segments 1–4 unicolorous
  2. 2a Abdominal segments 1–4 light ochreous (Fig. 25A, B, C)
  3. 3b Postgenal cleft 1.2–2.4 times as long as postgenal bridge (Fig. 16K)
  4. 4a Labral fan with 23 or 24 primary rays
