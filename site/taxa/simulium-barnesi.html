Diagnostic notesSimulium variegatum species-group (Takaoka et al. 2019). Male, pupa and larva unknown (Takaoka et al. 2019, p. 75). | Simulium s.str. (variegatum species-group). Female: claw with small subbasal tooth, basal section of radial vein bare, fore basitarsus with moderate dorsal hair crest, mid and hind femora mostly brownish, scutum densely covered with yellow hairs. Male, pupa and mature larva were unknown in Takaoka & Choochote (2004 pp. 193, 195, 197). | Mukherjee et al. (2022 p. 113) list S. barnesi under subgenus Gomphostilbia (printing/classification discrepancy; treated under subgenus Simulium in Thai keys).
