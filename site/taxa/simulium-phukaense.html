Simulium phukaense Takaoka & Choochote, 2005

ClassificationGenus Simulium, subgenus Simulium
Statusvalid
DistributionThailand (Takaoka et al. 2019) | Thailand (Srisuka et al. 2017)
ThailandThailand (Takaoka et al. 2019, p. 56) | Thailand: 37 mature larvae (0.2% of total), 6.9% stream occurrence; recorded only during rainy season in central region (Srisuka et al. 2017)
Diagnostic notesSimulium griseifrons species-group (Takaoka et al. 2019). Larva unknown (Takaoka et al. 2019, p. 75).
SourcesTakaoka et al. 2019, Srisuka et al. 2017

How it keys out

The chain of characters leading to this taxon in each key (a ready-made diagnosis).

Thailand: Simulium (Simulium) females (Takaoka et al. 2019) – couplet 25a
  1. 1b Claw without any tooth
  2. 15a Scutum with 5 distinct longitudinal vittae
  3. 16a Scutum with 1 median, 2 submedian and 2 lateral dark vittae when illuminated anteriorly and viewed dorsally
  4. 17b Fore tibia with white shiny area on outer surface. Hind tibia whitish or yellowish on basal 2/3 or more, dark brown to brownish black on remainder. Inner margin of ovipositor valve without such flap
  5. 18b Basal section of radius bare or partially haired
  6. 24b Ovipositor valve unpigmented along inner margin
  7. 25a Cibarium with medial projection shorter than its basal width. Abdominal segment 2 entirely dark brown
Thailand: Simulium (Simulium) males (Takaoka et al. 2019) – couplet 27a
  1. 1b Basal section of radius bare or with few hairs apically
  2. 7b Scutum otherwise
  3. 9b Mid femur and tibia otherwise
  4. 11a Mid tibia mostly dark (including posterior surface)
  5. 12b Fore tibia medium to dark brown, without white area on outer surface
  6. 27a Abdomen with dorsolateral pair of silvery spots on segments 2, 6 and 7
Thailand: Simulium (Simulium) pupae (Takaoka et al. 2019) – couplet 31b
  1. 1b Gill with 6-10 fiaments ⚠
  2. 4a Gill with 6 filaments
  3. 5b Gill with 6 thread-like filaments (with basal portions somewhat inflated or not) without minute spines
  4. 7b Cocoon shoe shaped or boot shaped
  5. 27b Cocoon tightly woven
  6. 29b Cocoon without such flap and window
  7. 30a Gill filaments decreasing in length and thickness from dorsal to ventral. Facial and frontal trichomes branched
  8. 31b Gill filaments dark brown. Terminal hooks absent
