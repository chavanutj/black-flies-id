Simulium lampangense Takaoka & Choochote, 2005

ClassificationGenus Simulium, subgenus Simulium
Statusvalid
DistributionThailand (Takaoka et al. 2019) | Thailand (Srisuka et al. 2017)
ThailandThailand (Takaoka et al. 2019, p. 57) | Thailand: 139 mature larvae (0.7% of total), 5.2% stream occurrence; associated with calcareous waterfalls (Srisuka et al. 2017)
Diagnostic notesSimulium multistriatum species-group (Takaoka et al. 2019).

  10. 34b Fore basitarsus 4.7-4.9 times as long as its greatest width
