Simulium rudnicki Takaoka & Davies, 1995

ClassificationGenus Simulium, subgenus Simulium
Statusvalid
DistributionPeninsular Malaysia, Thailand (Takaoka et al. 2019) | Langkawi Island, Malaysia (original description, Takaoka & Davies 1995) and Thailand (Kuvangkadilok & Takaoka 2000; Takaoka & Choochote 2004). | Thailand (Putt et al. 2023)
ThailandThailand (Takaoka et al. 2019, p. 56) | Mae Ya Waterfall (520 m, 20 pupae, 216 larvae, 21.VIII.1996) and Vachiratharn Waterfall (700 m, 83 larvae, 15.II.1997), Doi Inthanon NP, Chiang Mai Province; Tad Deuan Waterfall (170 m, 13 larvae, 26.I.1999), Srisatchanalai NP, Sukhothai Province, northern Thailand (on rocks/stones in 1-2 m streams, 16-22 C, pH 8.4-9.0) (Kuvangkadilok & Takaoka 2000 pp. 174-175); listed from Thailand (Takaoka & Choochote 2004 p. 189). | Thailand (COI reference sequences KF289404-KF289406) (Putt et al. 2023)
Diagnostic notesSimulium crocinum species-group (Takaoka et al. 2019). | Simulium s.str. (originally ungrouped in Takaoka & Davies 1995; placed in griseifrons group in Takaoka & Choochote 2004; transferred to crocinum species-group in Takaoka et al. 2019). Female: simple claw, unvittate scutum, mid femur entirely yellow, anterior gonapophyses with narrow transparent inner/posterior margin (similar to S. yongi which has darker legs). Male: mid femur and tibia almost yellow. Pupa: 6 almost sessile subequal slender gill filaments, wall-pocket cocoon with anterolateral windows, dorsal spine-combs only on segment 8. Larva: abdomen widest on segment 8, cephalic apotome light-medium brown, posterior circlet 144-160 rows of hooklets (Kuvangkadilok & Takaoka 2000 pp. 174-175; Takaoka & Choochote 2004 pp. 194-196). | S. crocinum species-group; COI intraspecific p-distance 0.25-0.50% (Putt et al. 2023).
SourcesTakaoka et al. 2019, Kuvangkadilok & Takaoka 2000, Takaoka & Choochote 2004, Putt et al. 2023
