Simulium lomkaoense Takaoka & Srisuka, 2014

ClassificationGenus Simulium, subgenus Simulium
Statusvalid
DistributionThailand (Takaoka et al. 2019) | Thailand (Srisuka et al. 2017)
ThailandThailand (Takaoka et al. 2019, p. 57) | Thailand: 98 mature larvae (0.5% of total), 3.5% stream occurrence; central Thailand, associated with high elevation (Srisuka et al. 2017)
Diagnostic notesSimulium malyschevi species-group (Takaoka et al. 2019). Described in Takaoka et al. (2014c).
SourcesTakaoka et al. 2019, Srisuka et al. 2017
