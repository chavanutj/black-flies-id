Simulium namdanense Takaoka, Srisuka & Saeung, 2020

ClassificationGenus Simulium, subgenus Gomphostilbia
Statusvalid
Other names in keysSimulium namdangense
DistributionThailand (Chiang Mai and Nan) (Takaoka et al. 2020)
ThailandType locality: Nam Khwang stream, Nam Dan, Pua District, Nan Province, 25-VII-2017; also Chiang Mai (Takaoka et al. 2020, pp. 130-135)
Diagnostic notesS. asakoae species-group (COI subgroup I). Female: elongate sensory vesicle 0.64-0.68x 3rd palp segment (vs 0.50-0.54x in S. lurauense), mandible with 3-5 outer teeth (vs 8 in S. thituyenae), wing length 2.0 mm (vs 1.6 mm in S. klonglanense). Male: brown upper-eye facets in 15-16 vertical columns and 16-17 horizontal rows (vs 14-15 x 14-15 in S. lurauense and 13-14 x 14-15 in S. klonglanense), sensory vesicle 0.33x 3rd palp segment (vs 0.25-0.29x in S. lurauense), hind basitarsus spindle-shaped (0.9x hind femur), ventral plate nearly straight medially in caudal view (printed as 'S. namdangense' in male key 10b). Pupa: cocoon without anterodorsal projection, small conical non-crenulated terminal hooks (couplet 9a with S. klonglanense). Larva: postgenal cleft 2.5x postgenal bridge (vs 3.7-4.0x in S. klonglanense), abdominal segments 1-4 light greyish (Takaoka et al. 2020, pp. 130-135).
SourcesTakaoka et al. 2020

  3. 4b Antenna brownish except scape, pedicel and base of first flagellomere yellowish

  5. 6b Ventral plate with ventral margin nearly straight medially when viewed caudally (Fig. 20F)
  6. 9b Upper-eye (large) facets in 14–16 vertical columns
  7. 10b Upper-eye (large) facets in 15 or 16 vertical columns ⚠
Thailand: Simulium asakoae species-group (pupae) (Takaoka et al. 2020) – couplet 9a
  1. 1b Cocoon with or without anterodorsal bulge or short anterodorsal projection (up to ca. 1.0 mm long) extended at most up to anteroventral tips of cocoon
  2. 3b Dorsum of abdominal segments 1 and 2 unpigmented or light yellowish
  3. 5b Dorsum of abdominal segment 1 without minute tubercles
  4. 7b Cocoon without anterodorsal projection
  5. 9a Terminal hooks conical, with outer margin as long as or slightly longer than inner margin and not crenulated (Figs 22K, 23K)
