ClassificationGenus Simulium, subgenus Gomphostilbia
Statusvalid

ThailandThailand (Takaoka et al. 2019, p. 55)

Thailand: Simulium (Gomphostilbia) females (Takaoka et al. 2019) – couplet 13a
  1. 1b Antenna with 9 flagellomeres
  2. 6b Posterior surface of hind tibia white or yellow on basal 2/5 or more
  3. 11a Hind tibia with dark subbasal spot
  4. 12b Sensory vesicle of medium size, 0.3 times as long as third palpomere of maxillary palp
  5. 13a Abdominal segment 2 pale
Thailand: Simulium (Gomphostilbia) males (Takaoka et al. 2019) – couplet 23a
  1. 1b Antenna with 8 or 9 flagellomeres
  2. 2b Antenna with 9 flagellomeres
  3. 5b Hind basitarsus slender, much narrower than hind tibia
  4. 12b Mid and hind femora almost dark except base pale
  5. 13b Hind tibia whitish on basal 1/2 or more
  6. 19b Style gradually tapered when viewed ventrolaterally
  7. 21b Ventral plate rounded ventrally when viewed posteriorly
  8. 22a Upper-eye facets in 9 or 10 vertical columns and 10 or 11 horizontal rows
  9. 23a Sensory vesicle small, globular, 0.15 times as long as third palpomere of maxillary palp
Thailand: Simulium (Gomphostilbia) pupae (Takaoka et al. 2019) – couplet 22b
  1. 1b Gill filamentous
  2. 7b Gill with 8 or 10 filaments
  3. 10b Gill with 8 filaments
  4. 12b Frons and anterodorsal part of thorax with tubercles
  5. 13b Face with 1 trichome on each side
  6. 14b Gill filaments arranged as 3+3+2 or 6+2 from dorsal to ventral
  7. 15b Gill filaments arranged in 3 groups (3, 3 and 2 filaments from dorsal to ventral)

  9. 20a Dorsal and middle triplets and ventral pair arising basally at same level
  10. 21a Terminal hooks wide, plate-like, with crenulated outer margins
  11. 22b Six filaments of two triplets nearly of same thickness
Thailand: Simulium (Gomphostilbia) mature larvae (Takaoka et al. 2019) – couplet 16b
  1. 1b Abdomen without protuberances dorsally
  2. 6a Postgenal cleft long, approaching or completely reaching posterior margin of hypostoma
  3. 7b Abdomen not constricted between segments 4 and 5 when viewed dorsally
  4. 8b Abdomen moderately or densely covered with branched dark spinous setae or spinules dorsally on segments 5-8
  5. 10b Histoblast of gill with 8 or 10 filaments
  6. 11b Thoracic cuticle almost bare or sparsely covered with minute spinules dorsally. Histoblast of pupal gill with 8 filaments
  7. 12b Minute dark spinous setae on abdominal segments 5-8 slender and hair-like basally, with long branches
  8. 13b Labral fan with 32-41 primary rays
