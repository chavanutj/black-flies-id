Simulium trangense Jitklang, Kuvangkadilok, Baimai, Takaoka & Adler, 2008

ClassificationGenus Simulium, subgenus Gomphostilbia
Statusvalid
DistributionPeninsular Malaysia, Thailand (Takaoka et al. 2019) | Thailand (Srisuka et al. 2017)
ThailandThailand (Takaoka et al. 2019, p. 55) | Southern Thailand (cited in Srisuka et al. 2017 p. 2); also cited as dominant in hot/cold seasons in Phu Phan, NE Thailand (Srisuka et al. 2017 p. 10)
Diagnostic notesSimulium ceylonicum species-group, S. sheilae subgroup (Takaoka et al. 2019).
SourcesTakaoka et al. 2019, Srisuka et al. 2017

Thailand: Simulium (Gomphostilbia) females (Takaoka et al. 2019) – couplet 23b
  1. 1b Antenna with 9 flagellomeres
  2. 6b Posterior surface of hind tibia white or yellow on basal 2/5 or more
  3. 11b Hind tibia without dark subbasal spot
  4. 16b Hair tuft on base of radius dark
  5. 22b Frons : head ratio 1.0 : 4.2-5.0. Sensory vesicle enlarged, 0.55-0.59 times as long as third palpomere of maxillary palp ⚠
  6. 23b Fore basitarsus 5.0-5.2 times as long as its greatest width
