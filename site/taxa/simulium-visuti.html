How it keys out

The chain of characters leading to this taxon in each key (a ready-made diagnosis).

Thailand: Simulium (Simulium) females (Takaoka et al. 2019) – couplet 22a
  1. 1b Claw without any tooth
  2. 15a Scutum with 5 distinct longitudinal vittae
  3. 16a Scutum with 1 median, 2 submedian and 2 lateral dark vittae when illuminated anteriorly and viewed dorsally
  4. 17b Fore tibia with white shiny area on outer surface. Hind tibia whitish or yellowish on basal 2/3 or more, dark brown to brownish black on remainder. Inner margin of ovipositor valve without such flap
  5. 18a Basal section of radius fully haired
  6. 19b Ovipositor valve without such internal projection
  7. 21b Ovipositor valve with 12-16 hairs, much shorter than those on sternite 8
  8. 22a Paraproct with about 60 medium-long to long stout hairs, several of which are much longer than those on sternite 8
Thailand: Simulium (Simulium) males (Takaoka et al. 2019) – couplet 3b
  1. 1a Basal section of radius fully haired
  2. 2a Abdomen with pair of shiny whitish pruinose spots dorsally or dorsolaterally on segments 2, 5, 6 and 7
  3. 3b Upper-eye facets in 20 vertical columns. Hind basitarsus spindle-shaped
Thailand: Simulium (Simulium) pupae (Takaoka et al. 2019) – couplet 28b
  1. 1b Gill with 6-10 fiaments ⚠
  2. 4a Gill with 6 filaments
  3. 5b Gill with 6 thread-like filaments (with basal portions somewhat inflated or not) without minute spines
  4. 7b Cocoon shoe shaped or boot shaped
  5. 27a Cocoon loosely woven, with many small open spaces
  6. 28b Gill filaments not so inflated, smaller in diameter than interspiracular trunk. Terminal hooks of moderate size, shorter than their basal width
Thailand: Simulium (Simulium) mature larvae (Takaoka et al. 2019) – couplet 12b
  1. 1b Histoblast of pupal gill with 3, 6, 8 or 10 filaments
  2. 3b Histoblast of pupal gill with 6-10 filaments
  3. 4a Histoblast of pupal gill with 6 filaments
  4. 5b Last abdominal segment without accessory sclerite ventrally
  5. 7b Abdominal segments lacking protuberances
  6. 9a Abdomen with its greatest width on segment 8
  7. 10b Cephalic apotome mostly light to medium brown. Body length 8.1-11.2 mm
  8. 11b Posterior circlet with less than 160 rows of hooklets ⚠
  9. 12b Posterior circlet with 136-138 rows of hooklets
