Simulium puaense Takaoka, Srisuka & Saeung, 2020

ClassificationGenus Simulium, subgenus Gomphostilbia
Statusvalid
DistributionThailand (Lampang and Nan) (Takaoka et al. 2020) | Thailand (Putt et al. 2023)
ThailandType locality: Nam Dan Village, Pua District, Nan Province, 1,157 m, 19°11'10.3"N 101°04'41.7"E, 25-VII-2017; also Lampang (Takaoka et al. 2020, pp. 64-67) | Thailand (COI reference sequences LC509555-LC509556) (Putt et al. 2023)
Diagnostic notesS. asakoae species-group (COI subgroup IV). Mature larva unknown. Female: body 1.9-2.2 mm, mandible with distinct outer teeth, claw tooth 0.43-0.47x claw, small sensory vesicle 0.24x 3rd palp segment. Male: brown upper-eye (large) facets in 9-10 vertical columns and 12 horizontal rows (vs vermilion in S. thungchangense and S. chaudinhense), fore basitarsus 6.6-7.2x width (vs 8.2x in S. vinhphucense), hind basitarsus 3.2-3.6x width and 1.1-1.2x hind femur (vs 1.5x in S. vinhphucense), ventral plate trapezoidal in caudal view (vs rounded in S. vinhphucense). Pupa: terminal hook outer/inner margin 2.0-2.9 (couplet 12a) (Takaoka et al. 2020, pp. 64-67). | S. asakoae species-group; COI intraspecific p-distance 0.74% (Putt et al. 2023).
SourcesTakaoka et al. 2020, Putt et al. 2023

How it keys out

The chain of characters leading to this taxon in each key (a ready-made diagnosis).

Thailand: Simulium asakoae species-group (females) (Takaoka et al. 2020) – couplet 11a
  1. 1b Hair tuft of base of radial vein yellow
  2. 2b Sensory vesicle short to medium-long, less than 0.4 times length of third palpal segment (Fig. 1A)
  3. 4a Mandible with distinct teeth on outer margin (Fig. 22B)
  4. 5a Body length 1.9–2.2 mm
  5. 6b Claw tooth 0.43–0.47 times length of claw
  6. 11a Sensory vesicle 0.24 times length of third palpal segment
Thailand: Simulium asakoae species-group (males) (Takaoka et al. 2020) – couplet 11a
  1. 1b Hair tuft of base of radial vein yellow
  2. 2b Upper-eye (large) facets medium to dark brown
  3. 4b Antenna brownish except scape, pedicel and base of first flagellomere yellowish
  4. 5b Hind basitarsus 1.0–1.3 times as wide as hind femur (Fig. 4C)
  5. 11a Upper-eye (large) facets in 9 or 10 vertical columns
Thailand: Simulium asakoae species-group (pupae) (Takaoka et al. 2020) – couplet 12a
  1. 1b Cocoon with or without anterodorsal bulge or short anterodorsal projection (up to ca. 1.0 mm long) extended at most up to anteroventral tips of cocoon
  2. 3b Dorsum of abdominal segments 1 and 2 unpigmented or light yellowish
  3. 5b Dorsum of abdominal segment 1 without minute tubercles
  4. 7b Cocoon without anterodorsal projection
  5. 9b Terminal hooks widened, plate-like, with outer margin over 1.5 times length of inner margin, and crenulated
  6. 10b Stalk of ventral pair thinner than interspiracular trunk
  7. 11b Gill arranged as (3+3)+2 from dorsal to ventral, with medium-long common basal stalk shorter than interspiracular trunk ⚠
  8. 12a Outer margin of terminal hook 2.0–2.9 times as long as inner margin ⚠
