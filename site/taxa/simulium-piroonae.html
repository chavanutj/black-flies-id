Simulium piroonae Takaoka & Srisuka, 2014

ClassificationGenus Simulium, subgenus Gomphostilbia
Statusvalid
DistributionThailand (Takaoka et al. 2019) | Thailand (Srisuka et al. 2022) | Thailand (Srisuka et al. 2017)
ThailandThailand (Takaoka et al. 2019, p. 55) | Thailand (Takaoka et al. 2014, cited in Srisuka et al. 2022, pp. 2, 4) | Thailand: 52 mature larvae (0.3% of total), 1.7% stream occurrence (Srisuka et al. 2017)
Diagnostic notesSimulium varicorne species-group, S. chumpornense subgroup (Takaoka et al. 2019). Described in Takaoka et al. (2014b). | S. varicorne species-group, S. chumpornense subgroup. Female: antenna with 8 flagellomeres, pleural membrane bare, subcosta bare, hind tibia darkened on apical half, sensory vesicle 0.25-0.30x 3rd palp segment (vs 0.33-0.39x in S. khelangense), fore basitarsus 5.54-5.68x greatest width (vs 6.29-6.38x in S. khelangense), head 4.30-4.54x greatest width of frons (vs 3.78-4.05x in S. kuvangkadilokae) (Srisuka et al. 2022, pp. 4, 9).
SourcesTakaoka et al. 2019, Srisuka et al. 2022, Srisuka et al. 2017

The chain of characters leading to this taxon in each key (a ready-made diagnosis).

  1. 1a Antenna with 7 or 8 flagellomeres
  2. 2b Antenna with 8 flagellomeres
  3. 3b Tergite 5 not shiny
  4. 4a Mandible with 3-5 teeth on outer margin ⚠
Thailand: Simulium (Gomphostilbia) males (Takaoka et al. 2019) – couplet 4b
  1. 1b Antenna with 8 or 9 flagellomeres
  2. 2a Antenna with 8 flagellomeres
  3. 3b Ventral plate nearly flat
  4. 4b Upper-eye facets in 11 or 12 vertical columns and 12 or 13 horizontal rows
Thailand: Simulium (Gomphostilbia) pupae (Takaoka et al. 2019) – couplet 8a
  1. 1b Gill filamentous
  2. 7a Gill with 6 filaments
  3. 8a Cocoon without anterodorsal projection
Thailand: Simulium (Gomphostilbia) mature larvae (Takaoka et al. 2019) – couplet 3a
  1. 1a Abdomen with protuberances dorsally
  2. 2b Abdominal segments 1 and 2 each with 1-3 pairs of protuberances
  3. 3a Abdominal segments 1 and 2 each with 3 pairs of protuberances
Simulium varicorne species-group (females) (Srisuka et al. 2022) – couplet 10b
  1. 1b Antenna with eight flagellomeres
  2. 3b Pleural membrane bare
  3. 4b Subcosta bare
  4. 8a Hind tibia darkened on apical half
  5. 9b Fore basitarsus 5.54–5.68 times as long as its greatest width
