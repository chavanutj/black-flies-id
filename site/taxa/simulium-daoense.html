Simulium daoense Takaoka & Adler, 2017

ClassificationGenus Simulium, subgenus Simulium
Statusvalid
DistributionThailand, Vietnam (Takaoka et al. 2019) | Vietnam: Lao Cai, Sa Pa, 1,315 m (paratypes OQ117837-OQ117840); Thailand (GenBank MG734010-MG734014) (Putt et al. 2023)
ThailandThailand (Takaoka et al. 2019, p. 57) | Thailand (GenBank reference sequences MG734010-MG734014 in COI tree) (Putt et al. 2023 Fig. 4)
Diagnostic notesSimulium multistriatum species-group (Takaoka et al. 2019). Described in Takaoka et al. (2017a). | S. multistriatum species-group. Extreme intraspecific COI divergence (0-15.10%, mean 7.10%) between paratypes and published topotypes (MG734007-MG734009), with the latter clustering inside the S. striatum species-group (Putt et al. 2023 pp. 10, 18).
SourcesTakaoka et al. 2019, Putt et al. 2023
