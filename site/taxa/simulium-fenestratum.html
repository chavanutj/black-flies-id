Simulium fenestratum Edwards, 1934

ClassificationGenus Simulium, subgenus Simulium
Statusvalid
Other names in keysSimulium fensestratum
DistributionIndonesia (Sumatra), Laos, Thailand (Takaoka et al. 2019) | Sumatra (Edwards 1934) and Thailand (Takaoka & Kuvangkadilok 1999; Kuvangkadilok & Takaoka 2000; Takaoka & Choochote 2004). | Thailand (all 6 regions) (Srisuka et al. 2017) | Indonesia: Sumatra (West Sumatra, associated with S. kotsuboyui, S. solokense and S. bengkuruense) (Takaoka 2025)
ThailandThailand (Takaoka et al. 2019, p. 57) | Listed from Thailand (Takaoka & Choochote 2004 p. 189; previously reported as S. sakishimaense per Takaoka & Kuvangkadilok 1999 p. 497); Maekham Waterfall, Phrae (Takaoka & Kuvangkadilok 1999 p. 501); Songkhla/Trang/Prachuap Khiri Khan with S. sheilae (Kuvangkadilok & Takaoka 2000 p. 172). | Thailand: most abundant species overall with 1,681 mature larvae (8.6% of total) and 2nd most frequent (82.8%, 48/58 sites across all 6 regions, dominant in all 3 seasons; 24% in Eastern region, 80-100% stream occurrence across regions) (Srisuka et al. 2017)
Diagnostic notesSimulium multistriatum species-group. Species complex (Thaijarern et al. 2018, cited in Takaoka et al. 2019). Previously misidentified as S. sakishimaense (not sensu Takaoka, 1977). Two larval morphoforms (A with dorsal abdominal protuberances, B without) noted in Sumatra and Thailand (Takaoka et al. 2019, p. 75). | Simulium s.str. (multistriatum species-group). Female: vittate scutum, smooth medial cibarial projection, 1 spermatheca, hind basitarsus whitish yellow on slightly more than basal 1/2. Male: upper eye 17 vertical columns x 17 horizontal rows, hind basitarsus dark with basal 2/5 pale, ventral plate with irregularly situated posterior teeth. Pupa: 8 gill filaments, wall-pocket cocoon with anterolateral window on each side. Larva: not separated from S. malayense in Takaoka & Choochote (2004 p. 196, where printed as 'fensestratum') (Takaoka & Choochote 2004 pp. 194-196).
SourcesTakaoka et al. 2019, Takaoka & Kuvangkadilok 1999, Kuvangkadilok & Takaoka 2000, Takaoka & Choochote 2004, Takaoka 2025, Srisuka et al. 2017
