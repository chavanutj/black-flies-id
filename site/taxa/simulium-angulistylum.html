Simulium angulistylum Takaoka & Davies, 1995

ClassificationGenus Simulium, subgenus Gomphostilbia
Statusvalid
DistributionPeninsular Malaysia, Thailand (Takaoka et al. 2019) | Peninsular Malaysia (original description, Takaoka & Davies 1995); Thailand (Kuvangkadilok & Takaoka 2000; Takaoka & Choochote 2004). | Thailand (Srisuka et al. 2017)
ThailandThailand (Takaoka et al. 2019, p. 55) | Nakhon Ratchasima (Haew Suwat Waterfall, Khao Yai NP, 630 m), Prachuap Khiri Khan (Huai Yang Waterfall, 75 m), Phangnga (Sa Nangmanora Waterfall, 150 m), Ranong (Khao Phra Narai Waterfall 120 m; Ngao Waterfall 50 m), Surat Thani (Muang Tuad Waterfall, Tai Rom Yen NP, 100 m), Ubon Ratchathani (Huai Luang Waterfall, Phu-Jong Na-Yoy NP, 300 m) (Kuvangkadilok & Takaoka 2000 pp. 172); listed from Thailand (Takaoka & Choochote 2004 p. 189). | Thailand: 515 mature larvae (2.7% of total), 22.4% stream occurrence across 58 sites; predominant in Western region (9.3%, n=317; 62.5% of sites); cited as dominant in rainy season in Phu Phan, NE Thailand (Srisuka et al. 2017)
Diagnostic notesSimulium epistum species-group, S. atratum subgroup. Species complex: chromosomal/molecular analyses revealed two or more cytospecies, cytoforms or molecular lineages in Thai populations (Pramual & Kuvangkadilok 2012, cited in Takaoka et al. 2019). | Gomphostilbia (placed in batoense species-group in Takaoka & Choochote 2004; transferred to epistum species-group, atratum subgroup in Takaoka et al. 2019). Characterized by 8 short slender pupal gill filaments (<1.6 mm, 3+3+2 arrangement, smooth antennal sheath) and male genitalia with an abruptly bent style and posteriorly attenuated ventral plate; female sensory vesicle 0.3x length of 3rd maxillary palpal segment; larval abdominal segments 5-8 with minute dark spinous setae bearing 5-12 (mostly 8-10) branches (Kuvangkadilok & Takaoka 2000 p. 172; Takaoka & Choochote 2004 pp. 191-193). | Treated as S. angulistylum complex (Srisuka et al. 2017).
SourcesTakaoka et al. 2019, Kuvangkadilok & Takaoka 2000, Takaoka & Choochote 2004, Srisuka et al. 2017
