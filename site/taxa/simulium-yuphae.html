Simulium yuphae Takaoka & Choochote, 2005

ClassificationGenus Simulium, subgenus Simulium
Statusvalid
DistributionThailand (Takaoka et al. 2019) | Thailand (Srisuka et al. 2017; Putt et al. 2023) | Vietnam: Thua Thien Hue (Bach Ma, 434-1,274 m; cytoform A, morphologically S. cavum = S. huense) and Vinh Phuc (Tam Dao, 975 m) (Adler et al. 2016)
ThailandThailand (Takaoka et al. 2019, p. 57) | Thailand: 311 mature larvae (1.6% of total), 36.2% stream occurrence; 3rd most frequent in Northern region (73.3% of sites); rainy season only in Eastern region (Srisuka et al. 2017); COI sequences MZ508681-MZ508683, MZ508687 (Putt et al. 2023)
Diagnostic notesSimulium tuberosum species-group, S. brevipar subgroup (Takaoka et al. 2019). Previously misidentified as S. brevipar (not sensu Takaoka & Davies, 1995). | S. tuberosum species-group. Cytoform A (fixed IIIL-12,13; undifferentiated sex chromosomes) is chromosomally identical between Thailand and Vietnamese S. cavum (= S. huense) (Adler et al. 2016 pp. 15, 22-23); cytoform B (Sa Pa, Vietnam) was later described as S. sapaense (Putt et al. 2023). COI intraspecific p-distance 0-0.25% in Thailand, non-monophyletic with S. sapaense (Putt et al. 2023 Fig. 4).
SourcesTakaoka et al. 2019, Adler et al. 2016, Srisuka et al. 2017, Putt et al. 2023
