Simulium suchitrae Takaoka, 2010

ClassificationGenus Simulium, subgenus Asiosimulium
Statusvalid
DistributionNepal (Srisuka et al. 2024) | Nepal (1,826 m, slow-flowing small rock stream) (Srisuka et al. 2017 p. 10)
Diagnostic notesSubgenus Asiosimulium. Male unknown. Female: cercus <2x basal width, abdominal segment 5 dull dorsally, spermatheca pear-shaped, arm of genital fork without projection (vs stout projection in S. furvum). Pupa: head integument without tubercles, gill with 28 filaments and extremely bulbous transparent basal fenestra. Larva: histoblast of pupal gill with extremely bulbous basal fenestra (Srisuka et al. 2024, pp. 2, 14-16).
SourcesSrisuka et al. 2024, Srisuka et al. 2017
