Simulium quinquestriatum (Shiraki, 1935)

ClassificationGenus Simulium, subgenus Simulium
Statusvalid
Other names in keysSimulium quiquestriatum
DistributionTaiwan, China, Japan, Korea, Thailand, Vietnam (Takaoka et al. 2019) | Thailand (Takaoka & Kuvangkadilok 1999; Kuvangkadilok & Takaoka 2000; Takaoka & Choochote 2004). | Thailand (Srisuka et al. 2017) | Vietnam: Nghe An, Quy Chau, 80 m (COI OQ117854) (Putt et al. 2023)
ThailandThailand (Takaoka et al. 2019, p. 57) | Listed from Thailand (Takaoka & Choochote 2004 p. 190); collected at Maekham Waterfall, Phrae (Takaoka & Kuvangkadilok 1999 p. 501) and with S. angulistylum, S. grossifilum and S. rudnicki (Kuvangkadilok & Takaoka 2000 pp. 172, 174-175). | Thailand: 614 mature larvae (3.2% of total), 34.1% stream occurrence; predominant in Northeastern region (8.3%, n=219; 90% sites); lowland streams (Srisuka et al. 2017)
Diagnostic notesSimulium striatum species-group (Takaoka et al. 2019). Misprinted as 'Simulium quiquestriatum' in the checklist (p. 57); spelled 'S. quinquestriatum' in the keys. | Simulium s.str. (striatum species-group). Female not separated from S. chiangmaiense and S. nakhonense in Takaoka & Choochote (2004 p. 193). Male: fore tibia without white shiny area, hind basitarsus whitish yellow on basal 1/2 (more or less). Pupa: 10 gill filaments arranged 2+3+3+2, all subequal in thickness. Larva: dorsal abdominal protuberances, pharate pupal gill 10 filaments in 2+3+3+2 arrangement (Takaoka & Choochote 2004 pp. 195-196). | S. striatum species-group. Non-monophyletic in COI; separated morphologically from S. nakhonense by pupal gill arrangement and from S. tavanense/S. taythienense by male wing/scutal hair characters (Putt et al. 2023 p. 16).
SourcesTakaoka et al. 2019, Takaoka & Kuvangkadilok 1999, Kuvangkadilok & Takaoka 2000, Takaoka & Choochote 2004, Srisuka et al. 2017, Putt et al. 2023

  1. 1b Claw without any tooth
  2. 15a Scutum with 5 distinct longitudinal vittae
  3. 16a Scutum with 1 median, 2 submedian and 2 lateral dark vittae when illuminated anteriorly and viewed dorsally
