Simulium siamense Takaoka & Suzuki, 1984

ClassificationGenus Simulium, subgenus Gomphostilbia
Statusvalid
DistributionNepal, Thailand, Vietnam (Takaoka et al. 2019) | Thailand (Takaoka & Kuvangkadilok 1999; Kuvangkadilok & Takaoka 2000; Takaoka & Choochote 2004). | Thailand (Srisuka et al. 2017; Putt et al. 2023)
ThailandThailand (Takaoka et al. 2019, p. 55) | Listed from Thailand (Takaoka & Choochote 2004 p. 189); collected across northern, northeastern and southern Thailand with S. chaliowae, S. chainarongi, S. angulistylum, S. dentistylum, S. nobile and S. rudnicki (Takaoka & Kuvangkadilok 1999 pp. 501, 504; Kuvangkadilok & Takaoka 2000 pp. 172-175). | Thailand: 1,441 mature larvae (7.4% of total; 4th most predominant), 3rd most frequent taxon (75.9%, 44/58 sites across all 6 regions, mainly lowland streams; 100% of Northeastern and Eastern sites, 90% Central, 66.7% Northern, 62.5% Western) (Srisuka et al. 2017)
Diagnostic notesSimulium batoense species-group, S. duolongum subgroup. Species complex (Kuvangkadilok et al. 2008; Pramual & Wongpakam 2011; cited in Takaoka et al. 2019). | Gomphostilbia (batoense species-group). Female: 11-segmented antenna, dark femora, hind tibia mostly dark. Male: slender hind basitarsus, hind tibia mostly dark, shiny whitish-pruinose dorsolateral patches on abdominal segments 2, 5, 6 and 7. Pupa: 8 long gill filaments (>1.9 mm), dorsal/middle triplet groups and ventral pair arising basally at the same level. Larva: minute dark spinous setae on abdominal segments 5-8 somewhat flat and stout basally with short apical branches (Takaoka & Choochote 2004 pp. 191-192). | S. batoense species-group; species complex. Thai COI sequences (HM775225, HM775232, HQ738688; 0.50-1.24% p-distance) form a sister clade and single COI OTU with two paratypes of Vietnamese S. yvonneae (OQ117804-OQ117805) (Putt et al. 2023 p. 10).
SourcesTakaoka et al. 2019, Takaoka & Kuvangkadilok 1999, Kuvangkadilok & Takaoka 2000, Takaoka & Choochote 2004, Srisuka et al. 2017, Putt et al. 2023
