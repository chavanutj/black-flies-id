Simulium tani Takaoka & Davies, 1995

ClassificationGenus Simulium, subgenus Simulium
Statusvalid
Other names in keysSimulium nitidithorax
DistributionPeninsular Malaysia, Indonesia (Sumatra), Thailand, Taiwan, Vietnam (Takaoka et al. 2019) | Thailand (Takaoka & Kuvangkadilok 1999; Kuvangkadilok & Takaoka 2000; Takaoka & Choochote 2004). | Malaysia: Peninsular Malaysia (Cameron Highlands, Pahang = cytoform K, representing S. tani s.str.) | Thailand (Chiang Mai, Tak, Chanthaburi, Kanchanaburi, Mae Hong Son; nationwide) | Vietnam (Lam Dong, Thua Thien Hue, Nghe An) | Taiwan (Hsinchu County = cytoform L) | Indonesia: Sumatra (West Sumatra, associated with S. kotsuboyui and S. solokense) (Low et al. 2016; Adler et al. 2016; Srisuka et al. 2017; Putt et al. 2023; Takaoka 2025)
ThailandThailand (Takaoka et al. 2019, p. 57) | Listed from Thailand (Takaoka & Choochote 2004 p. 190; previously reported as S. nitidithorax per Takaoka & Kuvangkadilok 1999 p. 497); collected at Maekham Waterfall, Phrae (Takaoka & Kuvangkadilok 1999 p. 501) and widely with S. angulistylum, S. sheilae, S. dentistylum, S. gombakense and S. grossifilum (Kuvangkadilok & Takaoka 2000 pp. 172-174). | Thailand: cytoforms A (Chiang Mai, Tak), B (Chanthaburi), C (Kanchanaburi) and G (Mae Hong Son) (Low et al. 2016 Table 1) | 385 mature larvae (2.0% of total), 6th most frequent taxon (48.3%, 28/58 sites; 100% of Eastern sites, 75% Western; rainy season only in Central region; lowland streams) (Srisuka et al. 2017)
Diagnostic notesSimulium tuberosum species-group, S. nitidithorax subgroup. Species complex (Tangkawanit et al. 2009, cited in Takaoka et al. 2019). Previously misidentified as S. nitidithorax (not sensu Puri, 1932). | Simulium s.str. (tuberosum species-group). Female: unvittate scutum with dark brown hairs only, no ventral hair cluster on abdominal segment 7, enlarged sensory vesicle (0.7x 3rd palpal segment). Male: much-enlarged hind basitarsus whitish yellow on basal 1/2 or slightly less, upper eye 15-17 vertical columns. Pupa: 6 gill filaments, wall-pocket cocoon without windows, head/thorax tuberculate, terminal hooks present. Larva: reddish brown body, cephalic apotome without posteromedian dark area, rectal organ 7-12 lobules/lobe, posterior circlet 70-74 rows (Takaoka & Choochote 2004 pp. 194-197). | S. tuberosum species-group; largest Oriental black fly species complex (9 Thai cytoforms A-I; Malaysian cytoform K [= holotype/s.str.]; Taiwanese cytoform L; Vietnamese cytoforms B2, M [= morphoform 'b' = S. suoivangense], N and S. xuandei). Low et al. (2016) show COI/COII fail to resolve the complex, whereas nuclear ECP1 + concatenated COI+COII+ECP1 validate cytoform K and morphoform 'b', reveal possible cryptic species in cytoform L, morphoform 'a' and S. xuandei, place cytoform B in early reproductive isolation, and indicate incomplete lineage sorting among Thai cytoforms A, C and G. Adler et al. (2016) find Vietnamese cytoforms B2 and N (both morphoform 'a') reproductively isolated in sympatry (3 fixed inversions IIIL-5,54,55 in N, no hybrids). Putt et al. (2023) report 0.50-5.94% COI p-distance and non-monophyly with S. suoivangense and S. xuandei; pupa retains terminal hooks (absent in S. suoivangense).
