Simulium grossifilum Takaoka & Davies, 1995

ClassificationGenus Simulium, subgenus Simulium
Statusvalid
DistributionPeninsular Malaysia, Thailand, Vietnam (Takaoka et al. 2019) | Peninsular Malaysia (original description, Takaoka & Davies 1995) and Thailand (Kuvangkadilok & Takaoka 2000; Takaoka & Choochote 2004). | Thailand (Srisuka et al. 2017) | Vietnam: Vinh Phuc, Tam Dao National Park, 997 m (Putt et al. 2023)
ThailandThailand (Takaoka et al. 2019, p. 56) | Muang Tuad Waterfall (100 m), Tai Rom Yen NP, Nakhon Si Thammarat Province (1 reared male, 8 larvae, 3.VI.1999), Ngao Waterfall (50 m), Ranong Province (56 larvae, 4.VIII.1999), Mae Yai Waterfall (100 m), Khao Sok NP, Surat Thani Province (1 reared female, 1 pupa, 3 larvae, 2.VIII.1999), southern Thailand (Kuvangkadilok & Takaoka 2000 pp. 173-174); listed from Thailand (Takaoka & Choochote 2004 p. 189). | Thailand: 15 mature larvae (0.1% of total), 3.5% stream occurrence (Srisuka et al. 2017)
ImportanceAttracted to humans in northern Vietnam (Putt et al. 2023 p. 2).
Diagnostic notesSimulium grossifilum species-group (Takaoka et al. 2019). | Simulium s.str. (placed in griseifrons species-group in Kuvangkadilok & Takaoka 2000 and Takaoka & Choochote 2004; transferred to grossifilum species-group in Takaoka et al. 2019). Female: vittate scutum, basal radial vein fully haired, hind basitarsus whitish on basal 3/5, rounded ovipositor valve with short setae and elongate internal/dorsal projection. Male: lamellate ventral plate ca. 2.3x as wide as long and much longer than arms. Pupa: 6 swollen/inflated tubular gill filaments with rounded apices covered with minute spines/setae. Larva: postgenal cleft medium-sized (nearly as long as wide, pointed apically), pharate pupal gill with 6 inflated tubular filaments (Kuvangkadilok & Takaoka 2000 pp. 173-174; Takaoka & Choochote 2004 pp. 193-197). | S. grossifilum species-group. Monophyletic in COI (0.00% intraspecific p-distance, n=4) and supported by PTP_ML, ASAP and GMYC (Putt et al. 2023).
SourcesTakaoka et al. 2019, Kuvangkadilok & Takaoka 2000, Takaoka & Choochote 2004, Srisuka et al. 2017, Putt et al. 2023
