Paraustrosimulium anthracinum (Bigot)

ClassificationGenus Paraustrosimulium
Statusvalid
Other names in keysAustrosimulium (Paraustrosimulium) anthracinum; Simulium anthracinum
DistributionSouthern Chile, from Tierra del Fuego to Angol (Dumbleton 1963)
Diagnostic notesFemale mandible toothed on both sides of apex; pupal breathing organ lamellar, pseudosegmented, without filaments; larva with anal spines (Dumbleton 1963 key couplet 1b).
SourcesDumbleton 1963
