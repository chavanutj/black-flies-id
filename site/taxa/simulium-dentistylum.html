Simulium dentistylum Takaoka & Davies, 1995

ClassificationGenus Simulium, subgenus Gomphostilbia
Statusvalid
DistributionPeninsular Malaysia, Thailand (Takaoka et al. 2019) | Peninsular Malaysia (original description, Takaoka & Davies 1995); Thailand (Kuvangkadilok & Takaoka 2000; Takaoka & Choochote 2004). | Thailand (Srisuka et al. 2017)
ThailandThailand (Takaoka et al. 2019, p. 55) | Huai Luang Waterfall (300 m), Phu-Jong Na-Yoy NP, Ubon Ratchathani Province, NE Thailand (1 reared male, 16.XI.1998) and Yong Waterfall (120 m), Nakhon Si Thammarat Province, southern Thailand (1 pupa, 19.IV.1999) (Kuvangkadilok & Takaoka 2000 p. 173; note Adler 2024 attributes this 2000 record to S. ngaoense while retaining S. dentistylum for Thailand); listed from Thailand (Takaoka & Choochote 2004 p. 189). | Thailand: 367 mature larvae (1.9% of total), 37.9% stream occurrence; present at 100% of Eastern sites (8.1%, n=163); associated with low elevation in central/northeastern regions (Srisuka et al. 2017)
Diagnostic notesSimulium batoense species-group, S. parahiyangum subgroup (Takaoka et al. 2019). Female unknown (Takaoka et al. 2019, p. 64). | Gomphostilbia (batoense species-group, parahiyangum subgroup). Female and male: 11-segmented antenna, mid and hind femora yellow except dark apical cap. Pupa: 8 short slender filaments (<1.6 mm) in 4 groups (2+1+3+2 from dorsal to ventral), antennal sheath with less marked ridges bearing a few tubercles (separating from S. parahiyangum which has 3+3+2 filaments and stronger multi-tuberculate ridges). Larva: lacking dorsal protuberances (present in S. parahiyangum), postgenal cleft very long reaching hypostomium, abdomen markedly constricted between segments 4 and 5 (Kuvangkadilok & Takaoka 2000 p. 173; Takaoka & Choochote 2004 pp. 191-192).
SourcesTakaoka et al. 2019, Kuvangkadilok & Takaoka 2000, Takaoka & Choochote 2004, Srisuka et al. 2017
