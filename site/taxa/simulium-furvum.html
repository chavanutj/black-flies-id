Simulium furvum Takaoka & Srisuka, 2013

ClassificationGenus Simulium, subgenus Asiosimulium
Statusvalid
DistributionThailand (Takaoka et al. 2019) | Thailand (Srisuka et al. 2024) | Thailand (Srisuka et al. 2017)
ThailandThailand (Takaoka et al. 2019, p. 54) | Thailand (Srisuka et al. 2024, p. 2, Fig. 1) | Thailand: 34 mature larvae (0.2% of total), 1.7% stream occurrence; restricted to sun-exposed lowland slow-flowing rock-bed streams during rainy season (Srisuka et al. 2017)
Diagnostic notesSubgenus Asiosimulium (Takaoka et al. 2019). Described in Takaoka et al. (2013a). | Subgenus Asiosimulium (COI Clade II). Female: cercus <2x basal width, abdominal segment 5 dull dorsally, spermatheca pear-shaped, arm of genital fork with stout projection. Male: hind basitarsus 0.90-1.00x hind tibia, abdominal segments 5-8 without shiny patches, upper-eye (large) facets in 16 vertical columns. Pupa: head and thorax moderately tuberculate, gill with 22 filaments. Larva: basal fenestra normal/small, postgenal cleft with pigmented sheath of subesophageal ganglion, gill histoblast with 22 filaments (Srisuka et al. 2024, pp. 14-16).
SourcesTakaoka et al. 2019, Srisuka et al. 2024, Srisuka et al. 2017
