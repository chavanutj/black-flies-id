Simulium parahiyangum Takaoka & Sigit, 1992

ClassificationGenus Simulium, subgenus Gomphostilbia
Statusvalid
DistributionIndonesia; reported from Thailand (Takaoka & Kuvangkadilok 1999; Kuvangkadilok & Takaoka 2000; Takaoka & Choochote 2004), though Thai populations were later described as S. ngaoense (Takaoka et al. 2018a, 2019). | India (Mukherjee et al. 2022) | Indonesia: Sumatra (West Sumatra, associated with S. kotsuboyui and S. solokense) (Takaoka 2025) | Thailand (Srisuka et al. 2017)
ThailandListed from Thailand (Takaoka & Choochote 2004 p. 189); collected in Ubon Ratchathani with S. chainarongi (Takaoka & Kuvangkadilok 1999 p. 504) and with S. angulistylum, S. dentistylum and S. grossifilum (Kuvangkadilok & Takaoka 2000 pp. 172-174). Thai populations were later described as S. ngaoense (Takaoka et al. 2018a, 2019). | Thailand: 3 mature larvae, 1.7% stream occurrence (Srisuka et al. 2017)
Diagnostic notesGomphostilbia (batoense species-group). Female: 11-segmented antenna, hind tibia with dark subbasal spot, enlarged sensory vesicle (0.5-0.6x 3rd palpal segment), genital fork arms without anterior projection. Male: upper eye 17 vertical columns x 15 horizontal rows, shiny patches on abdominal segments 2, 6 and 7. Pupa: 8 short filaments (<1.6 mm) in 3+3+2 arrangement, antennal sheath with marked ridges each bearing several tubercles. Larva: dorsal/dorsolateral protuberances on thoracic segment 3 and abdominal segments 1-5, postgenal cleft reaching hypostomium, thorax and abdomen densely covered with dark spines of various sizes (Kuvangkadilok & Takaoka 2000 p. 173; Takaoka & Choochote 2004 pp. 191-192).
SourcesTakaoka & Kuvangkadilok 1999, Kuvangkadilok & Takaoka 2000, Takaoka & Choochote 2004, Mukherjee et al. 2022, Takaoka 2025, Srisuka et al. 2017

The chain of characters leading to this taxon in each key (a ready-made diagnosis).

Thailand (2004): Simulium (Gomphostilbia) females (Takaoka & Choochote 2004) – couplet 7b
  1. 1b Antenna composed of 11 segments
  2. 3b Mid and hind femora alomst dark except base pale ⚠
  3. 4b Hind tibia whitish yellow or dark yellow on basal 2/5 or more
  4. 5a Hind tibia with a dark subbasal spot
  5. 6b Sensory vesicle enlarged, 0.5 or 0.6 times as long as 3rd maxillary palpal segment
  6. 7b Arms of genital fork without such a projection ⚠
Thailand (2004): Simulium (Gomphostilbia) males (Takaoka & Choochote 2004) – couplet 9b
  1. 1b Antenna composed of 11 segments
  2. 3b Hind basitarsus slender, much thinner than hind tibia
  3. 6b Mid and hind femora almost dark except base pale
  4. 7a Hind tibia almost dark except base pale
  5. 8b Abdominal segments 2, 6 and 7 each with a dorsolateral pair of shiny whitish-pruinose patches
  6. 9b Upper eye with 17 vertical columns and 15 horizontal rows of large facets ⚠
Thailand (2004): Simulium (Gomphostilbia) pupae (Takaoka & Choochote 2004) – couplet 6a
  1. 1b Gill with 8 or 10 filaments
  2. 2b Gill with 8 filaments
  3. 3a Gill filaments all shorter than pupal body (shorter than 1.6 mm), short-stalked
  4. 4b Antennal sheath with tubercles
  5. 6a Antennal sheath with marked ridges each corresponding to flagellar segments 1-9, each ridge covered with several tubercles; gill filaments arranged in 3 groups (3+3+2 filaments from dorsal to ventral) ⚠
Thailand (2004): Simulium (Gomphostilbia) mature larvae (Takaoka & Choochote 2004) – couplet 2a
  1. 1a Thoracic segment 3 and abdominal segments 1-5 each with 1 or 2 pairs of dorsal and/or dorsolateral protuberances
  2. 2a Postgenal cleft very long, its apex reaching the posterior margin of hypostomium; thorax and abdomen densely covered with dark spines of various sizes ⚠
