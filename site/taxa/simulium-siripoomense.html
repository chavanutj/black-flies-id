Simulium siripoomense Takaoka & Saito, 1996

ClassificationGenus Simulium, subgenus Simulium
Statusvalid
Other names in keysSimulium (Byssodon) siripoomense
DistributionThailand (Takaoka et al. 2019) | Thailand (Takaoka & Choochote 2004). | Thailand (Srisuka et al. 2017; Putt et al. 2023)
ThailandThailand (Takaoka et al. 2019, p. 57) | Listed from Thailand (Takaoka & Choochote 2004 p. 189; originally in Byssodon, transferred to Simulium s.str. by Takaoka & Mulla 2000 ref. 5 p. 197). | Thailand: 19 mature larvae (0.1% of total), 1.7% stream occurrence (Srisuka et al. 2017); COI reference sequences MZ508654, MZ508658-MZ508660 (Putt et al. 2023)
Diagnostic notesSimulium malyschevi species-group (Takaoka et al. 2019). | Simulium s.str. (malyschevi species-group). Female: claw with small subbasal tooth, bare basal radial vein, scutum moderately covered with brassy hairs, abdominal segment 7 with branched hairs medially on ventral surface. Male: narrow parallel-sided white hind basitarsus (apical 1/6 light brown). Pupa: 6 short-stalked subequal gill filaments, wall-pocket cocoon with anterolateral windows, dorsal spine-combs on segments 7-9. Larva: abdominal segments 1-8 each with 1-6 pairs of dorsal and dorsolateral protuberances (Takaoka & Choochote 2004 pp. 193-196). | S. malyschevi species-group; COI intraspecific p-distance 0.74-1.98% (Putt et al. 2023).
SourcesTakaoka et al. 2019, Takaoka & Choochote 2004, Srisuka et al. 2017, Putt et al. 2023
