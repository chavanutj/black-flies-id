Thailand: Simulium (Simulium) females (Takaoka et al. 2019) – couplet 10a
  1. 1a Claw with small subbasal tooth
  2. 2b Basal section of radius bare. Fore basitarsus with moderate dorsal hair crest
  3. 5b Mid and hind femora mostly brownish
  4. 8a Scutum densely covered with yellow hairs
  5. 9b Ovipositor valve with inner margin concave
  6. 10a Anteromedian surface of paraproct unpigmented
Thailand: Simulium (Simulium) males (Takaoka et al. 2019) – couplet 17b
  1. 1b Basal section of radius bare or with few hairs apically
  2. 7b Scutum otherwise
  3. 9b Mid femur and tibia otherwise
  4. 11a Mid tibia mostly dark (including posterior surface)
  5. 12a Fore tibia medium brown, with white shiny area on outer surface
  6. 13a Hind basitarsus white or whitish yellow on basal 1/2 or less and dark brown to brownish black on rest
  7. 14a Hind basitarsus somewhat enlarged, 4.0-5.0 times as long as its greatest width, and much narrower than greatest width of hind tibia
  8. 15b Abdomen with dorsolateral pair of silvery spots on segments 2, 5, 6 and 7
  9. 16b Upper-eye facets in 22-26 vertical columns
  10. 17b Upper-eye facets in 24-26 vertical columns
Thailand: Simulium (Simulium) pupae (Takaoka et al. 2019) – couplet 17a
  1. 1b Gill with 6-10 fiaments ⚠
  2. 4a Gill with 6 filaments
  3. 5b Gill with 6 thread-like filaments (with basal portions somewhat inflated or not) without minute spines
  4. 7a Cocoon wall-pocket shaped
  5. 8b Cocoon without lateral window
  6. 15a Integument of head and thorax bare (except posterior 1/2 of thorax with minute tubercles)
  7. 16b Cocoon thickly woven, not transparent, and its anterior margin well defined. Inner filament of ventral pair not narrowed basally. Spine-combs dorsally on abdominal segments 7-9. Terminal hooks present
  8. 17a Upper filament of dorsal pair more than twice as thick as interspiracular trunk
