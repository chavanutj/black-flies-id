Simulium rosliramlii Takaoka & Chen, 2017

ClassificationGenus Simulium, subgenus Simulium
Statusvalid
DistributionVietnam: Lao Cai, Sa Pa, 1,728 m (topotypes OQ117870-OQ117873; sites 11-13 at 1,105-1,750 m in Adler et al. 2016) (Adler et al. 2016; Putt et al. 2023)
Diagnostic notesS. tuberosum species-group; formally described from S. rufibasis cytoform B of Adler et al. (2016) (fixed IIIL-8,11,13; probable IS sex-linked rearrangements IS-27/28/29,31+13hb; shares polymorphic IIIL-64 with sympatric S. doipuiense A). Non-monophyletic in COI with high intraspecific divergence (1.24-8.17%, mean 4.63%) among topotypes (suggesting cryptic diversity) and 0.74% min distance to S. doipuiense complex; distinguished morphologically from S. doipuiense complex by female antennal color, male upper-eye facets and pupal frontal tubercles (Putt et al. 2023 pp. 2, 12, 16-18).
SourcesAdler et al. 2016, Putt et al. 2023
