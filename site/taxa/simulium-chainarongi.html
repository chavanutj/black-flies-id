Simulium chainarongi Kuvangkadilok & Takaoka, 1999

ClassificationGenus Simulium, subgenus Simulium
Statusvalid
DistributionThailand (Takaoka et al. 2019) | Thailand (Takaoka & Kuvangkadilok 1999; Takaoka & Choochote 2004).
ThailandThailand (Takaoka et al. 2019, p. 57) | Type locality: Klang Lum Duan, Yod Dome Wildlife Sanctuary, Ubon Ratchathani Province, NE Thailand (altitude 140 m, 16.IX.1998, holotype female, 2 paratype females, 1 male, 3 larvae); also Huai Luang Waterfall, Ubon Ratchathani (300 m) (Takaoka & Kuvangkadilok 1999 pp. 503-504; Kuvangkadilok & Takaoka 2000 p. 173); listed from Thailand (Takaoka & Choochote 2004 p. 189).
Diagnostic notesSimulium multistriatum species-group (Takaoka et al. 2019). Described in Takaoka & Kuvangkadilok (1999). | Simulium s.str. (multistriatum species-group); described by Kuvangkadilok & Takaoka in Takaoka & Kuvangkadilok (1999 pp. 501-504). Female (body 2.6 mm, wing 2.1 mm): 5-vittate scutum, simple claws, cibarium with smooth blunt medial projection, sternite 8 with 23-34 hairs/side, single ovoid spermatheca, mid and hind femora almost entirely dark brown (separating from S. chaliowae which has basal 1/5-1/4 yellow, and S. triglobus with yellow femora and 3 spermathecae). Male (2.6 mm, wing 2.0 mm): upper eye 20 vertical columns x 20 horizontal rows of large facets; hind basitarsus expanded (4.1x as long as wide) and dark yellow on basal 2/5 (vs entirely light brown in S. chaliowae); style 3.1x greatest width, not narrowed medially; ventral plate subquadrate with ca. 20 posterior teeth in 2 irregular rows. Pupa (3.0 mm): 8 short paired gill filaments (1.2-0.8 mm, 120 deg angle), basal portion of dorsalmost pair ca. 2.0x as thick as ventralmost pair (vs 1.4x in S. chaliowae) and subequal to or thicker than interspiracular trunk; shoe-shaped cocoon thickly woven without open spaces. Larva (4.8-5.0 mm): dorsolateral pair of small protuberances on abdominal segments 1-6 (separating from S. hirtinervis and S. chiangmaiense which have protuberances on segments 1-8); labral fan ca. 42 rays; postgenal cleft rounded, ca. 3.5x postgenal bridge; rectal gill 7-9 lobules/lobe; posterior circlet ca. 92 rows of up to 16 hooklets (Takaoka & Kuvangkadilok 1999 pp. 501-504; Takaoka & Choochote 2004 pp. 194-196).
SourcesTakaoka et al. 2019, Takaoka & Kuvangkadilok 1999, Kuvangkadilok & Takaoka 2000, Takaoka & Choochote 2004
