The chain of characters leading to this taxon in each key (a ready-made diagnosis).

Thailand: Simulium (Simulium) females (Takaoka et al. 2019) – couplet 4b
  1. 1a Claw with small subbasal tooth
  2. 2a Basal section of radius fully haired. Fore basitarsus with thick dorsal hair crest
  3. 3b Fore tibia yellow on basal 1/2 and darkened on apical 1/2
  4. 4b Mandible with 6 vestigial outer teeth
Thailand: Simulium (Simulium) males (Takaoka et al. 2019) – couplet 5b
  1. 1a Basal section of radius fully haired
  2. 2b Abdomen with pair of shiny white pruinose spots dorsally or dorsolaterally on segments 2, 6 and 7
  3. 4a Fore basitarsus with thick dorsal hair crest. Hind basitarsus nearly parallel-sided
  4. 5b Upper-eye facets in 17 vertical columns
Thailand: Simulium (Simulium) pupae (Takaoka et al. 2019) – couplet 34b
  1. 1b Gill with 6-10 fiaments ⚠
  2. 4a Gill with 6 filaments
  3. 5b Gill with 6 thread-like filaments (with basal portions somewhat inflated or not) without minute spines
  4. 7b Cocoon shoe shaped or boot shaped
  5. 27b Cocoon tightly woven
  6. 29b Cocoon without such flap and window
  7. 30b Gill filaments subequal in length and thickness to one another. Facial and frontal trichomes unbranched
  8. 32b Abdominal segment 8 without spine-combs dorsally
  9. 34b Two filaments of ventral pair thinner than interspiracular trunk
Thailand: Simulium (Simulium) mature larvae (Takaoka et al. 2019) – couplet 6b
  1. 1b Histoblast of pupal gill with 3, 6, 8 or 10 filaments
  2. 3b Histoblast of pupal gill with 6-10 filaments
  3. 4a Histoblast of pupal gill with 6 filaments
  4. 5a Last abdominal segment with accessory sclerite ventrally
  5. 6b Mandible without supernumerary serration
