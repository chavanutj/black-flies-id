Subgenera and species-groups of Austrosimulium (Dumbleton 1963) (Dumbleton 1963) – couplet 4a
  1. 1a Female mandible toothed on one side of apex only, spiniform setae not present on distal R1 vein; stem of female genital fork broad, tapering cephalad, without narrow parallel-sided median rib; basal row of spines on abdominal tergites of pupa absent; pupal breathing organ never lamellar and pseudosegmented, always with filaments; anal spines absent in larva (Aust. and N.Z.)
  2. 2b Antenna of adult with segment 2 longer than 3, tarsal claws with or without tooth; pupa with ventral abdominal hooks present; cocoon with or without floor; larva with semicircular sclerite and ventral tubercles present, antenna with segment 2 short and 3 longer than 1 plus 2 (mirabile, ungulatum, and australense groups)
  3. 4a Female without tooth on tarsal claw; pupa without ocular spine; larva with dorsal ends of semicircular sclerite expanded or forked (New Zealand) ⚠
