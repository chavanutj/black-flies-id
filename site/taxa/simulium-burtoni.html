Simulium burtoni Takaoka & Davies, 1995

ClassificationGenus Simulium, subgenus Gomphostilbia
Statusvalid
DistributionPeninsular Malaysia, Thailand (Takaoka et al. 2019) | Peninsular Malaysia (type locality, Takaoka & Davies 1995); Thailand (new record, Takaoka & Choochote 2004 p. 190). | Thailand (Srisuka et al. 2022) | Thailand (Srisuka et al. 2017)
ThailandThailand (Takaoka et al. 2019, p. 55) | Tambol Ban Laung, Doi Inthanon NP (3 females on human attractant, Jan-Feb 2004) and Hauy Mor, Chiang Mai Province (1 reared female from pupa, June 2001) (Takaoka & Choochote 2004 p. 190 Note 2). | Thailand (Doi Inthanon NP, Chiang Mai; Choochote et al. 2005, cited in Srisuka et al. 2022, p. 2) | Thailand: 523 mature larvae (2.7% of total), 24.1% stream occurrence; in Southern region present at 100% of sites and 2nd most predominant (19.8%, n=474) (Srisuka et al. 2017)
ImportanceThree adult females collected on human attractant at Doi Inthanon NP, Chiang Mai (Takaoka & Choochote 2004 p. 190 Note 2). | Females captured using human attractants at low and medium elevations in Doi Inthanon NP, Chiang Mai (Choochote et al. 2005, cited in Srisuka et al. 2022, p. 2).
Diagnostic notesSimulium varicorne species-group, S. burtoni subgroup (Takaoka et al. 2019). | Gomphostilbia (varicorne species-group). Antenna with 10 segments (2+8); female tergite 5 shiny (reexamination of Malaysian holotype showed tergite 5 is shiny, correcting original description as dull; Thai females also show a dark subbasal spot on hind tibia, Takaoka & Choochote 2004 p. 190 Note 2); male ventral plate produced ventrally; pupa with 8 long filaments (>1.9 mm), dorsal and middle triplets with 1 individual and 2 very long-stalked paired filaments; larva with slender hair-like branched spinous setae (0-5 branches) on abdominal segments 5-8 (Takaoka & Choochote 2004 pp. 191-193; compared with S. chumpornense in Kuvangkadilok & Takaoka 2000 p. 172). | S. varicorne species-group. Female: antenna with 8 flagellomeres (flagellomeres 3 and 5-8 darkened, others yellow), pleural membrane bare, subcosta haired ventrally, abdominal segments 5-8 shiny dorsally (Srisuka et al. 2022, p. 9).
SourcesTakaoka et al. 2019, Kuvangkadilok & Takaoka 2000, Takaoka & Choochote 2004, Srisuka et al. 2022, Srisuka et al. 2017
