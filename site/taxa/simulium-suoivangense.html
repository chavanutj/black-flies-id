Simulium suoivangense Takaoka & Pham, 2017

ClassificationGenus Simulium, subgenus Simulium
Statusvalid
DistributionVietnam: Lam Dong, Suoi Vang Natural Forest / Lac Duong, 1,443-1,452 m (paratype and topotypes OQ117877-OQ117880) (Low et al. 2016; Adler et al. 2016; Putt et al. 2023)
Diagnostic notesS. tuberosum species-group (S. tani complex); formally described from S. tani morphoform 'b' (= cytoform M) of Low et al. (2016) and Adler et al. (2016). Validated as a distinct species by morphology (absence of pupal terminal hooks, separating from S. tani complex), chromosomes (unique inversion IIIL-47 at 0.75 frequency) and nuclear ECP1 + concatenated COI+COII+ECP1 (Low et al. 2016; Adler et al. 2016; Putt et al. 2023 pp. 2, 16); non-monophyletic in COI alone (0.50-2.72% p-distance).
SourcesLow et al. 2016, Adler et al. 2016, Putt et al. 2023
