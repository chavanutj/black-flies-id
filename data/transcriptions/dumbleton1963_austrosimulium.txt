# key_id: dumbleton1963_austrosimulium
# Dumbleton, L.J. (1963) The classification and distribution of the Simuliidae (Diptera) with particular reference to the genus Austrosimulium. New Zealand Journal of Science 6: 320-357. Key on pp. 333-334.
# Key to the sub-genera and species-groups of Austrosimulium (Australia, New Zealand, southern Chile); adults, pupae and larvae. Results are groups; the species included are listed in the notes as printed.
1a | Female mandible toothed on one side of apex only, spiniform setae not present on distal R1 vein; stem of female genital fork broad, tapering cephalad, without narrow parallel-sided median rib; basal row of spines on abdominal tergites of pupa absent; pupal breathing organ never lamellar and pseudosegmented, always with filaments; anal spines absent in larva (Aust. and N.Z.) | 2 | | Subgenus Austrosimulium | Dumbleton 1963 p. 333
1b | Female mandible toothed on both sides of apex; spiniform setae present on distal R1 vein; stem of female genital fork if broad and tapering with prominent narrow parallel-sided median rib; basal row of spines on abdominal tergites of pupa present; pupal breathing organ lamellar, pseudosegmented, without filaments; anal spines present in larva (Southern Chile) | | Paraustrosimulium anthracinum | Subgenus Paraustrosimulium | Dumbleton 1963 p. 333. Printed: "subgenus Paraustrosimulium Wygodzinsky and Coscaron. (Includes only A. (P.) anthracinum (Bigot))." Paraustrosimulium is now treated as a separate genus (Adler, World Blackflies inventory).
2a | Antenna of adult with segment 3 longer than 2 (except in torrentium Tonnoir), female tarsal claw without tooth (with only a small tooth in magnum M. and M.) pupa without ventral abdominal hooks; cocoon without floor; larva without semicircular sclerite, with or without ventral tubercles, antennae with segments 1, 2, and 3 subequal in length (bancrofti and fusiosum groups) | 3 | | | Dumbleton 1963 p. 333. "fusiosum" as printed here; spelled "furiosum" in couplet 3 (the furiosum group).
2b | Antenna of adult with segment 2 longer than 3, tarsal claws with or without tooth; pupa with ventral abdominal hooks present; cocoon with or without floor; larva with semicircular sclerite and ventral tubercles present, antenna with segment 2 short and 3 longer than 1 plus 2 (mirabile, ungulatum, and australense groups) | 4 | | | Dumbleton 1963 p. 333
